= Encounter themes: provenance

All four scene assets are original drawings made for Lapis Cloud (V1.9.67, "Begegnungsraum Stufe 1"): floor plans seen from above.
They are not derived from any photograph, font, clip art or third-party artwork.
They are licensed under the licence of this repository.

[cols="2,3"]
|===
| Asset | What it shows

| `church/front.svg` | The chancel: altar steps, altar with two candlesticks, a lectern at the side.
| `church/row.svg`   | One row of pews: a bench per block (two blocks of three seats, aisle in the middle). Tiled downwards behind the seats.
| `hall/front.svg`   | The podium: stage with a step, lectern, two microphones, a table for the moderation.
| `hall/row.svg`     | One row of chairs: three chairs per block, aisle in the middle. Tiled downwards behind the seats.
|===

`church/church.svg` (V1.9.62, a front view of a nave with a rose window) was removed in V1.9.67 and replaced by the floor plans above.

Technical contract of every theme asset:

* Vector only (no raster image, no gradient, no `<script>`, no `<foreignObject>`, no external reference), at most 30 KB.
* Used as a CSS *mask* (`theme.css`, `.lapis-encounter-scene-front` and `.lapis-encounter-scene-rows`): only the alpha channel counts, so the drawing uses two tones through opacity -- lines at 1.0, areas at about 0.3 -- and the colour comes from the design token `--lapis-encounter-scene-ink`.
* The `row.svg` tile is 352 x 56 units, exactly the footprint of one row of seats (`--lapis-enc-row-w` x `--lapis-enc-row-h` in `theme.css`, checked against `EncounterSeating` by a DOM test). On a narrow screen the tile is scaled to 256 x 44 (`preserveAspectRatio="none"`); the benches are decorative, a slight mismatch with the narrower aisle is accepted.
* Decorative only (`aria-hidden`); nothing the person has to read or press ever sits on it.
