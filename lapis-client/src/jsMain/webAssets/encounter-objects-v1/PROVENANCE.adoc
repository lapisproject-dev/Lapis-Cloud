= Encounter objects (v1): provenance

All ten assets are original drawings made for Lapis Cloud (V1.9.152, "Begegnungsraum Stufe 2c", the fixed room templates): objects of a floor plan seen from above.
They are not derived from any photograph, font, clip art or third-party artwork. They are licensed under the licence of this repository.
Seven of them were drawn for this directory, the three block tiles (`block-pew.svg`, `block-chair.svg`, `block-stool.svg`, V1.9.153) replace the row tiles of V1.9.152 and are drawn in the grid of one block of three seats.

[cols="2,3"]
|===

| `altar.svg`        | The altar of the nave template: steps, an altar block with two candlesticks.
| `podium.svg`       | The podium of the hall template: a stage with a lectern, two microphones and a step.

| `table-round.svg`  | A round table top; drawn behind a table card of the foyer and hall templates.
| `table-long.svg`   | A long table top; drawn behind a table card of the small-group template.

| `block-chair.svg`  | One block of chairs (160 x 48): three chairs, centred on the three seats.
| `block-stool.svg`  | One block of stools (160 x 48): three stools, centred on the three seats.
|===

Technical contract of every object asset (checked by `EncounterObjectAssetsTest` and, for the SHA-256 values, by the Gradle task `stageVideoEffectAssets` on the staging AND on the webpack copy):

* Vector only: a `viewBox`, no raster image, no gradient, no `<text>`, no `<script>`, no `<foreignObject>`, no external reference; at most 8 KB.
* Used as a CSS *mask* (`theme.css`, `.lapis-encounter-scene-object`, `.lapis-encounter-block::before`, `.lapis-encounter-table::before`): only the alpha channel counts, so the drawing uses two tones through opacity -- lines at 1.0, areas at about 0.3 -- and the colour comes from the design token `--lapis-encounter-scene-ink`.
* The three block tiles are 160 x 48 units, exactly the footprint of one block of three seats (48 px seats, 8 px gap; 140 x 44 on a phone, where the tile is scaled to the block). The tile hangs on the block itself (`.lapis-encounter-block::before`), so it can never drift from the seats; `EncounterLayoutGeometryTest` measures that.
* Decorative only (`aria-hidden`); nothing the person has to read or press ever sits on it. A change of any file here needs a new SHA-256 in `lapis-client/build.gradle.kts` (`videoEffectExpectedSha256`).
