# Submitting travel expenses -- step by step

This page explains how to sign in and how to submit a trip in Lapis Cloud. No prior knowledge is needed.

## 1. Signing in

### Already a member with access?

Sign in with your e-mail address and password ("Sign in" page). If you forgot your password, choose "Forgot password?" below the sign-in form, enter your e-mail address
and request a reset. The e-mail can take a few minutes. Use the link in it to set a new password, then sign in again.

### No access yet? Become a member

1. Open the "Become a member" page (on the sign-in page: "No account yet? Become a member now.").
2. Enter your name, e-mail address and a password, optionally choose your regional chapter and confirm that you have read and accept the membership agreement. Click "Submit application".
3. The board reviews your application. Until then you are **not signed in yet**. The board decides explicitly -- there is no automatic approval.
4. After approval, sign in with the e-mail address and password you chose when applying. The board can inform you of the decision by a short e-mail; **do not rely on that e-mail
   arriving** -- if you hear nothing after a few days, try signing in or ask the board.

> Note: there is also a "friend account" (only for taking part in video conferences). It is created without a board review but is not a membership account:
> the travel expenses page does not appear in its menu.

## 2. Submitting travel expenses

Open **Travel expenses** in the menu. At the top you see your contact person for questions.

1. **Create a new claim.** Enter the purpose and the start and end of the trip (at most 31 days). Your membership number is shown automatically.
2. **Fill in the travel days.** There is one block per day. Enter when you left and returned (first day: departure only; last day: return only; days in between: nothing -- they count as
   24 hours). Tick the meals that were provided to you; that reduces the daily allowance. If your hotel includes breakfast it is already ticked and locked on the following day. **Save days** stores
   your entries. The totals you see while typing are a **preview** -- what counts is what stands after saving.
3. **Further costs.** Trips with your own car (whole kilometres, passengers raise the rate), public transport, accommodation (hotel, first night, number of nights, "with breakfast" if applicable),
   hospitality and other costs. Upload a **receipt** (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB) for all of them except the own-car trip, where a route map proof is optional.
4. **Advance and payout.** Enter an advance you already received (leave empty otherwise). Then choose consciously: **Pay out** or **Donation receipt (waiver of reimbursement)**. With the donation receipt you waive the
   reimbursement; the donation acknowledgement is issued **outside Lapis**. Save the choice.
5. **Submit.** "Submit for approval" is enabled as soon as everything is complete -- if not, the reason is shown right above the button. Please submit by **31 December of the travel year**; after that date you only see a notice, you can still submit.
6. **Status.** Under "My claims" you see whether your claim is submitted, rejected or "booked for payout". "Booked for payout" means the treasury has booked it -- the transfer is arranged separately. A rejected claim can be copied as a draft (you must upload the receipts again).

An advance larger than the claim is not supported -- please contact your contact person in that case.

## 3. Alternative

You can still use the old Excel template or write an e-mail to the treasurer. Lapis Cloud is the preferred way.
