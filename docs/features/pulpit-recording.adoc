= Optional recording of the pulpit (V1.9.143)
:toc:

An encounter room (church service or assembly) can be configured so that the *pulpit* -- the camera and the microphone of the person who holds the PULPIT
office -- is recorded into the document store. The congregation (in an assembly: the participants) is never recorded. The feature is **off by default** and
switched on in two steps: per instance by an ADMIN and per room by BOARD/ADMIN. This page is the operator view; the design and the threat model are in
link:../architecture/encounter-space.adoc[docs/architecture/encounter-space.adoc], section "Pulpit recording (V1.9.143)".

IMPORTANT: Whether and under which conditions a recording of a person is lawful depends on the **country** of the instance (data protection, personality and
copyright law, rules for religious communities and assemblies). Lapis Cloud does not decide that and is not legal advice. It makes the feature opt-in and puts the
responsibility, in writing, with the operator: an ADMIN has to read an operator notice and tick that the legal admissibility for their country has been checked.
The texts shown to the persons concerned are drafts the operator replaces with legally reviewed ones.

== Switching on

. *Instance (ADMIN):* settings, card "Videokonferenz", section "Begegnungsraum": "Aufzeichnung von Kanzel bzw. Podium erlauben". The switch opens the operator notice
  (four paragraphs, version in the foot). "Einschalten" stays locked until the box "Ich habe die rechtliche Zulässigkeit für mein Land geprüft und übernehme die
  Verantwortung." is ticked. The audit entry records the version and the hash of the German reference text. The switch is locked with a hint when this server has no
  recording service (`LAPIS_RECORDING_ENABLED`, the same egress service as the conference recording).
. *Room (BOARD/ADMIN):* the room form has "Kanzel-Aufzeichnung in diesem Raum erlauben" (assembly: "Podium-..."). It can only be switched **on** while the room is
  closed (the Art. 9 consent of non-members is a different text in a room with the option), and always **off** (which stops a running recording).
. *Optional:* "Zustimmung der Kanzel vor jeder Aufnahme verlangen" (instance setting, default **on**): the person at the pulpit is asked and has to agree before the
  first byte is recorded.

Switching the instance switch **off** stops every running recording at once. A new version of the operator notice only blocks *new* starts until an admin
acknowledges it; a running recording is not interrupted.

== Recording

* *Who starts and stops:* an ACTIVE PULPIT or STEWARD who is present in the room, with the toggle "Aufzeichnung starten/beenden" in the bar. BOARD/ADMIN without an
  office have no control (their access is the list of stored recordings).
* *Who is recorded:* only an ACTIVE PULPIT holder who is present and connected. A steward is never recorded, nobody of the congregation is, a person with a temporary
  floor is not. At most **two** pulpit persons per room (at most eight on the server) are recorded at the same time; the rest is reported.
* *Consent of the pulpit:* with the setting above the pulpit person sees a short dialog (the `REC_PULPIT` text), focus on "Nicht aufzeichnen"; Escape and the close
  button are a "no". Agreeing is remembered **for one recording only, in memory** (there is no table of it): it is dropped when the recording ends, when the person declines (which also stops their own running file), and when the `REC_PULPIT` text changes. A new start asks again.
* *Echo:* while a person of the congregation holds the floor no recording runs -- a start is refused, a floor granted during a recording ends it (the pulpit's
  loudspeaker would carry that voice into the file).
* *What everybody sees:* a band "Aufzeichnung läuft · Nur die Kanzel wird aufgezeichnet" (symbol and words, no animation, a short form "Aufnahme" on a narrow
  screen) in every scene and in the full screen; people who arrive later are told point to point. A screen reader hears "Aufzeichnung gestartet/beendet".
  The band never says "off" while a recording may still run (an unreachable LiveKit does not switch it off).
  A person who enters a room that is being recorded is told by the answer of the entry itself, so the band is there from the first moment.
* *Waiting for the pulpit:* while a start waits for the pulpit's answer, the starting officer's button is locked and polls the server; it unlocks as soon as the
  pulpit declined or the request ran out (two minutes). The start is audited with the person who requested it, also when the pulpit's agreement completes it.
* *Automatic stops* (audited without an actor): `INSTANCE_DISABLED`, `ROOM_OPTION_OFF`, `SESSION_CLOSED`, `SOURCE_NOT_PULPIT` (the office was withdrawn),
  `MAX_DURATION`, `FLOOR_GRANTED`, `DISK_LOW` (a volume ran full while recording), `CONSENT_WITHDRAWN`, `RESTART_ORPHAN` (after a server restart).

== Storage and deletion

* The finished file is archived as a document in the folder "Aufzeichnungen Begegnungsraum" (created `BOARD_ONLY`), access level `BOARD_ONLY`, which cannot be loosened (neither the recording nor its folder; the service refuses with a conflict) (BOARD, ADMIN and --
  as for every `BOARD_ONLY` document of the store -- TREASURER can read it). The title carries the room and the moment ("Kanzel-Aufzeichnung – <room> – 16.10.2026 10:30"),
  never a name; the file name only a random UUID.
* A marker table (`pulpit_recording_document`: document, room, moment) marks it. It has **no person column**.
* **Retention is unlimited.** Nothing is deleted automatically (that is a decision of the owner).
* **Deletion is hard:** BOARD/ADMIN delete a recording in the list under the room administration ("Aufzeichnungen"), or with the ordinary "delete document" of the
  store -- both remove the bytes of every version, the version rows, the marker and the document. Older **backups** keep a copy until they rotate; the dialog says so.

== Known limitations

* Not verified against a real LiveKit/egress yet (v1.13.5): the shape of the participant file egress and whether the egress participant is ignored by the encounter
  poller are assumptions; see the staging script "Pulpit recording".
* The camera of the pulpit person can show people who are physically present next to them; the software cannot prevent that.
* The state (who agreed, which files run) is in server memory: it is lost on a restart (the clock then stops orphaned egresses; a file of a lost session stays on the
  egress volume with a warning -- nothing is deleted and nothing archived without a known uploader) and is **not shared between several server instances**.
* The consent variants for non-members and the placeholder texts are drafts; the translations are model translations.
* The proof of the pulpit person's agreement with a person attached lives outside Lapis Cloud; the audit entry of a start only records *whether* the pulpit had
  agreed and the hashes of the two texts.
