= Voting right as a member attribute and donor sign-in (V1.9.298)
:toc:

Two related changes in one wave:

. *Voting right* is an attribute of a member, independent of the membership (and of the membership tier). A member without voting right is still a member -- invited, in the committees, with
  every other right of a member -- but is in *no electorate*: not frozen into a vote, election or consensus round, not in the denominator of the quorum, no ballot.
. A *donor* (`DONOR`, "Spender") may now sign in, with the same rights as a friend or guest plus the room-entry behaviour of a member. A donor is never a member and never holds an administrative role.

Both changes were decided by the owner on 2026-11-09. The architecture is in link:../architecture/voting-right-and-donors.adoc[Voting right and donors: architecture].

== Behaviour changes

. Every member has a voting right, default *yes*. Nothing changes for any existing member.
. The LupaBB status "Neumitglied" now means: member (`ACTIVE`) *without* voting right. The next sync takes the voting right away from every member that LupaBB reports as "Neumitglied" (unless an administrator
  set the value by hand, see below). "Mitglied" means "with voting right".
. A donor can sign in (`LOGIN_BLOCKED` no longer contains `DONOR`). A change from `ACTIVE` to `DONOR` no longer ends the sessions of the person; the status is read fresh on every request and applies at once.
. A donor account is always created by an administrator, always with the role *Mitglied*. A donor can never be BOARD, TREASURER or ADMIN; an open request "suspend" with the target status `DONOR` fails when it is
  executed (and a new one is refused).
. The Keycloak sign-in does not link a donor by its e-mail address (the implicit link); an administrator can link a donor by hand.
. "Beschlussfähig ab" (the committee's quorum count) counts the people with voting right only.

== The attribute

`member.voting_right` (boolean, default `true`), `member.voting_right_source` (`DEFAULT`, `MANUAL`, `LUPABB`) and `member.voting_right_changed_at` (UTC instant, shown in the organization zone).

* `DEFAULT`: never touched. The value is `true`.
* `MANUAL`: an administrator set it. The LupaBB sync *never* overwrites it; it only shows an information line "Stimmrecht von Hand gesetzt (ja), LupaBB meldet nein -- wird nicht übernommen".
* `LUPABB`: the sync set it, or an administrator handed a manual value back to the sync ("Wieder dem LupaBB-Abgleich überlassen").

=== Who sets it

* *ADMIN only*, in the member editor (section "Stimmrecht", only for a member with the status `ACTIVE`). Never the own row. An anonymised or non-member row is refused.
* The LupaBB sync, for the labels "Mitglied" (yes) and "Neumitglied" (no) only, never for an anonymised or deceased member, never over a `MANUAL` value, never for a member with a management role (those
  differences are listed as "by hand", `ADMIN_ROLE_MANUAL`).
* A new member that the sync creates from a "Neumitglied" record is created without voting right (source `LUPABB`). The CSV import is a one-time bootstrap tool and creates every `ACTIVE` member with voting right
  (default) -- unchanged.

=== Audit

Every change writes a `MEMBER` audit entry carrying one fixed key only, never a value or a name: `member.voting_right.granted`, `.revoked`, `.released_to_sync`, `.granted_by_sync`, `.revoked_by_sync`. Setting the
value a member already has by hand writes and audits nothing.

== Where the voting right counts

The electorate is *membership AND voting right* (`electorateMemberIds`). It is used wherever a circle of voters is *frozen* or decided:

[cols="2,3"]
|===
| Decision | Rule

| Vote (`openVote`), election (`openVoting`), consensus round (freeze, reopen) | the frozen snapshot contains only people with voting right
| Quorum (`computeQuorum`, `checkQuorum`) | the denominator is the electorate (live: members with voting right; frozen: the snapshot)
| Ballot (`castVoteBallot`, `castElectionBallot`, `castResistanceBallot`) | the caller must be in the snapshot *and* hold the voting right *now* (the live floor, `requireVotingMembership`)
| Own participation, dashboard signals, conference ballot cards | `eligible` is false without voting right, even if the snapshot lists the person
| "Beschlussfähig ab" (committee count) | the electorate count
|===

=== A voting right changed after the freeze

The same rule as for a member who leaves the association (see link:rule-based-committees.adoc[Rule-based committees]): the person *stays* in the snapshot and in the denominator, but can no longer vote -- the live check
is the lower bound. A voting right *granted* after the freeze gives no vote: the person is not in the snapshot.

=== Where it deliberately does not count

* Invitations, mail merges and group messages: members without voting right are still invited and addressed (`eligibleMemberIds` is the membership).
* Submitting a motion, standing as a candidate (passive right to vote) and proposing an option in the COLLECTION phase of a consensus: not a ballot. *Open question for the board.*
* Opening a vote (a task of the session leadership). "Cannot open" is read as "is not in the frozen circle".
* Polls (`PollService`): non-binding opinion polls for active members, no committee electorate.
* The attendance list by name (`NameListAttendanceRule`) shows the electorate: a member without voting right is not on it and is recorded like a guest.

== The member without voting right sees

A notice in place of the vote, ballot or rating button -- never a disabled button. The directory shows the text badge "ohne Stimmrecht" for such a member. The member editor says, below the field: "Wird das
Stimmrecht nach dem Start einer Abstimmung entzogen, zählt das Mitglied dort weiter zur Beschlussfähigkeit, kann aber nicht mehr abstimmen."

== Donors

A donor (status `DONOR`) is treated like a friend/guest wherever being "not a member" restricts (`NON_MEMBER` now contains `DONOR`: fail-closed), and gets everything those two may do:

[cols="2,2,3"]
|===
| Area | Donor | Note

| Sign in | yes | account created by an administrator (`Konto anlegen`), role *Mitglied* only
| Video conference | enters a room by its id from an invitation; member token lifetime, not counted against the guest cap; **must still acknowledge the consent text** (a donor is a non-member, the proof of consent is stored exactly as for a guest or friend) | cannot create a room, list rooms or moderate (a creator who became a donor loses the creator's moderation)
| Event conference | public events only; treated like a member inside | never a moderator
| Encounter space | enters like a member, no guest policy applies; must acknowledge the encounter consent text (incl. recording/map paragraphs) before entering | no office (PULPIT/STEWARD)
| Carpool, tender bids, events (self-registration) | like friend/guest | `CARPOOL_PASSENGER`, `TENDER_PARTICIPANT`, `EVENT_SELF_REGISTRANT`
| LTR (account, social network, transfers received, bidding on LTR tenders), MCP | yes (default of open question Q1) | like a friend
| Politician rating | yes, counted as a *guest* rater (default of open question Q2) |
| Notice board, activism, mailing lists, polls, member directory, member card, photo, map, background upload | no | members only
| Voting, elections, consensus | no | not a member
|===

=== Opening a donor account

*Konto anlegen* in the member editor (ADMIN): role *Mitglied* is the only offer for a donor. The Keycloak implicit link is blocked for a donor (the e-mail address would otherwise open an account that nobody
granted); an administrator can link a donor by hand (*Keycloak link*). Import and LupaBB sync never create an account. A legacy donor who kept an account from an earlier change from active to donor can sign in again without any administrator action. At startup `DonorRoleDowngrade` sets every donor account that still holds BOARD, TREASURER or ADMIN to member (audit entry, sessions ended). A donor must be in a conference room (an open participation) before the roster, recording and stream state of that room answer, like any other non-member.

== Not verified

* Nothing ran against a real instance or the real LupaBB. All tests use H2, a PostgreSQL container and a fake LupaBB server.
* The translations of the new texts were produced by machine; nobody has proofread them. No screen reader was used.
* *Whether the articles of association allow members (or supporters) without voting right is not checked.* The board has to confirm it before the first "Neumitglied" sync.

== Open questions

* Q1/Q2 (LTR and politician rating for donors): the defaults above are implemented; the alternative is to take `DONOR` out of `LTR_ELIGIBLE` / `POLITICIAN_RATER`.
* Q3: the sync never writes the voting right of an anonymised or deceased member. The status change away from `DECEASED` that the sync already allows (V1.9.226) is unchanged.
* Peer approval (second administrator) for withdrawing the voting right of a member with a management role. Interim rule (implemented): one administrator alone never withdraws the voting right of a BOARD, TREASURER or ADMIN account holder (peer protection refusal); the account role has to be changed first. Withdrawals during an open decision in the target's committee are not specially guarded.
* Passive right to vote (candidates), motions and options without voting right.
* "Ausgeschlossen" of LupaBB (still `WITHDRAWN`; "Förderer" was decided on 2026-11-10, see link:lupabb-sync.adoc[LupaBB sync]).
