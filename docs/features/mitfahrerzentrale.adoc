= Mitfahrerzentrale (V1.9.12)
:toc:

A carpooling bulletin board for members: any `ACTIVE` member can offer a ride (`OFFER`, 1-8 free
seats) or ask for one (`REQUEST`) between two free-text places on a given date, up to 180 days
out, capped at 10 future postings per member. Contacting a posting's author routes through the
existing direct-message mechanism rather than a bespoke messaging system -- `CarpoolPostingDto`
deliberately carries no author id the client could address directly; the server alone resolves the
recipient from the posting. Building this exposed two real gaps in that pre-existing mechanism,
both closed as part of this wave: direct messages had never had a client UI at all before (the
"Kommunikation" screen was upgraded from a bare unread-counter to a real message list with an
inline reply form), and `sendDirectMessage` had never validated for an empty body or a message to
oneself. A posting drops out of the public feed the day after its departure date but is only hard-
deleted seven days later (`CarpoolRetentionPoller`), so an author can still see and duplicate a
just-expired trip of their own. Full design, the RPC surface, and the direct-message-reuse
decision: `docs/architecture/carpool.adoc`.

== Riding along (V1.9.125)

Since V1.9.125 a person can ride along on an offer, and the driver decides. **This changes who may use the carpool centre**: a named
list of six actions is open to `ACTIVE`, `GUEST` and `FRIEND` (a federated guest or a self-registered friend); everything else stays
members-only (see "Access rule change" in `docs/architecture/carpool.adoc`).

* **Both directions, one row.** A passenger asks ("Mitfahrt anfragen") and the driver confirms or declines; the driver invites a person
  (exact-name search, see below) and the person confirms or declines. There is one participation per ride and person; a new request after
  a decline or a withdrawal revives the same row. States: `OPEN`, `CONFIRMED`, `DECLINED`, `WITHDRAWN`.
* **Seats.** A card shows "2 von 3 Plätzen frei" or "Ausgebucht". The last seat can never be taken twice (the confirmation runs under a
  lock on the ride) and the driver cannot lower the seats below the confirmed passengers.
* **Meeting point.** The driver enters a short free text ("Treffpunkt und Uhrzeit", at most 200 characters). Only the driver and a
  *confirmed* passenger see it. A guest or friend cannot send or read direct messages, so this is their only channel.
* **What a guest sees.** The offers only (no requests), without the free-text note, and no "Eintrag erstellen", no type filter and no
  "Kontakt aufnehmen".
* **"Meine Mitfahrten".** The rides of the person as a passenger, waiting items first; a declined or withdrawn entry disappears seven days
  after the decision. The sidebar shows the number of things waiting.
* **Exact-name search.** The driver enters a first and a last name (at least three characters each). Only a person whose display name matches
  exactly (case, umlauts and spacing are ignored) is found, at most three hits, never a prefix. Whatever the answer -- nobody, too short, too
  many searches -- the screen says the same: "Keine Person mit diesem Namen gefunden. Schicken Sie der Person den Link zu dieser Fahrt."
* **Notifications.** In the app: "Meine Mitfahrten" and the sidebar number. By e-mail (when the instance has mail configured): a short
  message **without names and without the meeting point** to active members and to friends with a verified address; never to a guest.
* **Travel expenses.** On a mileage line of one's own travel expense report the field "Mitfahrer" shows a suggestion ("Laut Mitfahrerzentrale
  2 bestätigte Mitfahrer") with a button "Übernehmen". It is never filled in automatically.

== Limits (honest status)

* There is **no distance or kilometre calculation** and no structured place data.
* The search compares the **whole display name** (a member has no separate first and last name field): titles, middle names and names shorter
  than three characters cannot be found.
* The travel expense suggestion refers to the **travel period of the report**, not to a single line (a mileage line has no date); when several
  trips fall into the period it shows the largest number and says so.
* The suggestion disappears with the ride (seven days after the departure).
* Invitation tokens live in memory: after a restart the driver searches again. Rate limits (20 requests, 20 invitations, 10 searches and 60
  decisions per hour and person) are per server instance.
* A person who leaves the organisation keeps their rows; new confirmations are refused, nothing ends automatically.

== What doesn't work yet

No structured place data (no postal code, no map, no distance calculation) and no notification when a new posting matching a member's
interest appears.
