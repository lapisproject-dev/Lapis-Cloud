= Encounter space (Begegnungsraum) with a temporary floor (V1.9.61 to V1.9.138)
:toc:

A standing room (church service or assembly) in which a few office holders speak and a congregation listens. It is built on the LiveKit stack
with a different authority model and strict data minimisation (Art. 9 GDPR). The full design lives in
link:../architecture/encounter-space.adoc[docs/architecture/encounter-space.adoc]; this page is the short operator view.

== What a room offers

* Offices (PULPIT, STEWARD) per room; everybody else listens. Only an office holder's token may publish.
* Reactions (hand, amen, applause, heart, flower, laughter, amazement, question, crying -- as configured; since V1.9.164 nine options in all; the five new ones are opt-in, crying is in no profile default, a question is only a mood symbol and never a raised hand or a place in the queue; there is still no thumbs-up), chat, seats and, in assemblies, tables with their own audio group. A table is shown as its table top with the seats on a ring around it (V1.9.159); the card is at least 172 px wide, so a 320 px phone shows one table per row.
* Pulpit extras: blessing and bell (church service), transmission, device choice.
* Presence is transient: nothing about who attended is stored.
* Optional, off by default: the pulpit (only the PULPIT office holder, never the congregation) can be recorded into the document store, see
  link:pulpit-recording.adoc[Optional recording of the pulpit (V1.9.143)]. Without that option (the default) nothing is recorded at all, and the entry panel and the consent
  text say so; with it, they say the opposite.

== The control bar stays in view (V1.9.165)

The control bar of the room (reactions, microphone and camera, chat, "Mehr", "Verlassen") is always visible at the bottom edge of the screen, however far a person has scrolled through a large room.
This holds in the browser window, in full screen and in the fallback full screen (for browsers without the full screen function) alike. The bar never hides itself automatically. The "Mehr" sheet and the device panel open directly above the bar.
Not covered by this change: limiting the height of the podium and zooming or fitting the room to the screen.

== The map (V1.9.166)

A room of the kind *assembly* can have a *map* (BOARD/ADMIN switch it on in the room form, off by default, only while the room is closed). Members who are present can step onto the map ("Auf die Karte"), walk with the arrow keys or W/A/S/D (on a phone with the four buttons, or by tapping a tile), jump to the entrance, the podium or the middle, and see where the other members stand and which way they face. The list "In Ihrer Nähe" names the members within two tiles; the key L reads out the own place and those names. Escape moves the keyboard focus to "Karte verlassen". Each person is either seated, on the map, or neither: stepping onto the map gives up a bench or table seat, and sitting down takes you off the map.

Since V1.9.167 the map opens as a *layer* above the room: the dock has a button "Karte öffnen" (a dot on it and the name "Karte öffnen, Sie sind auf der Karte" show that you still stand on the map; on a narrow screen it sits in "Mehr"). The layer shows a short privacy line ("Ihre Position sehen nur anwesende Mitglieder. Nichts wird gespeichert."), with the longer notice of the room behind "Mehr". Escape, the close button or a click outside close the layer; on the map the first Escape moves to "Karte verlassen". Closing the layer never takes you off the map. The control bar stays usable while the layer is open. Guests have no such button.

Positions live only in the memory of the server; nobody is recorded, there is no history of walks and no list of who was where. Guests do not appear on the map and do not see it. An office holder (podium, moderation) can look at the map but does not walk on it. The map is checked in tests only; nobody has used it on a real phone yet.

== Theatre hall (V1.9.167)

The *hall* room template, when the room has tables, looks like a theatre: the tables stand in the middle (the *parterre*, always centred), and every row of seats is split into a *left* and a *right loge* beside it. Seat numbers, names and the keyboard operation are the same as before (the right arrow key from the third seat goes to the fourth, across the parterre). On a phone the loges stand below the tables, side by side or, on the narrowest screens, one below the other. The tables of every room template are now centred.

== Room template (V1.9.152)

When a room is created or edited (BOARD/ADMIN) the form offers a *room template*: nave (church service), foyer and cafe, hall, or small-group room. It decides how the room looks: an altar with pews, a counter with round tables and stools, a podium with rows of chairs, or a board with long tables. A template that does not fit the chosen room kind is greyed out with the reason; choosing the foyer or the small-group room switches the tables on and pre-fills them. The template can only be changed while the room is closed.

It is decoration for the room and is the same for everybody who is present: it changes nothing about who may speak, where people sit or which entry notice they acknowledge, and it stores nothing about a person. "Szene aus" in the control bar hides it on one device; high-contrast settings hide the drawings by themselves.

== What other people in the room see and hear of a guest (V1.9.148)

The entry notice that a guest (or friend) acknowledges is administered in the agreement texts (kinds `ENC_CHURCH` and `ENC_ASSEM`, see link:agreement-texts.adoc[Agreement texts]) and its built-in placeholder states exactly this:

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| What | Who gets it
| Display name; the seat with the person's initials; the table and the seat at the table; whether the person is a guest (sent to every client, shown to moderators only); reactions such as a raised hand, at the person's seat | Everybody present
| Chat messages with the name (not stored) | Everybody present at that moment
| The name in the floor banner, and the voice, while the person has the word and has switched the microphone on themselves | Everybody present
| The voice at a table, only after the person switched the microphone on themselves | The people at that table
| The picture | Never sent
|===

Nothing about who attended is stored; the only trace is the proof of the acknowledgment (label, hash, date). Whenever you publish a new notice, every non-member acknowledges again.

== Temporary floor ("Rederecht", V1.9.138)

An office holder can give *one* person of the congregation the floor for two minutes. The person's microphone then reaches everybody in the
room; afterwards the right is taken back by the server.

* *Who gives it:* only an ACTIVE PULPIT/STEWARD of this room who is present. BOARD/ADMIN without an office see the list of hands but get no
  button.
* *How:* the list of hands ("Wortmeldungen", numbered in the order the hands went up) has a "Wort erteilen" button per person. While somebody
  speaks, the office holder sees "<Name> spricht · noch 1:32", "+1 Min", "+2 Min" (up to ten minutes in total) and "Wort entziehen".
* *Who receives it:* a present person of the congregation who is not silenced and does not sit at a table. The microphone is never switched
  on by itself: the person clicks "Mikrofon einschalten" (the browser's permission prompt belongs to that click).
* *What everybody sees:* a band "Gerade spricht jemand aus der Gemeinde" (or "... Versammlung") with the time left -- no name and no seat. The
  last ten seconds are emphasised by a thicker border (no animation). A screen reader hears three fixed sentences over a floor's life.
* *What is stored:* nothing. The state is in server memory only; a restart ends every floor. No audit entry, no log line with a person.
* *Microphone only:* the server raises that one connection to "microphone only"; camera and screen share stay impossible, also after the end
  (the permission keeps the source list, so an empty list can never mean "everything").

The security model, the expiry and revoke ladder, the poller's tolerance and the threat model are in the architecture document, section
"Temporary floor grant (V1.9.138)". The staging check list is `docs/architecture/encounter-space-staging-test.adoc`, "Temporary floor script".

=== Known limitations

* Not verified against a real LiveKit yet; only tested in Chromium.
* The floor is lost on a server restart (then nobody speaks).
* No device choice and no noise suppression for the floor microphone; one person at a time.
* The order of hands holds from the moment the office holder's client arrived (a hand raised earlier appears at its next renewal).
* Translations are model translations.
