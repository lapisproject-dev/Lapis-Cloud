= Party accountability report: positions, entry, Excel import and report draft (V1.9.225 R1, V1.9.256 R2)
:toc:

NOTE: The `[kuml]` blocks of this document are rendered and validated by the `:docs-render:renderDocs` Gradle task.

Waves R1 and R2 of the accountability report under the German Parties Act (section 24 PartG). R1 stores the **figures** of the report per reporting unit and year, typed in by
hand or imported from an Excel file. R2 (V1.9.256) turns them into a **draft** of the report as a PDF or an Excel file. The draft is marked "ENTWURF -- nicht amtlich" on every page and in
every file; nothing on the page promises a finished, official or audited report.

*This is a working aid, not legal advice.* The position list, the labels and the unit levels have not yet been checked against the official sample of the Bundestag
and the statute text, and the auditor has not seen the result (see <<not-verified>>).

== Who sees it

* Only an organisation with the setting *political party* (`isPoliticalParty`), and there only the roles **BOARD** and **ADMIN**. A treasurer does not see it.
* The server checks the **role first** and the organisation switch second, on every RPC method and every HTTP route. The gate itself therefore reveals nothing to a person below the board
  (they get "forbidden", never "not enabled"). The fact is not secret otherwise: the session info (`politicalParty`) is sent to every logged-in member. Switching the setting off keeps all stored values; they are readable again after
  switching it on.
* Sidebar: group *Finanzen*, entry *Rechenschaftsbericht (PartG)*, route `/rechenschaftsbericht`.

== What a person does

Four tabs, and a year picker (default: last year; 2001 up to the current year).

*Übersicht*:: one row per reporting unit (the federal level, each regional chapter, and the *summary* of its subordinate regional units), with a fill state
("Nicht erfasst", "Teilweise (n von 35)", "Vollständig"), the totals for income, expenses and net assets, and three icon actions: enter values, download the Excel
template, import an Excel file. A button *Bericht erzeugen* leads to the fourth tab.
*Werte erfassen*:: the 42 positions in the order of the statute. 35 are typed in, 7 are sums shown live (total income, material expenses, total expenses, surplus or
deficit, total assets, total liabilities, net assets). Every typed-in position has a field for the report year **and** one for the year before.
*Positionen*:: the fixed catalogue with the statute reference and the organisation's own hint per position (up to 500 characters, plain text), shown during entry.
*Bericht*:: see <<report-draft>>.

== The report draft (R2)

On the tab *Bericht* a person picks the **scope**: the whole party (all units) or one unit, and reads a **checklist** of what is still missing. Then two buttons download the
draft: *PDF herunterladen* and *Excel herunterladen*. Both are always enabled: a gap never blocks the export (nothing is locked), it shows up in the file instead.

*Checklist*:: per unit "x of 35 positions not entered" (with the list of the positions and a link to the entry tab of that unit), the units that are complete as one line, and a
neutral note when the year before is missing or incomplete ("Vorjahr nicht erfasst -- Spalte bleibt leer"). A unit without subordinate regional units keeps its summary row open for
good: enter zeros with *Leere Felder mit 0 füllen* (see <<open-questions>>).
*Not entered is never 0*:: a position that nobody entered is printed as `n. e.` (PDF) or "nicht erfasst" (XLSX). A sum whose inputs are not all entered is printed with its partial amount
and `(unvollst.)`, and a footnote names the missing positions per unit. A share that cannot be computed (base is 0, or the number is partial) is `n. b.`
*Layout of the PDF*:: A4 landscape. Page 1: title, draft notice, completeness. Then the summary (the whole party or the unit, income Nr. 1 to 10 and expenses Nr. 1 to 3 as their own lines, total and
surplus, with shares of the total income and the total expenses, the year before beside it, and a table by level); the income matrix (one row per unit and the whole party, one column per income
position, report year first, then the year before); the expenses per unit; the assets and liabilities per unit (without share columns). Every page carries the header line, "Seite x von y", the
legend and a diagonal watermark behind the content. All sums include the subsidies between branches and are **not consolidated** (that follows in a later wave); the summary says so.
*Layout of the XLSX*:: six fixed sheets: Vollständigkeit, Zusammenfassung, Einnahmen, Ausgaben, Vermögen, Hinweise. Row 1 of every sheet is the PDF header line, the table starts in row 3 in the order
of the PDF. Amounts are real numbers (euro format, a negative result stays negative), shares are fractions with the format `0.0%`. There is no formula, no macro and no shared string; text that starts with
`=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is stored with the quote-prefix style.
*Audit*:: every successful export writes one entry (`PARTG_REPORT_EXPORT`, action `CREATE`) with the format, year, scope and counters. It holds no amount and no name.
*Limits*:: 10 exports per member and minute, two renderings at a time, at most 201 units, 600 pages and 8 MB per file. A larger report is refused with "too large".
*Known deviation*:: the minus sign in the PDF is the ASCII hyphen (`-1.234,56`), because the standard PDF fonts have no U+2212. The Excel file holds real negative numbers.

=== 0 is not the same as empty

An empty field means *not entered*; **0,00** is a value ("entered as zero"). "Leere Felder im Rechnungsjahr mit 0 füllen" asks first and fills only the empty fields of the
report year, never the year before. Deleting a field's content deletes the stored value.

=== The year before

The column "Vorjahr" is stored in **its own year** (with its own revision). That is how last year's figures arrive in the system although the instance did not exist
then, and how the "year before" column of a later report is filled.

== Excel import

1. Download the template of the unit and year (`partg-r1-1`): a protected sheet without a password, only the value cells unlocked, sum rows as formulas.
2. Fill it in Excel or LibreOffice. Empty cells change nothing.
3. *Excel-Datei importieren*: choose the file, *Prüfen* shows every change (old and new value) and every error (row, column, a fixed text per error code),
   *Übernehmen* writes **everything or nothing**. It is not possible while errors exist.

A file for another unit or year is refused (`TARGET_MISMATCH`), never taken over. Formulas are **not evaluated**: a typed-in cell with a formula takes the result stored in
the file and is marked in the preview; a formula without a stored result is an error. Macro files, files above 2 MB, and files with more than 500 rows of content are refused.

== What R1 and R2 do not do

* R2 produces a draft only. No official, filed or audited report; no consolidation of the subsidies between branches (R5); no signature, no submission (R6).
* No mapping from the ledger accounts to the positions, no consolidation, no auditor access, no workflow (R4 to R6).
* Subordinate regional units are **one summary row per regional chapter**, not the individual district associations. Whether that is enough for the report of 2025 is an
  open question (the auditor picks at least ten individual subordinate units, section 29 PartG).
* A pending import preview lives **in memory**: a restart of the server drops it and the person uploads the file again.

[[open-questions]]
== Open questions

. **Summary content.** The sample was read as: income Nr. 1 to 9 and expenses Nr. 1 to 2 for the whole party, with totals with and without subsidies from branches. R2 shows Nr. 1 to 10
  and Nr. 1 to 3, each on its own labelled line, the totals "including subsidies, not consolidated (follows in R5)" and the table by level only in that variant. A column "without subsidies"
  (E11 minus E10, A04 minus A03) is trivial to add. The row order lives in `PartgReportLayout.SUMMARY_INCOME_CODES` / `SUMMARY_EXPENSE_CODES`.
. **Units without subordinate regional units.** A regional chapter always has a `SUB:` unit, which stays "not entered" until zeros are entered; there is no "does not apply" marker (it would need a
  migration and a legal decision). R3 or R6 may add one.
. **Order of the units in the matrix.** The order of `units()`: federal level, then per regional chapter (by name) the state unit and its subordinate summary, then the whole party. The sample's
  grouping ("Landesverbände A to D") and the place of a "nachgeordnete" summary row are not confirmed.
. **Share columns of the balance.** Shares are defined against the total income or total expenses only, so the assets and liabilities have no share columns.
. **Position list** against the sample, the matrix axes, and whether Nr. 10 and Nr. 3 belong on the summary page.
. The column "-" of the R1 overview for a total that is not entered stays as it is (a candidate for the same "n. e." wording).

[[not-verified]]
== Not verified

* The PDF and the XLSX of R2 were **not compared pixel by pixel with the Bundestag sample**, **not opened in real Excel, LibreOffice or Word**, **not accepted by the Bundestag administration** and **not seen by the auditor**.
  They are tested by reading them back (text extraction of the PDF, XML parse of the XLSX).
* The PDF is deterministic in the tests (same document, same bytes); that is not guaranteed across PDFBox versions.

* The list of positions, their labels and the unit levels against the official Bundestag sample and the statute text.
* The template in real Excel and LibreOffice (opening, sheet protection, formulas, saving again and importing again). The reader is tested with hand-built files in the shapes
  both programs write (shared strings, missing cell references, formula results of type `str`).
* Acceptance by the auditor.
