= Party accountability report: positions, entry, Excel import, report draft, separate statements and the link to the bookings (V1.9.225 R1, V1.9.256 R2, V1.9.269 R3, V1.9.283 R3b, V1.9.287 R3c, V1.9.289 R4a)
:toc:

NOTE: The `[kuml]` blocks of this document are rendered and validated by the `:docs-render:renderDocs` Gradle task.

Waves R1, R2, R3, R3b, R3c and R4a of the accountability report under the German Parties Act (section 24 PartG). R1 stores the **figures** of the report per reporting unit and year, typed in by
hand or imported from an Excel file. R2 (V1.9.256) turns them into a **draft** of the report as a PDF or an Excel file. The draft is marked "ENTWURF -- nicht amtlich" on every page and in
every file; nothing on the page promises a finished, official or audited report.

*This is a working aid, not legal advice.* The position list, the labels and the unit levels have not yet been checked against the official sample of the Bundestag
and the statute text, and the auditor has not seen the result (see <<not-verified>>).

== Who sees it

* Only an organisation with the setting *political party* (`isPoliticalParty`), and there only the roles **BOARD** and **ADMIN**. A treasurer does not see it (R4a keeps this unchanged, see Q21; the consolidation of R5 is federal only as well).
* **R4a, the chapter treasurer:** a plain organization member with an active *chapter-treasurer grant* (given by an ADMIN per chapter) reaches the *Übersicht*, *Werte erfassen* and *Kontenzuordnung* (read only) for the units `STATE:<chapter>` and `SUB:<chapter>` of the chapters granted. Every other unit answers "not found" on reading and "forbidden" on writing; the other tabs and every other method stay federal (BOARD/ADMIN). The gate order is: role (BOARD/ADMIN), then the grant (read fresh on every call, so a revoke is effective at once), then the party switch.
* The server checks the **role first** and the organisation switch second, on every RPC method and every HTTP route. The gate itself therefore reveals nothing to a person below the board
  (they get "forbidden", never "not enabled"). The fact is not secret otherwise: the session info (`politicalParty`) is sent to every logged-in member. Switching the setting off keeps all stored values; they are readable again after
  switching it on.
* Sidebar: group *Finanzen*, entry *Rechenschaftsbericht (PartG)*, route `/rechenschaftsbericht`.

== What a person does

Seven tabs (below 640 px a select with the label "Bereich" replaces the buttons), and a year picker (default: last year; 2001 up to the current year).

*Übersicht*:: one row per reporting unit (the federal level, each regional chapter, and the *summary* of its subordinate regional units), with a fill state
("Nicht erfasst", "Teilweise (n von 35)", "Vollständig"), the totals for income, expenses and net assets, and three icon actions: enter values, download the Excel
template, import an Excel file. A button *Bericht erzeugen* leads to the fourth tab.
*Werte erfassen*:: the 42 positions in the order of the statute. 35 are typed in, 7 are sums shown live (total income, material expenses, total expenses, surplus or
deficit, total assets, total liabilities, net assets). Every typed-in position has a field for the report year **and** one for the year before.
*Kontenzuordnung*:: which ledger account feeds which position, see <<ledger-link>>.
*Positionen*:: the fixed catalogue with the statute reference and the organisation's own hint per position (up to 500 characters, plain text), shown during entry.
*Ausweise*:: the separate statements A and B and the member count of one unit and year, see <<separate-statements>>.
*Erläuterungen*:: the notes of the organisation for the year, see <<notes>>.
*Bericht*:: see <<report-draft>>.

[[report-draft]]
== The report draft (R2)

On the tab *Bericht* a person picks the **scope**: the whole party (all units) or one unit, and reads a **checklist** of what is still missing. Then two buttons download the
draft: *PDF herunterladen* and *Excel herunterladen*. Both are always enabled: a gap never blocks the export (nothing is locked), it shows up in the file instead.

*Checklist*:: per unit "x of 35 positions not entered" (with the list of the positions and a link to the entry tab of that unit), the units that are complete as one line, and a
neutral note when the year before is missing or incomplete ("Vorjahr nicht erfasst -- Spalte bleibt leer"). A unit without subordinate regional units keeps its summary row open for
good: enter zeros with *Leere Felder mit 0 füllen* (see <<open-questions>>).
*Not entered is never 0*:: a position that nobody entered is printed as `n. e.` (PDF) or "nicht erfasst" (XLSX). A sum whose inputs are not all entered is printed with its partial amount
and `(unvollst.)`, and a footnote names the missing positions per unit. A share that cannot be computed (base is 0, or the number is partial) is `n. b.`
*Layout of the PDF*:: A4 landscape. Page 1: title, draft notice, completeness. Then the summary (the whole party or the unit, income Nr. 1 to 10 and expenses Nr. 1 to 3 as their own lines, total and
surplus, with shares of the total income and the total expenses, the year before beside it, and a table by level); the income matrix (one row per unit and the whole party, one column per income
position, report year first, then the year before); the expenses per unit; the assets and liabilities per unit (without share columns). Every page carries the header line, "Seite x von y", the
legend and a diagonal watermark behind the content. All sums include the subsidies between branches and are **not consolidated** (that follows in a later wave); the summary says so.
*Layout of the XLSX*:: thirteen fixed sheets: Vollständigkeit, Zusammenfassung, Einnahmen, Ausgaben, Vermögen, Ausweis A, Ausweis B, Ausweis C, Ausweis D, Mitglieder, Ausweis F, Erläuterungen, Hinweise (from Ausweis A on they are R3/R3b/R3c; since R3c, D has its own sheet right after C, before that it had none and the sheet "Vollständigkeit" carried a placeholder line; now that sheet only counts the enterprises of D, without amounts). Row 1 of every sheet is the PDF header line, the table starts in row 3 in the order
of the PDF. Amounts are real numbers (euro format, a negative result stays negative), shares are fractions with the format `0.0%`. There is no formula, no macro and no shared string; text that starts with
`=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is stored with the quote-prefix style.
*Audit*:: every successful export writes one entry (`PARTG_REPORT_EXPORT`, action `CREATE`) with the format, year, scope and counters. It holds no amount and no name.
*Limits*:: 10 exports per member and minute, two renderings at a time, at most 201 units, 600 pages and 8 MB per file. A larger report is refused with "too large".
*Known deviation*:: the minus sign in the PDF is the ASCII hyphen (`-1.234,56`), because the standard PDF fonts have no U+2212. The Excel file holds real negative numbers.

=== 0 is not the same as empty

An empty field means *not entered*; **0,00** is a value ("entered as zero"). "Leere Felder im Rechnungsjahr mit 0 füllen" asks first and fills only the empty fields of the
report year, never the year before. Deleting a field's content deletes the stored value.

=== The year before

The column "Vorjahr" is stored in **its own year** (with its own revision). That is how last year's figures arrive in the system although the instance did not exist
then, and how the "year before" column of a later report is filled.

== Excel import

1. Download the template of the unit and year (`partg-r1-1`): a protected sheet without a password, only the value cells unlocked, sum rows as formulas.
2. Fill it in Excel or LibreOffice. Empty cells change nothing.
3. *Excel-Datei importieren*: choose the file, *Prüfen* shows every change (old and new value) and every error (row, column, a fixed text per error code),
   *Übernehmen* writes **everything or nothing**. It is not possible while errors exist.

A file for another unit or year is refused (`TARGET_MISMATCH`), never taken over. Formulas are **not evaluated**: a typed-in cell with a formula takes the result stored in
the file and is marked in the preview; a formula without a stored result is an error. Macro files, files above 2 MB, and files with more than 500 rows of content are refused.

[[separate-statements]]
== Separate statements (R3)

Tab *Ausweise*, per unit and year. Only the board and admins see it (same gate as everything else); it shows personal data of third parties.

*A, deductions*:: four amounts: not assignable donations, cash donations over 1,000 EUR, donations over 3,300 EUR without a statement, donations reported in error. Empty is *not entered*; 0 is a value.
*Member count on 31 December*:: a whole number per unit. For the **current year** and a federal or state unit the page shows the number of active, non-anonymised members in the register as a *suggestion*
("Heute im Mitgliederregister: n (Stand TT.MM.JJJJ, nicht der 31.12.)") and a button that **only fills the field**. The register has no subordinate associations and does not know earlier years. In the report the
whole-party row shows the value of the federal unit; the units are **not summed** (a member is counted once, in the unit where it was entered).
*B, persons over 10,000 EUR*:: one row per person and unit: kind (natural or legal person), name, street, postal code, city, country (two capital letters), kind of contribution (donation, fee, both) and the yearly
amount. **Only more than 10,000.00 EUR is stored** (client, service and a database CHECK; 10,000.00 EUR is refused, 10,000.01 EUR accepted). At most 500 rows per unit and year; one row open at a time; the list is sorted by
amount (descending), name, id, so the *row number* of a hint changes when an amount changes. The PDF replaces characters outside WinAnsi (for instance Cyrillic) by `?`; the Excel file keeps them.
*Limit*:: the rule is applied **per unit and row**. A person with 6,000 EUR to the federal unit and 6,000 EUR to a state unit is in neither list; combining a person over units is R5 (open question Q1).

== Statements C and F (R3b, V1.9.283)

*C, sponsoring (draft)*:: one row per sponsor and unit with all of its services (date in the report year, amount, optional purpose). **All** sponsors and services are stored; whether a sponsor is part of the report is *computed*: a single service strictly above 750.00 EUR, or a yearly sum strictly above 6,000.00 EUR, per sponsor and unit (like B, services to several units are not combined). The PDF and XLSX list only these sponsors, with the reason (Einzelleistung, Jahressumme, beides) and, indented below, the single services above 750.00 EUR. The screen shows the state while typing (debounced 400 ms, `aria-live="polite"`, symbol plus words). Caps: 500 sponsors, 100 services per sponsor, 2000 per unit and year. Hints: P07/P08 (address missing/incomplete, all sponsors), P09 (under both limits, not in the report).
*D, business activity*:: since R3c (V1.9.287) recorded, see <<statement-d>>.
*F, youth organisations (draft)*:: name, relationship to the party (free text), income, expenses (each optional, empty = not entered); the result is computed (income minus expenses) and "nicht berechenbar" when one figure is missing. At most 20 per unit and year. Hint P10 (no figures).
*Pre-fill of B*:: not built. `journal_entry` has person keys, but neither it nor `posting`, `ledger_account` or `cost_center` has a reference to `regional_chapter`; a suggestion per unit would have to guess the unit (R5).
*Headings*:: "(Entwurf)" and no section number: the official layout and the paragraph references (section 24 (8a) and (12) PartG, per the owner's summary) are *unverified*.

[[notes]]
== Notes (R3)

Tab *Erläuterungen*, per year for the organisation. Five blocks, each with the switch *In den Bericht aufnehmen* and a text of at most 4,000 characters: general information on accounting, relief under section 28 PartG
(with the two choices for paragraph 1 and 3), holdings (plus up to 50 structured rows: name, seat, share in %, capital, result -- the result may be negative), media products, valuation of real estate.
A block that is switched off does not appear in the report, even if it holds text. The grey text in a field is a placeholder, never a value.

[[plausibility]]
== Plausibility hints (R3)

Hints above the download buttons, as a badge "n zu prüfen" per unit in the overview and next to the rows of the list. They never block anything, never contain a name, address or amount (only unit, row numbers, a position number),
and always show a symbol **and** words (amber "Bitte prüfen", neutral "Zur Info"; no red).

[cols="1,3,2",options="header"]
|===
| Code | Condition | Level
| P01 | street, postal code and city of a row are all empty | check
| P02 | at least one but not all of the three is empty | check
| P03 | deduction "cash over 1,000 EUR" is greater than 0 | check
| P04 | the row is a legal person | info
| P05 | a typed-in position differs from the year before by more than 1,000.00 EUR **and** by more than 50 percent (both strictly; integer arithmetic; a missing year before gives no hint; a year before of 0 counts the percentage as exceeded) | info
| P06 | gaps: no member count (federal always; a state unit once it has any data), a deduction field empty (units with an annex or values), the accounting-principles note not included (organisation) | check
| P07 to P10 | see statements C and F (R3b) | check or info
| P11 | statement D: the sum differs from E05 or A02F by more than 1.00 EUR, or the position is not entered (R3c) | check
| P12 | statement D: a row lacks income or expenses (R3c) | check
| P13 | statement D is empty although E05 or A02F is above 0 (R3c) | info
|===

At most 2,000 hints; when P05 would exceed that, the remaining ones are cut off and a single P05 info without position marks the cut. Units without any data produce no deduction hint, so 100 untouched chapters do not create noise.

[[statement-d]]
== Statement D: business activity (R3c, V1.9.287)

*What it is*:: one row per **own business operation** of a unit and year: name (required), legal form, seat, share of the party in percent, income, expenses (each optional; empty = *not entered*, 0 = a value). The result (income minus expenses) is **computed**, may be negative, and is "nicht berechenbar" when a figure is missing. At most 50 rows per unit and year (checked under the lock of the annex row). Rows are sorted by creation time, then id, so the row number does not change when something is typed.
*Table*:: `partg_report_enterprise` (migration V143, additive). It hangs on the annex of the unit and year and has **no link** to the holdings: holdings belong to the organisation and year (notes), enterprises to a unit; holdings stay the place for investments (BP03, E05A).
*R1 is read, never written*:: D does not pre-fill and does not overwrite E05 (income from business activity) or A02F (expenses). The screen shows the status of an adjustment as words and a symbol, **without any amount**: "stimmt überein", "weicht ab (mehr als 1,00 EUR)", "weicht ab (Position im Bericht nicht erfasst)" and "nicht vergleichbar, Angaben fehlen". A difference counts only when it is *strictly greater* than 1.00 EUR (100 cents; integer arithmetic). The PDF and the XLSX show D only and no adjustment.
*Sums*:: one sum row **per unit** (no sum over the whole party: that would pretend a consolidation which only R5 brings). A sum is "nicht berechenbar" when a row lacks that figure.
*Hints*:: P11 (check): the sum differs from E05 or A02F by more than 1.00 EUR, or the position is not entered; carries the position code, no row numbers, no amount. P12 (check): rows without complete figures. P13 (info): E05 or A02F is entered above zero but D has no row (also when the unit has no annex row yet; an entered 0 gives no hint).
*Write path*:: RPC `saveEnterprise` and `deleteEnterprise` (board and admin; party switch; write rate limit; revision fence). Audit entity `PARTG_REPORT_ANNEX` with the change codes `ENTERPRISE_ADDED`, `ENTERPRISE_UPDATED` and `ENTERPRISE_REMOVED` (note: `REMOVED`, while R3b uses `*_DELETED`), carrying ids and counters only, never a name or an amount.
*Data protection*:: the rows are organisation data; `created_by` and `updated_by` are "changed by you" data (exported without name and amount, retained on erasure). Whether a free-text name can hold a person's name is open, see Q16.
*Help text on the screen*:: "Hier tragen Sie eigene Geschäftsbetriebe ein (Positionen E05 und A02F). Beteiligungen an Unternehmen (BP03, E05A) gehören in die Erläuterungen. Bitte keine Namen von Personen eintragen."

[[ledger-link]]
== The link to the bookings (R4a, V1.9.289)

R4a connects the ledger to the report. It proposes values from the bookings; **nothing is written without a person taking it over**, and the proposal is recomputed on the server at that moment.

*Chapter on the booking*:: `journal_entry.regional_chapter_id` (nullable; `NULL` = federal level). It is set **only when the entry is created and never changed** (a posted entry is immutable and no RPC updates a chapter). Existing entries stay federal (no backfill). A federal caller books into the federal level or into an existing chapter; a chapter treasurer must name one of the chapters granted. The automatic bridges (contributions, donations, event fees, open items, travel expenses, volunteer allowances) always book on the federal level in R4a.
*Chapter treasurer*:: table `regional_chapter_treasurer`; a member may hold several active grants (at most 10 per member and 5 per chapter); only an ADMIN gives or revokes (*Gliederungsverwaltung*, "Kassenwart verwalten"). The treasurer sees *Kontenplan & Journal* with the journal, the general ledger and the cash book **of the granted chapters only**; the sidebar shows nothing else of the finance group. What the server allows is a fixed allowlist (`BookingScopeAllowlistScanTest`): the master data (accounts, cost centres, booking units), booking (draft, post, post a draft) and reading the journal, one entry, the general ledger and the cash book. Everything else (income statement, balance sheet, four-sphere statement, cost-centre report, donation duty, use of funds, annual statement, DATEV, VAT preview, exports, bank import, set-up, account maintenance, external donors) is **federal and unchanged**: those reports consolidate over all units. A single entry or a draft of a foreign chapter answers "not found". **Donations are federal only** (`ChapterDonationFederalOnlyException`): the party donation checks are organisation-wide and would disclose prior donation totals of other units; the donor fields are also hidden from the treasurer's journal view (the category stays).
*Cash check per unit*:: the rule "the cash stock never becomes negative" is checked per (account, chapter). A chapter should keep its own cash accounts; the cash book of the federal level without a filter shows the sum of all units, the filter separates them.
*Mapping account -> position*:: table `partg_account_mapping` (one row per account; a missing row = not mapped). The target is a **LEAF** position of the matching section (income account -> income leaf, expense -> expense, asset -> asset, liability -> liability); a sum is never a target; an equity account can only be "Bewusst nicht im Bericht". The rule is `PartgLedgerRules` (shared, used by the client and re-checked by the server). Federal roles save (at most 2000 changes per call, one transaction under the settings-row lock, each change names the expected old state, a mismatch is a conflict). Audit entity `PARTG_ACCOUNT_MAPPING` with account number and codes, no amount, no name.
*Suggestions*:: only "aus Parteien-Kontenplan": the template `StandardPartyChart` (version `partei-skr42-2026-11`) suggests a position per account number. A suggestion fills only unmapped accounts, is drawn dashed with a tick box, and marks nothing until it is ticked, taken over ("Ausgewählte übernehmen") **and saved** ("Zuordnung speichern"; the bar says "Nicht gespeichert"). The button "Parteien-Kontenplan anlegen" (set-up step *Kontenplan*, TREASURER/ADMIN, parties only) creates the missing accounts idempotently: a number that exists with the same name is skipped, one with another name is reported ("Übersprungen (anderer Name)") and never renamed. It creates **no mapping**.
*Mode per unit and year*:: `partg_report_unit_mode`: **A** central booking in Lapis, **B** own cash booked in Lapis, **C** own cash outside Lapis (default; a missing row = C). A summary of sub-units (`SUB:`) is always C and never stored. Only federal roles change a mode. With bookings in that unit and year the caller must confirm the **current number** of bookings ("Für %1 gibt es im Jahr %2 bereits %3 Buchungen. Die Buchungen bleiben unverändert."); a stale or missing number is refused. The bookings are never touched. Audit entity `PARTG_UNIT_MODE` (the count, no amount).
*Proposal*:: only in mode A or B for a federal or state unit. Income and expense positions are the **flows of the report year** (posted entries, entry date in the year), asset and liability positions the **balances at 31 December** (cumulative). The sign follows the section (income and liability: credit minus debit; expense and asset: debit minus credit). A position gets a proposal iff at least one account is mapped to it. A **negative** value is shown but cannot be taken over. The dialog "Vorschlag aus Buchungen" lists only the differences (entered | from the bookings | difference). A value that was typed in or imported is **never pre-ticked**; an empty value or one that itself came from the bookings is.
*Taking over*:: `applyLedgerProposal` takes the chosen positions with source **`LEDGER`**. The server **recomputes under the lock** (chapter row, sheet of the year before, sheet of the year; revisions compared) and refuses a pick whose amount differs, is negative or does not exist (`PartyReportProposalStaleException`: "Buchungen haben sich geändert, Vorschlag neu laden"). Only values of the report year are written. A hand change turns a `LEDGER` value into `MANUAL`; an unchanged value keeps its source. The entry tab shows "aus Buchungen" below such a value. Audit: one entry per value and a collective entry with the count and the codes.
*Hints*:: **P14** (check, mode A/B): accounts without a position that have postings in the year (a count, target: the mapping tab). **P15** (check, mode C): the unit has bookings in the year (a count, target: the overview). **P16** (info): a value taken from the bookings no longer equals the current proposal. None carries an amount or a name. **Behaviour change:** an existing party organisation that already books in Lapis gets P15 for the federal unit at once (the default is C); that is intended.
*Exports*:: the DATEV export, the Lexoffice/sevDesk push and the R2 PDF/XLSX are unchanged; the chapter is **not** exported (Q21-follow-up). The R2 export is still federal.
*Data protection*:: all new tables are organisation data. `updated_by` of the mapping and the mode and `granted_by_member_id` of a grant are "changed by you" data (exported without amount and name); the active grants of an erased member are revoked at once, the rows stay as history.

== Consolidation over the units (R5, V1.9.290)

R5 puts the units together to the **whole party** and takes the transfers between them out. It is a **draft**: nothing here is an official, filed or audited statement, and a value that cannot be computed is "n. b.", never 0.

*Who sees it*:: the federal roles (BOARD, ADMIN) in the tab *Konsolidierung* (last, rightmost; below 640 px an entry of the select "Bereich") and in the first row of the overview. A chapter treasurer does not see the tab, does not call the service and does not see the hints P17 to P21, not even for their own unit. The server refuses all five calls and the whole-party export for them (the gate is the unscoped one).
*Included and excluded units*:: a unit is part of the total when it has **at least one stored value** (any source) in the year. Every other unit is listed as *excluded* with its reason ("keine Daten", or "Buchungen vorhanden, nicht übernommen" when it books in Lapis in mode A or B and has posted bookings but no value was taken over). An excluded unit is **never counted as 0** and is no reason for "n. b.". The same list exists for the year before.
*The 42 positions*:: four columns: sum of the units (the old "Gesamtpartei" column, now honestly named), eliminated, consolidated, year before. A leaf is a number only if **every included unit** entered it; else "n. b." with the number of units without a value. A sum is a number only if all its inputs are (same formula `PartgPositions.sums`, same inputs `PartgReportComputation.inputsOf`).
*The matching table*:: a **pair** is the yearly sum of one relationship between two units; one pair per (year, kind, payer, payee). Kind SUBSIDY: payer's A03 against payee's E10. Kind BALANCE: payer's BP05 against payee's SP04. Each side's amount is optional, at least one is needed. **Eliminated is the minimum** of the two sides, from **both** positions, and **only** if both units are included and both amounts are stated; a pair with one side is shown as "Gegenseite fehlt" and eliminates nothing. A difference between the two sides, or between a unit's entered value and what the pairs account for, stays in the list "Nicht abgeglichen"; it is never booked away. If the elimination of a position is larger than its sum the cell is "n. b." -- it is **never capped**. The result (RES) and the net assets (NET) are the same consolidated and as a sum of the units (a symmetric elimination does not move them; tested). The year before is consolidated with its own pairs; if it has none although it reports transfer positions, the column says so.
*Statement B over all units*:: computed from the B rows of all units. A **group** (made by hand) of 2 to 20 rows of **different** units of the same year is one person: the display row is the one with the highest amount (tie: smallest id), the amount is the sum. The server only **suggests** candidates (same kind, name after NFKD normalisation without diacritics, lower case and collapsed white space, **non-empty** postal code, same country, different units); they are computed on each call, never stored, and carry ids only. Combining asks for confirmation naming the units; dissolving gives the rows back as singles. A group whose row was deleted keeps standing but is ignored below two rows and raises P21. The fixed notice about the open legal question (Q1) is always under the list.
*Hints P17 to P21* (federal view; counts and codes only, target "Konsolidierung"):: *P17* (check) the pairs of a unit and side differ from A03, E10, BP05 or SP04 by **strictly more than 100 cents**, or the position is not entered; *P18* (check) a pair has one amount only or names a unit without data (count per payer unit); *P19* (info) a transfer position is above zero and the unit has no pair on that side; *P20* (info) number of candidates; *P21* (check) a group with fewer than two rows, or a row changed after the group was made (count). They share the cap of 2000 with all other hints.
*Export*:: a whole-party PDF gets the section **appended after all existing pages** ("Gesamtpartei (konsolidiert) -- Entwurf, nicht geprüft": the 42 positions with four columns, the matching table, the list "Nicht abgeglichen", statement B consolidated); the XLSX of the whole party gets the sheets 14 "Konsolidierung", 15 "Abgleich" and 16 "Ausweis B (konsolidiert)" after "Hinweise". The export of one unit is unchanged (13 sheets). The free-text note of a pair is **not** exported. Rule for the cells: "n. b." is text, never 0; no formula; text starting with `= + - @` is quote-prefixed.
*Audit and data protection*:: audit entities `PARTG_INTRA_TRANSFER` (codes `TRANSFER_ADDED/_UPDATED/_DELETED`; year, kind, both unit keys, two flags) and `PARTG_DONOR_COMBINATION` (codes `COMBINATION_CREATED/_DISSOLVED`; year, row count, unit keys, donor row ids). **Never a name, a note or an amount.** The four new tables are organisation data; `created_by` and `updated_by` are "changed by you" data (year and time), retained on erasure. The combination row holds only two ids; the donor rows stay the documented third-party gap.
*GoBD*:: no journal entry is read for writing or changed. A "counter-unit on the booking" was deliberately not built.

== What R1 to R5 do not do

* R2 to R5 produce a draft only. No official, filed or audited report, no signature, no submission (R6).
* **R5 (done):** consolidation over the units, matching table, combining donor rows by hand. **R5b (not built):** a proposal for B from the bookings (only the federal level, through the donor keys of the donation) and the entry of **partial amounts below 10,000 EUR**. The partial amounts are a new collection of data below the legal threshold and therefore a legal question of its own (Q1). **The case 6,000 + 6,000 EUR is still not recognised.**
* Not in R5: C over units (Q6), a whole-party sum of D (Q15), transfers inside the units of one `SUB:` summary (Q25).

== Older limits (R1, R2)

* R2 produces a draft only. No official, filed or audited report; no consolidation of the subsidies between branches (R5); no signature, no submission (R6).
* No mapping from the ledger accounts to the positions, no consolidation, no auditor access, no workflow (R4 to R6).
* Subordinate regional units are **one summary row per regional chapter**, not the individual district associations. Whether that is enough for the report of 2025 is an
  open question (the auditor picks at least ten individual subordinate units, section 29 PartG).
* A pending import preview lives **in memory**: a restart of the server drops it and the person uploads the file again.

[[open-questions]]
== Open questions

. **Q1 (R3): per unit or for the whole party?** Does the 10,000 EUR limit of section 25 (3) PartG apply per regional unit or for the whole party? R3 implements per unit and row and says so in the UI, because section 25 was not read in full. Combining a person over units would be R5.
. **Q2 (R3): member count.** Entered per unit; the whole-party row shows the federal value and is **not summed**. Whether the Bundestag sample wants a split by state is open. P06 asks for a count only for the federal and state units.
. **Q3 (R3): who is a member?** The suggestion counts `ACTIVE` members that are not anonymised (as the public statistics do). Family members, guests, friends and donors do not count. The board has to confirm that.
. **Q4 (R3): who deletes after the retention period?** Nothing is deleted automatically; a deletion routine would come with R6.
. **Q5 (R3): are P05 and P06 part of the sample or only a convenience?** They are hints only; the risk of a wrong one is low.
. **Q6 (R3b): C per unit or for the whole party?** Implemented per sponsor and unit (like B); combining a sponsor over units would be R5.
. **Q7 (R3b): sponsoring versus donation.** What counts as sponsoring (service in return) and what as a donation is a legal question; the board decides per entry.
. **Q8 (R3b): which services does the sample list** when the yearly-sum rule applies? R3b lists only services above 750.00 EUR singly.
. **Q9 (R3b): official layout of C, D and F** (columns, order, headings) is unknown.
. **Q10 (R3b): relationship to the party in F** is free text; how the sample determines it is unknown.
. **Q11 (R3b, R3c): D versus the holdings and E05/A02F:** R3c implements D as a breakdown of E05 and A02F by own business operation and keeps holdings in the notes. Whether the official sample also asks D for holdings, or for a breakdown by media or real estate, cannot be said without the sample.
. **Q12 (R3b): pre-fill of B without a unit on the bookings** (R5).
. **Q13 (R3b): data minimisation.** Sponsors under both limits are stored (the yearly sum needs them) although their data is not in the report; P09 makes them visible. Is that acceptable?
. **Q14 (R3c): is the tolerance of 1.00 EUR appropriate** for the adjustment of D against E05/A02F (strictly more than 100 cents counts as a difference)?
. **Q15 (R3c): are the columns right, and is a whole-party sum needed?** D lists name, legal form, seat, share, income, expenses and the computed result, with one sum per unit and none over the whole party (that would be a consolidation, R5).
. **Q16 (R3c): names of persons.** The name of a sole proprietorship can be the name of a person. The help text asks not to enter names of persons; nothing prevents it technically. How to treat this under data protection law is open; the table is not registered as a third-party data root.
. **Q17 (R4a): cash basis or accrual?** The PartG asks for income and expenses; the proposal sums the posted income and expense accounts of the year. Whether receivables, liabilities and deferrals falsify that is open.
. **Q18 (R4a): valuation of assets at the closing date.** The asset and liability positions are the cumulative book balances at 31 December. Valuation rules (depreciation, provisions) are not applied.
. **Q19 (R4a): transfers between units.** Subsidies between branches (E10 / A03) and receivables from or liabilities to branches (BP05, SP04) are booked by hand; the consolidation (R5, V1.9.290) takes them out with a matching table, see Q24.
. **Q20 (R4a): bookings of sub-units.** A summary of sub-units is always mode C; whether a level below the state unit is needed on the booking is open.
. **Q21 (R4a): who may edit mapping and mode?** The design names TREASURER and ADMIN, the report is BOARD and ADMIN since R1. R4a keeps BOARD/ADMIN. The treasurer's access to the report, their own exports and a journal CSV with the "Gliederung" column are R4b.
. **Q22 (R4a): chart of accounts and positions.** The numbers, names and suggested positions of the party chart are assumptions (confidence LOW or MED) and must be confirmed by the tax advisor or auditor.
. **Q23 (R4a): booking of cash per chapter.** Is a per-(account, chapter) cash check what the finance board wants, or should chapters be forced to own cash accounts?
. **Q24 (R5): the elimination rule.** Is the **minimum** of the two sides the right amount to eliminate, and how are differences treated (shown, explained, adjusted)? R5 eliminates the minimum, shows the rest and adjusts nothing. The auditor or the tax advisor has to confirm it. *Status: open.*
. **Q25 (R5): transfers inside a `SUB:` summary.** Payments between subordinate branches of **one** state sit inside one unit (`SUB:`) and cannot be eliminated with the matching table. Default: documented, not built.
. **Q26 (R5): audit without amounts.** The audit entries carry no amount (the design forbids it), so "who changed an amount" is only visible through `updated_by` and `updated_at` of the row. Is that enough for the GoBD review? Default: keep it.
. **Q27 (R5): the free-text note of a pair.** Kept (at most 200 characters, never in the audit, a log or an export) or dropped for data minimisation? The help text asks not to enter names of persons; nothing prevents it technically.

. **Summary content.** The sample was read as: income Nr. 1 to 9 and expenses Nr. 1 to 2 for the whole party, with totals with and without subsidies from branches. R2 shows Nr. 1 to 10
  and Nr. 1 to 3, each on its own labelled line, the totals "including subsidies, not consolidated (follows in R5)" and the table by level only in that variant. A column "without subsidies"
  (E11 minus E10, A04 minus A03) is trivial to add. The row order lives in `PartgReportLayout.SUMMARY_INCOME_CODES` / `SUMMARY_EXPENSE_CODES`.
. **Units without subordinate regional units.** A regional chapter always has a `SUB:` unit, which stays "not entered" until zeros are entered; there is no "does not apply" marker (it would need a
  migration and a legal decision). R3 or R6 may add one.
. **Order of the units in the matrix.** The order of `units()`: federal level, then per regional chapter (by name) the state unit and its subordinate summary, then the whole party. The sample's
  grouping ("Landesverbände A to D") and the place of a "nachgeordnete" summary row are not confirmed.
. **Share columns of the balance.** Shares are defined against the total income or total expenses only, so the assets and liabilities have no share columns.
. **Position list** against the sample, the matrix axes, and whether Nr. 10 and Nr. 3 belong on the summary page.
. The column "-" of the R1 overview for a total that is not entered stays as it is (a candidate for the same "n. e." wording).
