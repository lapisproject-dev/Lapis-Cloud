= Travel expense reimbursement (V1.4.11, PdV form V1.9.122)
:toc:

NOTE: V1.9.122 reworks the editor and the calculation to follow the PdV travel expense form (template 2024, notes of
23.07.2025). The V1.4.11 text below still describes the state machine, the four-eyes rule, the receipt handling and
the booking, all unchanged; the sections "PdV form (V1.9.122)" and "Settings and contact person" describe what is new, and the
limitations list at the end names what the new model deliberately does not do.

Any authenticated member can file a self-service travel expense report for a board/functionary
trip — "board and functionaries" describes the real usage, not a technical restriction. A report
carries one **day line per travel day** (the day allowance, see below) plus further lines: **mileage**
(kilometers × an operator-configured rate), and four receipted kinds -- **public transport**, **accommodation**,
**hospitality** and **other** (a fixed amount plus at least one uploaded receipt). (Before V1.9.122 there were three kinds: mileage, a flat
**per diem** and generic **receipted**; reports created then stay readable and submittable.) A line's kind is fixed once created — no dropdown,
separate "add line" actions instead. A draft can be edited freely; `submitReport`
(`DRAFT -> REQUESTED`) validates every line, requires a rate to be configured for every kind
actually used, and **freezes** each mileage/per-diem line's rate into `rate_snapshot` — a later rate
change never retroactively changes an already-submitted report. BOARD/ADMIN decide in a queue at
`/travel-expense-approvals` (**not** TREASURER, same narrower gate `/contribution-relief` already
established) with a mandatory decision note on both approval and rejection, and a **four-eyes rule
that closes a gap the contribution-relief queue leaves open**: neither the report's subject nor
whoever submitted it "on behalf of" them may decide it (`decideReport`'s own `ForbiddenException`
covers both `subjectMemberId` and `requestedBy`). Approval books an EXPENSE-side debit per line
against an operator-configured `travelExpenseAccountId` and one CREDIT for the total against the
payment bank account (`TravelExpensePostingBridge`) — the first "money out" bridge in this
codebase — in the same transaction as the decision; a failed booking (unconfigured/inactive/
wrong-type account, or a transient cash-register shortfall) leaves the report `APPROVED` with a
recorded `executionError`, retryable via a dedicated RPC method exactly like the contribution-
relief precedent. Receipt files travel over dedicated HTTP routes (never Kilua RPC), with the
declared `Content-Type` discarded entirely in favor of a server-side magic-byte sniff
(`application/pdf`/`image/jpeg`/`image/png` only, deliberately no `image/svg+xml`), a 10 MiB
streaming cap, and an IDOR gate on download (subject/requester/BOARD/ADMIN only). See
`docs/architecture/travel-expenses.adoc` for the full state machine, booking shape, and security
model.

== PdV form (V1.9.122)

=== The day grid

`createDraft`/`updateDraft` keep **exactly one `DAY_ALLOWANCE` line per travel day** (at most 31 days). The member fills one fieldset per
day: departure and/or arrival time (which fields appear depends on the day's *position* in the trip, see below), the three meals that were
provided, and an optional destination. "Tage speichern" calls `saveDays`; the server recomputes **every** amount with the shared
calculator -- the client shows the same numbers live, as a clearly marked preview ("Vorschau -- nicht gespeichert"), but never supplies one.

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Position | Time rule

| single day
| departure and arrival, arrival not before departure; duration = arrival − departure

| first day
| departure only; the day runs until 24:00, duration = 24:00 − departure

| middle day
| no times; always 24 h

| last day
| arrival only; the day starts at 00:00, duration = arrival
|===

The position rule lives in `MultiDayRule` -- **the one place** that decides how an arrival/departure day counts (the statute, section 9 paragraph 4a EStG,
pays a flat amount for those days on a trip with an overnight stay; the form and the design team ask for a count by hours; this is documented
as an open question to the treasurer in the CHANGELOG). Night trips across midnight are **not** added up, and a daylight-saving shift is
ignored (wall clock without a time zone).

=== Tiers, meals, rounding

* Three ascending thresholds in hours (default **8 / 14 / 24**) and three amounts (default **6,00 / 12,00 / 28,00 EUR**). The thresholds are **inclusive**
  ("ab", as printed on the form): exactly 8 h already earns the first tier. `PerDiemTierRule` is the one place.
* A provided breakfast / lunch / dinner reduces the day by a percentage (default **20 / 40 / 40 %**) of the **third tier's** amount. Each deduction is
  rounded **HALF_UP to whole cents** (the single rounding rule of the whole calculation); the sum of the deductions is **capped at the day's
  allowance** (a day never goes negative).
* An overnight stay **with breakfast** supplies the breakfast of the following mornings: the server deducts it whatever the client sends, and the grid shows
  the box checked and locked with the reason.
* Mileage: whole kilometers 1..5000 times the mileage rate, plus the **passenger surcharge** (default 0,02 EUR per km and passenger, 0..8 passengers).
  The mileage rate has **no default** (`NULL` = not offered), exactly as in V1.4.11.
* Everything is calculated in whole cents with overflow checks; nothing uses floating point.

=== Advance, payout, donation waiver

The member may enter an **advance** already received and **must choose** how to settle, **without a preselection**: *Auszahlen* (pay out) or
*Spendenquittung (Aufwandsverzicht)* (waive the reimbursement and ask for a donation receipt). The donation receipt itself is issued **outside
Lapis**. The payout amount is `total − advance` and never negative; an advance **above the total** is not supported (submit refuses it and points
to the contact person).

=== Submission

`submitReport` additionally requires: a payout choice; complete day times (a day that cannot be calculated is refused, never frozen as 0);
a receipt on every public-transport, accommodation, hospitality and other line (a **map proof for mileage is optional**); a total above 0.
It **freezes the whole rate set** as JSON in `rates_snapshot` (so a later settings change never touches a submitted report) and the member's
membership number in `membership_number_snapshot` (may be empty). The informational submission deadline is 31 December of the travel year: a hint
is shown after it, **it never blocks**.

=== Booking

With an advance and/or a donation waiver the credit side splits (see `docs/architecture/travel-expenses.adoc`): *bank* for the payout,
*advance account* for the advance, *donation-waiver income account* for a waived amount -- never the bank, because no money flows.

== Settings and contact person

The ADMIN-only page **`/travel-expense-settings`** (sidebar: System → Reisekosten-Einstellungen) holds: the three tiers (hours and amounts), the three meal
percentages, the mileage rate and passenger surcharge, the legacy flat per-day rate (only for reports created before V1.9.122), the two optional account
mappings (advance, donation waiver) and the **contact person** (name, e-mail, phone, free hint text) that every member sees at the top of the travel
expense page. "Standardwerte der PdV-Vorlage übernehmen" only **fills the form**; nothing is saved until "Speichern". **This is where the contact
person is entered** -- the block stays empty ("Kontaktdaten werden noch ergänzt.") until an administrator does so; nothing is pre-filled.

Ranges (validated by the server before the database): tier hours 1 ≤ h1 < h2 < h3 ≤ 24; tier amounts 0 ≤ a1 ≤ a2 ≤ a3 ≤ 100,00 EUR; meal percentages
0..100; passenger surcharge 0..0,20 EUR/km; mileage rate 0,01..1,00 EUR/km (whole cents); contact fields trimmed, blank becomes `NULL`, e-mail validated
(no CR/LF), phone `^[+0-9 ()/\-]{3,40}$`, hint plain text only.

CAUTION: The default values are the **PdV template** -- provisional and PdV-specific, *not* the statutory 14 / 28 EUR. They are database column defaults and
therefore take effect on **every** instance until an administrator reviews them; the settings page says so in a fixed notice.

=== The calculation core in one picture

The pure functions live in `lapis-shared` (`commonMain`, so the server and the browser run the very same code); the server persists what they return.

[source,kuml,name="travel-expense-calculator"]
----
classDiagram(name = "Travel expense calculation core") {
    val rateSet = classOf(name = "TravelExpenseRateSet") {
        attribute(name = "tierHours", type = "List<Int>") // 8, 14, 24 -- inclusive
        attribute(name = "tierCents", type = "List<Long>") // 600, 1200, 2800
        attribute(name = "breakfastPercent", type = "Int") // 20, of tier 3
        attribute(name = "mileageCentsPerKm", type = "Long") // null = not offered
        attribute(name = "passengerCentsPerKm", type = "Long") // 2
    }
    val multiDay = classOf(name = "MultiDayRule") {
        operation(name = "positionOf") { returns("DayPosition") }
        operation(name = "absenceMinutes") { returns("Int") }
    }
    val tier = classOf(name = "PerDiemTierRule") { operation(name = "tierFor") { returns("Long") } }
    val meals = classOf(name = "MealDeductionRule") { operation(name = "deductionCents") { returns("Long") } }
    val calculator = classOf(name = "TravelExpenseCalculator") {
        operation(name = "day") { returns("CalcResult<DayResult>") }
        operation(name = "mileage") { returns("CalcResult<Long>") }
        operation(name = "totals") { returns("Totals") }
    }
    val snapshot = classOf(name = "ratesSnapshot (JSON, frozen at submit)") { attribute(name = "version", type = "Int") }

    association(source = calculator, target = multiDay) { target { multiplicity("1") } }
    association(source = calculator, target = tier) { target { multiplicity("1") } }
    association(source = calculator, target = meals) { target { multiplicity("1") } }
    association(source = calculator, target = rateSet, id = "uses") { target { multiplicity("1") } }
    association(source = rateSet, target = snapshot, id = "frozen") { target { multiplicity("0..1") } }
}
----

== What doesn't work yet (V1.9.122)

* The default tier values are provisional; the PdV form's footnote mentions 24 EUR in one place and 28 EUR in another -- 28 EUR is used.
* Thresholds are inclusive ("ab"), not the statutory "more than".
* An arrival/departure day is counted by hours (see `MultiDayRule`), not as the statutory flat 14 EUR.
* Night trips across midnight are not added up.
* An advance larger than the claim is not supported.
* No SEPA payment file, no donation receipt (Zuwendungsbestätigung) generation, no VAT columns.
* The contact person is empty until an administrator enters it.
* The help link on the travel expense page points to `+<platform>/de/help/travel-expenses+` in German and `+<platform>/help/travel-expenses+` in every other UI language, which answers 404 until the website release that carries the page.
* Old MILEAGE lines with fractional kilometers cannot be submitted any more (`mileage_fractional_km_reenter`) -- enter the line again.
* A legacy mileage rate stored with sub-cent digits is rounded HALF_UP to whole cents when the rate set is built.

== What doesn't work yet (V1.4.11, partly superseded)

Mileage and per-diem rates are **operator-configured values with no automatic adjustment to
changes in the law** — `NULL` means "not configured", and the affected line kind is disabled in
the form until an ADMIN sets it; there is no rate history, only the currently configured value is
editable, and a submitted report is immune to a later change because its rate is frozen. No
Verpflegungsmehraufwand reduction per the full German travel-expense rules (arrival/departure-day
special cases, cuts for meals provided by the organization) — a flat per-diem × days calculation
is a nachweis aid for the board, not an automated compliance decision, the same framing the
Mittelverwendungsrechnung caveat already uses elsewhere in this document. **No payout** — the end
state reads "Zur Auszahlung gebucht" ("Posted for payout") and means exactly that: a journal
entry. There is no payment run in this wave; the treasury arranges the transfer separately. No
line-by-line partial approval; rejection with a mandatory reason plus "copy as draft" is the
intended correction path (receipts are not copied — new line ids — and must be re-uploaded). The
Übungsleiter-/Ehrenamtspauschale gap this section used to name here is closed by V1.4.12 as its
own, separate feature and bridge — see "Volunteer/instructor allowance" below; no shared
abstraction was forced between the two domains. The
Gemeinnützigkeit sphere every posting uses is hardcoded to `IDEELLER_BEREICH`, not configurable
(a board trip is practically always ideeller Bereich under Rams' "one account, one field" logic
applied to the sphere; revisit if a Zweckbetrieb-funded trip ever needs this). Receipt files are
**not** removed by a DSGVO erasure once a report has left `DRAFT` — they *are* the accounting
voucher (§147 AO); unlike the contribution-relief wave's Art. 9 free text, there is no automatic
redaction sweep here, because there is no Art. 9 free text to redact and §147 AO already demands
10 years' retention regardless. Inline receipt preview was deliberately not built (attachment-only
download, `nosniff`, no `image/svg+xml` in the allowlist).
