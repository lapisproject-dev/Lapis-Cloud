= Donation conversion at the gold price of a reference date
:toc:

NOTE: Off by default. Not reviewed by a tax adviser. Architecture: link:../architecture/price-oracle-reference-date.adoc[price-oracle-reference-date].

== Turning it on (administrator)

. Set `LAPIS_ORACLE_SNAPSHOT_ENABLED=true` on one instance (see the deploy README). Without the hourly price history every reference-date rate has to be confirmed by hand.
. Price oracle, edit the configuration: anchor *Gold (XAU)*, conversion rate *Gold price at the reference date*, save. The setting is only available with the gold anchor.

== Converting a donation (treasurer, board, administrator)

. Open the price oracle, section "Convert donation to LTR". Choose the member, then the *donation* (date and amount). The amount is the donation's and cannot be changed; the reference date is pre-filled with the day the bank booked the donation (yesterday at the earliest). A member without an open donation cannot be converted for. Since V175 every captured donation is offered: bank donations, PayPal and Stripe donations. Anonymous donations (no member) are never offered and can never be converted.
. Press "Check the rate for the reference date". The page shows the reference date, the day whose rate is used (a weekend or a London bank holiday moves it to the day before), the basis, the sources with their raw values and the rate.
. Press "Convert to LTR" and confirm. The booking uses exactly the rate you saw; if it changed in the meantime, nothing is booked and you check again.

== Donations without a bank booking (PayPal, Stripe)

A donation that is not backed by exactly one posted bank line has no booking day. The list shows it with the day it arrived and the channel ("kein Buchungstag"). The reference date is then *always* chosen by hand: the date field starts empty, the reason field is shown at once and is required (10 to 500 characters, no IBAN or account number), and the receipt says "chosen manually". The server derives the origin itself and never takes it from the form.

== A different reference date

If you change the reference date to another day than the booking day, a text field "Reason for the different reporting date" appears (10 to 500 characters, no IBAN or account number). It is required; "Reset to booking day" removes it again. The receipt shows the origin of the date and the reason. A donation can be converted once only; trying again (also through another entry) is refused.

== Existing data (V175)

The V175 migration creates the missing donations and links earlier "free" conversions (made before the donation entity, without a payment) to their donation, but only when the match is unambiguous in both directions (one free donation of that member, amount and currency, and one such conversion). The date stays "manual" and gets the text "Altbestand vor V175: nachträglich mit Spende verknüpft, Stichtag ohne Begründung erfasst". All others stay as they were and are shown as "Altbestand: ohne Spende erfasst". The migration header carries the diagnosis queries (counts only). **A backup taken after V175 cannot be restored onto a server that is still before V175.**

== When there is no rate

The page says why (no stored rate for that day, quorum not reached, or an older price history without the raw values) and offers "Confirm the rate manually": enter the rate and the evidence (source and reason, 20 to 2000 characters). A confirmed rate cannot be changed. If it deviates strongly from the stored price of that day you must acknowledge the deviation. A member who confirmed a rate cannot receive LTR converted at it; another person books it.
