= Staging test plan: opinion polls in the web client (V1.9.31, V1.9.41)
:toc:

== Purpose

Everything in V1.9.31 is covered by unit, DOM and tripwire tests against stubbed servers. What only a real server can show: the anonymity of a
real answer (database rows), the open-poll limits against real data, the lazy deadline, and the behaviour on two devices at once. This plan is
the mandatory part of the wave's acceptance. It was **not executed** by the author of the wave: nothing was deployed (access data and the
staging server belong to the operator), so every row of "Actual results" below is still open.

== Set-up

. Accounts: one ADMIN, one BOARD, one committee CHAIR (active seat in an active committee), one ACTIVE member without any seat, one FRIEND, one guest.
. Two ACTIVE members who can be logged in on two devices (or two browser profiles) at the same time.
. Browser language German for the first pass, English for step 18.

== Steps

[cols="1,4,4", options="header"]
|===
| # | Action | Expected
| 1 | As ACTIVE member without a seat open "Umfragen" | the list shows "Zurzeit keine offenen Umfragen." or the open polls; there is no button "Neue Umfrage"
| 2 | As ADMIN, BOARD and CHAIR in turn open "Umfragen" | each sees "Neue Umfrage" in the title row
| 3 | As CHAIR: create a poll with the defaults (question, two options) | the poll opens; the deadline is 7 days ahead; the page shows the box "Unverbindlich -- ein Stimmungsbild, kein Beschluss."
| 4 | In the form: leave the question empty, use a 501 character question, enter the same option twice (also with other case and spaces), add options up to ten | the start button stays disabled and the first reason is shown under it; a duplicate is named at the second field; the eleventh option cannot be added
| 5 | Create one poll with "Ohne Frist" and one with "Eigenes Datum" 10 minutes ahead | "Sie müssen die Umfrage dann selbst schließen." for the first; the second is refused by the form
| 6 | Create polls until the limits are hit (5 open polls of one creator, then 10 within 24 hours) | after the limit, one fixed sentence ("Zurzeit können Sie keine weitere Umfrage starten ...") and no server text
| 7 | Open a poll as the two members on two devices | each sees the booth, nothing is preselected, "Weiter" is disabled until a choice is made
| 8 | Member 1: choose, "Weiter", read the check step, "Zurück", "Weiter", "Endgültig abgeben" -- double-click the last button | exactly one answer; "Ihre Antwort ist gespeichert."; the option text is nowhere on the page afterwards
| 9 | Member 1 reloads the detail | "Sie haben an dieser Umfrage teilgenommen."; no booth; no count
| 10 | While the poll is open run in the database: `SELECT option_id FROM poll_response` and `SELECT member_id FROM poll_participation` | the two tables share no key; no member is next to an option; no time column
| 11 | As the creator open the detail | "Umfrage schließen" and "Umfrage abbrechen" are shown below the booth area; as another CHAIR they are not
| 12 | Close the poll with fewer than 5 answers | the result shows, in both blocks, "Zu wenige Antworten für eine anonyme Auswertung (mindestens 5)."; no numbers
| 13 | Run a poll to exactly 4 and exactly 5 answers (all different members) and close both | 4: withheld; 5: head block with percents and counts, weighted block either numbers or one of the four reasons
| 14 | Close a poll with weighted result available | two blocks side by side at 900 px and wider, one column below; options in the order of the poll, no winner highlighted, no bold; the explaining sentence is under "Nach LTR-Gewicht"
| 15 | Abort a poll: type a wrong word, then "ABBRECHEN" | the confirm button stays disabled until the exact word; the close button reads "Zurück"; afterwards "Diese Umfrage wurde abgebrochen. Die Antworten werden nie ausgewertet."
| 16 | Let the deadline of an open poll pass while the detail is open on a device, then answer | no error toast; the booth says "Die Umfrage ist beendet."
| 17 | As FRIEND and as guest open `/polls` and `/polls/<an id>` directly | silent redirect to the start page, no toast, no server text; the navigation has no "Umfragen" entry
| 18 | Switch to English and walk through steps 3, 8, 12, 15 | every text is English; the typed keyword is the English one ("ABORT")
| 19 | A poll whose question, option and creator name contain `###KvI18nS###` and `<img ...>` | shown as plain text in the list, the detail, the booth, the check step and the result
| 20 | Phone width (below 900 px) | the list is a card list, the result is one column, nothing scrolls sideways
|===

== Steps for the consensus kinds (V1.9.41)

Additional accounts: five further ACTIVE members (a result needs at least five answers). Browser language German first, then English.

[cols="1,4,4", options="header"]
|===
| # | Action | Expected
| 21 | As CHAIR open "Umfragen" | the button "Neue Umfrage" sits in the title row; no form is shown; the button opens it, `Escape` closes an unchanged form at once and asks for a changed one
| 22 | In the form switch "Art der Umfrage" through the three kinds | exactly one sentence explains the chosen kind; the note about "Keine Änderung" shows only for the decision; "Erklärung hinzufügen" shows only for the consensus kinds; typed explanations survive a switch to "Einzelauswahl" and back but are not sent for a single choice
| 23 | Create a decision with three options, one explanation of 1000 characters (then 1001) | 1000 is accepted, 1001 blocks the start button with a sentence; the counter appears from 800
| 24 | Open the poll as a member | the booth lists "Keine Änderung" (plaque P) first, then the options; the explanation of an option is shown clamped; nothing is preselected; "Prüfen" waits until all options are rated
| 25 | Rate, check, go back, check again, submit -- double-click the last button | the ratings are restored when going back; exactly one answer; "Ihre Antwort ist gespeichert."; no rating remains on the page
| 26 | In the database: `SELECT * FROM poll_response_rating` and `poll_response` | no member column anywhere, no time column; the response row has `option_id` NULL and `weight_ltr` 0; one rating row per option; the ledger has no new entry
| 27 | Try to answer the same poll again, and a classic poll through the consensus booth path (API) | the second answer is a conflict; the wrong kind is `BadRequest` and writes nothing
| 28 | Close the decision with 4 answers, then run another with 5 | 4: only "Unverbindliches Stimmungsbild · 4 Antworten" and the withheld sentence; 5: the result with Ø, Summe, 10er, group conflict, strong objection, the distribution behind its switch
| 29 | Rate so that the passive option ties with an option; then so that two options tie | the passive option leads and the tie sentence is shown; for two options: the lowest maximum decides (sentence shown), or "Gleichstand, kein eindeutiges Ergebnis."
| 30 | Create and close a ranking | "Rangliste nach Widerstand", ranks "1.", "2. gleichauf"; no badge, no bar
| 31 | Phone width 320 px | the scale of the booth breaks into 0-5 and 6-10; nothing scrolls sideways
| 32 | A question, an option and an explanation with `###KvI18nS###` and `<img ...>` | shown as plain text in the form, the booth, the check step, the options list and the result
|===
