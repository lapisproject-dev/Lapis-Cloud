= Auction hints on partner servers
:toc:

WARNING: Status: **implemented (steps c1 to c4), off by default (instance switch, partner switch, seller consent), not legally reviewed, released with 0.57.0 (off). The legal-review gate of wave (c) is NOT lifted.** Whether the organisations are joint controllers in the sense of Art. 26 GDPR is not clarified (same open question as with the carpool exchange). Staging only: see link:auction-federation-staging-test.adoc[the staging test plan] and the link:../features/auction-federation-runbook.adoc[operator runbook].

== Goal

A seller may show an open euro auction to partner servers as a *preview hint*. Nothing but the hint leaves the origin server: no seller, no bidder, no bid, no offer, no payment data, no description and no URL. Bidding, buying and paying happen on the origin server only. A hint is the wire type `AuctionTeaser` (exactly ten fields, pinned by `AuctionTeaserTripwireTest`).

== Wire format

`GET /api/federation/auction-feed/v1` (private, server to server, never under `/api/public` or `/api/embed`). The envelope has `schemaVersion`, `kind`, `complete`, `items`, `withdrawn`. An item has `originHost`, `hops` (always 0 in c2), `maxHops`, `teaser`, `via` (empty in c2) and `originSignature`. A withdrawal is a pseudonymous id and a UTC day. `buyNowMinor = null` is left out of the JSON on purpose.

`complete = false` means: a list was cut (scan limit, item cap, byte cap) or an item could not be signed. A receiver must then not treat a missing hint as withdrawn.

== Pseudonymous id and signature

* `teaserId` = HMAC-SHA256 of the auction id with a key derived from `LAPIS_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEY` and the purpose `lapis-auction-teaser-id/v1` (different from the carpool purpose). Rotating the key changes every id (a partner sees "all withdrawn, all new").
* `originSignature` signs, in this order and LF-separated: purpose `lapis-auction-teaser-origin/v1`, teaser id, origin host, revision, base64url(title), currency, price kind, price, buy-now price or `-`, offers flag, `endsAt`, `priceAsOf`, `maxHops`. `hops` and `via` are not signed. `AuctionOriginSignatures` is the only place that builds or checks the statement.

== Throttle of the shown price

The feed must not reveal the bid history. `AuctionTeaserSnapshots` is the only place that decides over a new revision:

* Without a bid (`START`) a change of the start price goes out at once (it reveals no bid).
* With bids (`CURRENT`) a new price is shown at most every 30 minutes (`PRICE_REFRESH_MINUTES`); the first change from `START` to `CURRENT` is throttled the same way.
* The Sofortkauf price is only sent while it lies above the transferred (throttled) price. A partner can therefore see "Sofortkauf" for up to 30 minutes after the real price reached it; the origin refuses the purchase as it always did.
* A change of the Sofortkauf price, of the offers flag or of the currency changes the content hash and so the revision immediately.
* Revisions rise strictly (`max(prev + 1, minute of the wall clock)`); the update is `WHERE revision = old`, so two parallel builds cannot overwrite each other. After a withdrawal and a new consent the revision jumps above the old one.

The visible price comes from one source, `AuctionFiatPricing.visiblePrice`, shared by the detail view, `acceptOffer` and the feed.

== Consent

The seller (an active member who confirmed the euro notice; `mayShareAuction` is the only place for that rule) calls `setAuctionFederationOptIn`. Rules (`mayConsentToAuctionFederation`): euro only, still open and not over, at least two hours left, a presentable title, instance switch on; plus the module switches and a configured key. At most 20 auctions per seller are shared. A stranger's, an unknown and a malformed auction id get the same `NotFoundException`. Taking the consent back is always allowed. Lock order: auction row, member row (consent only), snapshot/tombstone rows, audit entry last.

Triggers (the hooks are pinned by `AuctionFederationTripwireTest` (j)): consent taken back; Sofortkauf; an accepted price offer; the time-based close (also the lazy one through `getAuction`); EUR to LTR (the consent goes, a switch back does not share again); instance switch off; auction or euro module off (consents stay); one partner switched off (tombstones for that partner only). Consented auctions that ended without any access, or whose seller left, are withdrawn by a bounded lazy sweep (at most 50 per build). The consent ends with the auction (a deviation from "keep the flag": an ended auction can never be shared again, and the sweep stays self-limiting).

A tombstone lives `min(created + 14 days, end + 2 days)` and is not written when that is already over. Consent-driven withdrawals write a tombstone only if some partner fetched the feed at or after the consent (`requireServed`). Known limit: the feed commits its served moment with its body, so a feed that is still open while the consent is withdrawn is not seen; its hint is then not withdrawn explicitly (a complete next answer drops it, an incomplete one keeps it until the auction ends). Without a key the consent is still cleared and the miss is counted. A hint that is back (instance switched on again) gets a fresh revision and its global tombstone goes ("item wins").

A partner that was served before and is no longer approved gets, as long as it is active, not blocked and still has live withdrawals, a trailing answer with the withdrawals only.

== Feed route and gate

Per IP budget before the signature, per partner and global budgets only after approval, four concurrent builds, 1 MiB, 1000 items, 1000 withdrawals, scan limit 5000. Every refusal is the same bytes (`404 {"error":"unavailable"}`), also the same as the carpool refusal. The signature covers the constant path and the own host, never the request's Host header, so a carpool signature or one made for another server is not valid here. `partner_server.auction_*` is written only by `PartnerServerStore`. `AuctionTeaserFeedGate` and the reach list use the one predicate `mayPullAuctionFeed`.

== Landing route

`https://<origin>/app#/auction/hint/<id>`: the id sits in the fragment. `resolveAuctionHint` answers `Available(auctionId)` only for a consented, open, euro auction with the switches on; everything else, also an exhausted per-member budget, is the one `Unavailable` answer. It writes nothing and does not run the lazy settlement. A visitor who is not signed in has the validated id remembered in `sessionStorage` (once, 30 minutes) and returns after the login; `safeReturnTo` is unchanged. The page shows the normal card on top of the auction screen (no detail page) and never shows the id.

== Audit

Consent on and off: `AUCTION` entries `AUCTION_FEDERATION_CONSENT_ON/OFF` (event, currency, status, time; never an amount, title or id). System withdrawals write no extra entry (the settlement or the switch is audited already). Switches: `PARTNER_SERVER`. Feed `200`: `AUCTION_FEDERATION_FEED` with counts only.

== What c2 deliberately did not do (kept for the record)

No pull, no relay, no received hints, no list of partner auctions for members, no hide or block: these came with c3 (next section). No tombstones at restore, GDPR export or erasure, member exit (c4). No release: the legal-review gate of wave (c) stands.

== Receiving side (c3)

No schema change: V169 already holds `auction_received_hint`, `auction_received_tombstone` (kind `WITHDRAWN` or `HIDDEN`), `partner_server.auction_pull_*` and the relay columns of `auction_teaser_tombstone`. **Off by default, not legally reviewed, the legal-review gate is not lifted.**

=== Pull

`AuctionPullPoller` (a copy of the carpool poller, built and started only in `Application.kt`) pulls every direct partner that `mayPullAuctionFeed` allows: 30 minutes plus up to 10 minutes of jitter after a success, back-off from 15 minutes doubling to 24 hours after a failure (a `429` waits at least an hour, from 8 failures the pull counts as paused). At most 2 pulls run in parallel, 20 partners are due per tick, 5000 items and withdrawals per tick. The request is made by `AuctionTeaserFeedClient`, the only file of the federation package that opens a connection: https only, the host is resolved once and pinned, no redirect, 1 MiB, 10 seconds, signed with the own federation key over the auction path (a carpool signature is not valid here). A pull happens only while the instance switch AND the auction module are on; every tick, whatever the switches say, also sweeps expired hints and tombstones (our own tombstones were never deleted before c3), stale sources and the consent sweep of the origin side.

=== Rules of a received hint

The reader checks the key sets before it decodes anything: a root, item or teaser key this server does not know (a seller name, an IBAN, a bid count, a URL) rejects the item as `UNKNOWN_FIELD` and the field is never stored. Then, in a fixed order: format (canonical hosts, path consistent with the hop count, the feed host is the last relay), signature present, blocked, hop limit, content rules. The content rules (`AuctionTeaserValidator`, also applied by the feed builder before it signs, so what we would reject we never publish) in a fixed order: title (no control, bidi or zero-width characters, no link, NFC, at most 80 code points), currency (LTR is refused by name and by the supported list), amount, expired, end (a whole minute, at most 90 days ahead), price date (not in the future beyond 5 minutes, not after the end, a whole minute).

Unlike the carpool reader the auction reader applies its own hop limit already on receipt: with limit 1 only hints of direct origins are taken (wire hops 0), with limit 2 also relayed ones (wire hops 1). The store checks the limit again inside the transaction (it may have changed meanwhile).

=== Verification and revisions

The store verifies the origin signature against the key pinned for the origin (a direct partner's pin or the pin of the partner recursion); no key means `UNKNOWN_ORIGIN_KEY` and no network call. A key pin that changed between the request and the apply voids the answer (`applied = false`, nothing written); a pending key holds both exchanges (the pin is shared). Per (origin host, teaser id): a lower revision is `REPLAY`; the same revision with other content is `TAMPER` (the fields are compared, not only the signature); a higher revision replaces content and signature whatever the source. The path (hops, via, source) changes only when the source is the same or the new path is shorter, or when the old path would violate a lowered hop limit of the origin; a shorter path of a foreign source needs room in its own cap.

=== Caps, no displacement

At most 1000 rows per source and 5000 in total, with the carpool fair share (`max(200, 5000 / sharing partners)`, never above 1000). A hint that does not fit is rejected as `CAPACITY`; no row of another source is ever deleted for it. Local blocks of reported withdrawals: 5000 per reporting source, 20000 in total; pass-on tombstones: 20000 in total, 5000 per reporter.

=== Withdrawal, HIDDEN, relay

A withdrawal reported by the source (or by the origin itself) removes the copy and writes a `WITHDRAWN` block until the end plus 2 days. Only the reporter, or the origin itself at hop 0, may bring the hint back; then the block and the pass-on tombstone are deleted. A copy that could have travelled (`hops < maxHops`) is reported as withdrawn to every partner as a pass-on tombstone, but only if a partner fetched our feed since we got it. An administrator can hide one hint (`hidePartnerAuctionHint`): the copy goes and a `HIDDEN` block is written until the end plus 2 days. `HIDDEN` is final: not even the origin brings it back, a later withdrawal never weakens it (one row per origin and id, only upgraded from `WITHDRAWN` to `HIDDEN`), a newer signed revision with a later end prolongs it. A hidden hint is not handed on and nothing is reported to a partner or the origin (the copy only disappears from our own feed).

The relay works only with hop limit 2: stored hops 1 with `maxHops` 2 is re-published with `hops = 1` and the own host appended to `via`, signed as the origin signed it (the teaser is rebuilt from the stored columns and verified against the stored signature in a test). It is never handed to its source, its origin or a relay on its path, never when the source stopped sharing, the origin is blocked or any live block exists. An own live tombstone beats a relay copy with the same id. Lowering the limit 2 to 1 simply ends the relay (no explicit withdrawals are written; the receiving partners drop what a complete answer no longer lists).

=== Retention and staleness

A hint is shown only while its end lies in the future (server filter and client minute tick); the row is deleted by the next pull tick. A source whose last successful pull is older than 24 hours loses its copies (the forwardable ones are reported as withdrawn). Blocks and tombstones are deleted when they expire.

=== Visibility

`listPartnerAuctionHints` is for an ACTIVE member (guest, friend and donor are refused; the gate runs before the page check) and returns an empty page while the instance switch or the auction module is off. The Euro module of the receiver is not needed to look. `getAuctionFederationAvailability.partnerHintsVisible` tells the client whether to ask. The page is sorted by end, id, host and shows exactly the DTO fields (no signature, path or source). A partner can offer fake auctions under its own name; the levers are the per-partner switch, the administrator's hiding and the caps.

=== Audit

Hiding writes one `PARTNER_SERVER` entry `AUCTION_RECEIVED_HINT_HIDDEN` with the host and the pseudonymous id, never the title or an amount. Reading and pulling write no entry (counters in memory only).

=== Known limits

* Offset paging: rows can shift between two requests.
* A partner can offer fake auctions under its own name.
* Lowering the hop limit does not write explicit withdrawals.
* Hidden hints are not un-hidden in this step (a hidden hint comes back only after its block ran out, which is after the auction ended).

== Decisions of 2026-11-24

. Euro auctions only; an LTR auction is never shared (consent, builder filter, sweep and the receiving validator all refuse it).
. The shown price of a running auction (`CURRENT`) is refreshed at most once per 30 minutes (one revision per 30 minutes).
. Hop limit at most 2, default 1.
. A hint shows the badge "Preisvorschlag möglich" when price offers are possible.
. Only active members of the receiving server see hints (guests, friends and donors do not).
. The local administrator can hide a hint on his own server only; nothing is told to the origin.
. Showing needs both switches: the instance switch and the auction module.
. Everything is off by default.

== GDPR (c4)

* *Export.* `AuctionPersonalData.exportMember` lists, for an auction the member SOLD, `sharedWithPartnerServers` and `sharedWithPartnerServersSince`. The winner's export (`auctionsWon`) does not show the seller's consent.
* *Erasure of a seller.* `AuctionWithdrawals.onSellerErased` is the FIRST call of `eraseMember`, before any row of the member changes: the opted-in auctions of the seller are locked (the caller holds the member row lock), a global tombstone is written for every hint that was handed out, and the consent ends (the auction row itself is retained, so the consent must not outlive the erasure). Without the teaser key the consent still ends and the miss is counted.
* *Registry.* `PersonalDataRegistry` describes the four tables; none carries a member id (the pseudonym is a keyed HMAC).
* *Joint controllership* between the organisations (Art. 26 GDPR) is **not clarified**; the operator runbook says so.

== Backup and restore (c4)

* The snapshot, the received hints and the received tombstones are *not* in a backup; `auction_teaser_tombstone` is.
* While the rows are written, `neutralizeFederationRow` switches the exchange off and clears every consent and every stale consent moment in the same transaction as the row, so no partner pull can see a restored consent.
* The ids and ends of the auctions that carried a consent in the backup, that are still open (status `OPEN`, not LTR, end in the future), are collected (at most 10,000). After `resetAfterRestore`, `AuctionFederationStore.withdrawAfterRestore` writes ONE global tombstone for each, in its own transaction. This runs on success and also after a failed restore (better too many withdrawals than too few). Without the teaser key nothing is written.
* The tombstone id is derived from (teaser id, scope), so a tombstone that came back with the backup is not duplicated. The audit entry `AUCTION_RESET_BY_RESTORE` carries the number of consents only.
* An ETag that came back with the backup is handled by the poller (a `304` after a restore forgets the ETag).

== Member exit (c4)

The consent sweep (`AuctionWithdrawals.sweep`, run by the feed build and by the poller tick) finds consented auctions whose seller is no longer an active member (status outside the member set, or anonymized). Since c4 the filter on the seller runs in the database (a join), before the limit, so more than `limit` auctions of active sellers can no longer hide the auction of a seller who left.

== Lock order

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Step | Rows
| 1 | the partner row (feed route, `setPartnerAuctionSharing`)
| 2 | the member row (`forMemberUpdate`, erasure)
| 3 | auction rows, ascending id
| 4 | tombstones and snapshots
| 5 | the audit entry, always last
|===

A restore needs no auction lock: it runs exclusively against an empty target.

== Known limits (c4)

* The feed commits its served moment together with its body; a feed transaction that is still open while a consent is withdrawn is not seen by `requireServed`.
* The restore withdraws at most 10,000 hints; a larger set is cut and logged as truncated.
* The carpool restore writes the same global tombstones for the shared, still open offers of the backup (`CarpoolFederationStore.withdrawAfterRestore`).
* A partner with an incomplete feed keeps a copy until the end of the auction or 24 hours without refresh.
* Joint controllership (Art. 26 GDPR), the distance-selling rules and the platform liability for foreign hints are not clarified.

== What this step does not do

No new capability: no new field on the wire, no schema change (V169 unchanged), no new route, no message between servers, no LTR hints, no bidding over the federation, no images, no route from a member to the origin to report a hint. No release: the legal-review gate of wave (c) stands.
