= Auction hints on partner servers (origin side)
:toc:

WARNING: This describes step **c2 "origin side"** of wave (c) of link:auction-fiat-and-federation.adoc[Auctions in euros]. The feed is **off by default** (instance switch, partner switch, seller consent) and **has not been legally reviewed**. Receiving hints from partners (c3) and restore, GDPR export/erasure and member exit (c4) are **not implemented yet**.

== Goal

A seller may show an open euro auction to partner servers as a *preview hint*. Nothing but the hint leaves the origin server: no seller, no bidder, no bid, no offer, no payment data, no description and no URL. Bidding, buying and paying happen on the origin server only. A hint is the wire type `AuctionTeaser` (exactly ten fields, pinned by `AuctionTeaserTripwireTest`).

== Wire format

`GET /api/federation/auction-feed/v1` (private, server to server, never under `/api/public` or `/api/embed`). The envelope has `schemaVersion`, `kind`, `complete`, `items`, `withdrawn`. An item has `originHost`, `hops` (always 0 in c2), `maxHops`, `teaser`, `via` (empty in c2) and `originSignature`. A withdrawal is a pseudonymous id and a UTC day. `buyNowMinor = null` is left out of the JSON on purpose.

`complete = false` means: a list was cut (scan limit, item cap, byte cap) or an item could not be signed. A receiver must then not treat a missing hint as withdrawn.

== Pseudonymous id and signature

* `teaserId` = HMAC-SHA256 of the auction id with a key derived from `LAPIS_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEY` and the purpose `lapis-auction-teaser-id/v1` (different from the carpool purpose). Rotating the key changes every id (a partner sees "all withdrawn, all new").
* `originSignature` signs, in this order and LF-separated: purpose `lapis-auction-teaser-origin/v1`, teaser id, origin host, revision, base64url(title), currency, price kind, price, buy-now price or `-`, offers flag, `endsAt`, `priceAsOf`, `maxHops`. `hops` and `via` are not signed. `AuctionOriginSignatures` is the only place that builds or checks the statement.

== Throttle of the shown price

The feed must not reveal the bid history. `AuctionTeaserSnapshots` is the only place that decides over a new revision:

* Without a bid (`START`) a change of the start price goes out at once (it reveals no bid).
* With bids (`CURRENT`) a new price is shown at most every 30 minutes (`PRICE_REFRESH_MINUTES`); the first change from `START` to `CURRENT` is throttled the same way.
* The Sofortkauf price is only sent while it lies above the transferred (throttled) price. A partner can therefore see "Sofortkauf" for up to 30 minutes after the real price reached it; the origin refuses the purchase as it always did.
* A change of the Sofortkauf price, of the offers flag or of the currency changes the content hash and so the revision immediately.
* Revisions rise strictly (`max(prev + 1, minute of the wall clock)`); the update is `WHERE revision = old`, so two parallel builds cannot overwrite each other. After a withdrawal and a new consent the revision jumps above the old one.

The visible price comes from one source, `AuctionFiatPricing.visiblePrice`, shared by the detail view, `acceptOffer` and the feed.

== Consent

The seller (an active member who confirmed the euro notice; `mayShareAuction` is the only place for that rule) calls `setAuctionFederationOptIn`. Rules (`mayConsentToAuctionFederation`): euro only, still open and not over, at least two hours left, a presentable title, instance switch on; plus the module switches and a configured key. At most 20 auctions per seller are shared. A stranger's, an unknown and a malformed auction id get the same `NotFoundException`. Taking the consent back is always allowed. Lock order: auction row, member row (consent only), snapshot/tombstone rows, audit entry last.

== Withdrawal

----
stateDiagram(name = "Shared hint") {
    val shared = state(name = "SHARED")
    val revised = state(name = "REVISED")

    val expired = state(name = "EXPIRED")
    transition(from = shared, to = revised, label = "price or buy-now change")
    transition(from = revised, to = revised, label = "next change")
    transition(from = shared, to = withdrawn, label = "consent back, sale, end, switch off")
    transition(from = revised, to = withdrawn, label = "consent back, sale, end, switch off")
    transition(from = withdrawn, to = shared, label = "new consent (higher revision)")
    transition(from = withdrawn, to = expired, label = "tombstone life over")
}
----

Triggers (the hooks are pinned by `AuctionFederationTripwireTest` (j)): consent taken back; Sofortkauf; an accepted price offer; the time-based close (also the lazy one through `getAuction`); EUR to LTR (the consent goes, a switch back does not share again); instance switch off; auction or euro module off (consents stay); one partner switched off (tombstones for that partner only). Consented auctions that ended without any access, or whose seller left, are withdrawn by a bounded lazy sweep (at most 50 per build). The consent ends with the auction (a deviation from "keep the flag": an ended auction can never be shared again, and the sweep stays self-limiting).

A tombstone lives `min(created + 14 days, end + 2 days)` and is not written when that is already over. Consent-driven withdrawals write a tombstone only if some partner fetched the feed at or after the consent (`requireServed`). Known limit: the feed commits its served moment with its body, so a feed that is still open while the consent is withdrawn is not seen; its hint is then not withdrawn explicitly (a complete next answer drops it, an incomplete one keeps it until the auction ends). Without a key the consent is still cleared and the miss is counted. A hint that is back (instance switched on again) gets a fresh revision and its global tombstone goes ("item wins").

A partner that was served before and is no longer approved gets, as long as it is active, not blocked and still has live withdrawals, a trailing answer with the withdrawals only.

== Feed route and gate

Per IP budget before the signature, per partner and global budgets only after approval, four concurrent builds, 1 MiB, 1000 items, 1000 withdrawals, scan limit 5000. Every refusal is the same bytes (`404 {"error":"unavailable"}`), also the same as the carpool refusal. The signature covers the constant path and the own host, never the request's Host header, so a carpool signature or one made for another server is not valid here. `partner_server.auction_*` is written only by `PartnerServerStore`. `AuctionTeaserFeedGate` and the reach list use the one predicate `mayPullAuctionFeed`.

== Landing route

`https://<origin>/app#/auction/hint/<id>`: the id sits in the fragment. `resolveAuctionHint` answers `Available(auctionId)` only for a consented, open, euro auction with the switches on; everything else, also an exhausted per-member budget, is the one `Unavailable` answer. It writes nothing and does not run the lazy settlement. A visitor who is not signed in has the validated id remembered in `sessionStorage` (once, 30 minutes) and returns after the login; `safeReturnTo` is unchanged. The page shows the normal card on top of the auction screen (no detail page) and never shows the id.

== Audit

Consent on and off: `AUCTION` entries `AUCTION_FEDERATION_CONSENT_ON/OFF` (event, currency, status, time; never an amount, title or id). System withdrawals write no extra entry (the settlement or the switch is audited already). Switches: `PARTNER_SERVER`. Feed `200`: `AUCTION_FEDERATION_FEED` with counts only.

== What c2 deliberately does not do

No pull, no relay, no received hints, no list of partner auctions for members, no hide or block (c3). No tombstones at restore, GDPR export or erasure, member exit (c4). No release: the legal-review gate of wave (c) stands.
