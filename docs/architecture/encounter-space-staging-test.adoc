= Encounter space: staging test script (V1.9.61, wave B1)
:toc:

This script is *written, not executed*: wave B1 was built without a reachable LiveKit instance. It verifies on `staging` what the unit and
scenario tests can only assume (see `docs/architecture/encounter-space.adoc`, "Assumptions about LiveKit"). Run it before wave B2 builds a
client on top, and record the results below. Checks 18-28 (V1.9.62) cover the client of wave B2 and are written, not run either.

== Setup

. Deploy the version with `V73` to staging (run `./gradlew :lapis-server:flywayRepair` first -- the baseline's audit CHECK was widened in place, see the
  operator note at the top of `V73__encounter_space.sql`; take a backup).
. As BOARD: create a space (`MEMBERS_AND_GUESTS`), give one member the office `PULPIT` and one the office `STEWARD`.
. Two devices, two browsers each: Chrome and Safari. Devices: one for the pulpit, one for a congregation member (plain `ACTIVE`), a third
  login for a `GUEST`/`FRIEND`.

== Checks

[cols="1,3,2", options="header"]
|===
| # | Check | Expected
| 1 | The congregation member enters and *tries to publish* (camera/microphone) with the token from `enterSpace` | LiveKit refuses the publication; the member can subscribe to the pulpit. Note the exact client-side symptom for B2.
| 2 | The congregation member edits the token's `video.canPublish` to `true` and reconnects | the signature check fails, the connection is refused.
| 3 | The congregation member calls `IConferenceService.joinRoom` / `IConferenceBreakoutService.rejoinMainRoomToken` with the session room id | `ForbiddenException`; no token is returned.
| 4 | The pulpit publishes | audio/video reach the congregation.
| 5 | A steward calls `removeFromSpace` for a connected congregation member, then the member tries to reconnect with the *old* token | the old token still works directly against LiveKit for up to the poller period (60 s) and the poller disconnects the person again; `enterSpace` answers `ForbiddenException`.
| 6 | `silenceInSpace`, then the member reconnects | the new token has `canPublishData = false`; the data channel is refused.
| 7 | A `GUEST` enters: consent is required once, the token is valid for 15 minutes; wait 16 minutes and let the client reconnect | after expiry the client needs `enterSpace` again (no consent prompt the second time).
| 8 | The pulpit leaves, the room stays empty for 31 minutes with the session open | LiveKit removes the room (`empty_timeout` 1800, and `departure_timeout` 1800 for a room somebody had joined -- the server sets both in `CreateRoom`); on the next poller run the session is closed with reason `EMPTY`; `audit_log_entry` gets `{"state":"CLOSED","reason":"EMPTY"}` with no actor.
| 9 | Connect a client with an old token to the deleted room (`auto_create`) | LiveKit may recreate the room; the poller closes/deletes it again (check `ListRooms`).
| 10 | Close the space with 3 people present | no `conference_participation` row remains for the session (`SELECT count(*) ... WHERE room_id = <session>` is 0), the LiveKit room is gone.
| 11 | Kill the server process while people are present, restart | after the second poller run (about 60 s after start; the first run only records who is absent, the two-sweep rule) the orphaned rows of the open session whose clients are gone are deleted; rows of ended sessions are deleted immediately at start.
| 12 | Read the LiveKit and server logs on `pdv2` during checks 1 to 10 | LiveKit logs participant identities (member UUIDs) at INFO -- note it, decide on log rotation/level (open ops task); the *server* logs no identity, name or room id.
| 13 | Start an external stream (`startStream`) as the steward with `SINGLE_PARTICIPANT` on the pulpit | only the pulpit's picture is streamed; the same with `GRID` is refused.
| 14 | Two devices call `openSpace` at the same moment | exactly one session; `ListRooms` shows no leftover room of the loser.
| 15 | The pulpit joins briefly (audio test), disconnects, and a congregation member enters 25 minutes later | the room is still there (`departure_timeout` 1800 was honoured by LiveKit; with the default 20 s it would be gone and the session closed as `EMPTY`); the member gets a token. Confirm `CreateRoom` accepts `departure_timeout` and `ListRooms` reports it.
| 16 | A congregation member's network drops for ~10 s and `livekit-client` reconnects with the old token | the presence row survives the poller (two absent sweeps are needed); `listPresent` still answers the person.
| 17 | The BOARD withdraws the pulpit's office while the pulpit is connected | the pulpit is disconnected; a re-entry via `enterSpace` yields a listen-only token (`canPublish = false`). Also reconnect directly to LiveKit with the old token: this still succeeds with publish rights until `tokenTtlMinutes` expires (known limitation, see Assumptions about LiveKit).
| 18 | B2 (Chrome and Safari): a congregation member enters, never touching the page before | no permission prompt for camera or microphone; the pulpit is heard only after "Ton einschalten" if the browser blocked autoplay (the band appears), then without the band.
| 19 | B2: the pulpit person clicks "Eintreten und Kanzel übernehmen" | the camera starts in that click (one permission prompt), the microphone is off, the band "Ihr Mikrofon ist aus." is shown; "Einschalten" switches it on.
| 20 | B2: two devices -- the congregation member raises a hand and sends an amen | the symbol appears at the member's seat on the other device (hand stays until lowered or 90 s without renewal, amen about 3 s); an office holder sees the hand in the list "Erhobene Hände"; no number appears anywhere.
| 21 | B2: a steward silences the member | the member's hand, amen and chat are disabled with "Sie wurden von einem Ordner stummgeschaltet." (after the member re-enters, `canPublishData = false`); reading continues.
| 22 | B2: a steward removes a congregation member, and tries the same against the pulpit person and against a BOARD member without an office | the member leaves and cannot re-enter this session; the menu is absent for the pulpit and for another steward; for the BOARD member the server refuses and the page shows "Diese Person können Sie hier nicht entfernen.".
| 23 | B2: the pulpit person (or a steward) clicks "Türen schließen" and confirms | everybody present lands on the entry/closed view with "Der Gottesdienst ist beendet."; the notice of the room is shown while closed.
| 24 | B2: a moderator starts the transmission (a non-listed test RTMP destination, if available) | only the pulpit's picture is sent (`SINGLE_PARTICIPANT`); there is no layout choice and no YouTube destination is created; everybody present sees "Live (nur Kanzel)" within 30 s; stop removes it.
| 25 | B2: open `#/begegnung/<id>` through the mobile bridge (`encounter/spaces/<id>/webview-session`) | the room opens inside the WebView with the session cookie; camera/microphone permissions are requested only for an office holder.
| 26 | B2: switch the scene off, reload, then enable the operating system's high-contrast mode | the choice survives the reload (one key in browser storage, nothing else); in high-contrast mode the scene is hidden by itself.
| 27 | B2: VoiceOver (Safari) or TalkBack | the pulpit region reads "Kanzel: <name>"; seats read the person's name (and "Hand erhoben"); the pews are a list "Gemeinde"; no single-letter shortcut fires; the side sheet traps the focus and Escape closes it.
| 28 | B2: leave the page (close the tab) while inside | within the poller period the presence row is gone (`pagehide` is best effort); `listPresent` no longer names the person.
|===
