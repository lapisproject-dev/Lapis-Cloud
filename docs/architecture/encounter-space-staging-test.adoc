= Encounter space: staging test script (V1.9.61, wave B1)
:toc:

This script is *written, not executed*: wave B1 was built without a reachable LiveKit instance. It verifies on `staging` what the unit and
scenario tests can only assume (see `docs/architecture/encounter-space.adoc`, "Assumptions about LiveKit"). Run it before wave B2 builds a
client on top, and record the results below. Checks 18-28 (V1.9.62) cover the client of wave B2 and are written, not run either.

== Setup

. Deploy the version with `V73` to staging (run `./gradlew :lapis-server:flywayRepair` first -- the baseline's audit CHECK was widened in place, see the
  operator note at the top of `V73__encounter_space.sql`; take a backup).
. As BOARD: create a space (`MEMBERS_AND_GUESTS`), give one member the office `PULPIT` and one the office `STEWARD`.
. Two devices, two browsers each: Chrome and Safari. Devices: one for the pulpit, one for a congregation member (plain `ACTIVE`), a third
  login for a `GUEST`/`FRIEND`.

== Checks

[cols="1,3,2", options="header"]
|===
| # | Check | Expected
| 1 | The congregation member enters and *tries to publish* (camera/microphone) with the token from `enterSpace` | LiveKit refuses the publication; the member can subscribe to the pulpit. Note the exact client-side symptom for B2.
| 2 | The congregation member edits the token's `video.canPublish` to `true` and reconnects | the signature check fails, the connection is refused.
| 3 | The congregation member calls `IConferenceService.joinRoom` / `IConferenceBreakoutService.rejoinMainRoomToken` with the session room id | `ForbiddenException`; no token is returned.
| 4 | The pulpit publishes | audio/video reach the congregation.
| 5 | A steward calls `removeFromSpace` for a connected congregation member, then the member tries to reconnect with the *old* token | the old token still works directly against LiveKit for up to the poller period (60 s) and the poller disconnects the person again; `enterSpace` answers `ForbiddenException`.
| 6 | `silenceInSpace`, then the member reconnects | the new token has `canPublishData = false`; the data channel is refused.
| 7 | A `GUEST` enters: consent is required once, the token is valid for 15 minutes; wait 16 minutes and let the client reconnect | after expiry the client needs `enterSpace` again (no consent prompt the second time).
| 8 | The pulpit leaves, the room stays empty for 31 minutes with the session open | LiveKit removes the room (`empty_timeout` 1800, and `departure_timeout` 1800 for a room somebody had joined -- the server sets both in `CreateRoom`); on the next poller run the session is closed with reason `EMPTY`; `audit_log_entry` gets `{"state":"CLOSED","reason":"EMPTY"}` with no actor.
| 9 | Connect a client with an old token to the deleted room (`auto_create`) | LiveKit may recreate the room; the poller closes/deletes it again (check `ListRooms`).
| 10 | Close the space with 3 people present | no `conference_participation` row remains for the session (`SELECT count(*) ... WHERE room_id = <session>` is 0), the LiveKit room is gone.
| 11 | Kill the server process while people are present, restart | after the second poller run (about 60 s after start; the first run only records who is absent, the two-sweep rule) the orphaned rows of the open session whose clients are gone are deleted; rows of ended sessions are deleted immediately at start.
| 12 | Read the LiveKit and server logs on `pdv2` during checks 1 to 10 | LiveKit logs participant identities (member UUIDs) at INFO -- note it, decide on log rotation/level (open ops task); the *server* logs no identity, name or room id.
| 13 | Start an external stream (`startStream`) as the steward with `SINGLE_PARTICIPANT` on the pulpit | only the pulpit's picture is streamed; the same with `GRID` is refused.
| 14 | Two devices call `openSpace` at the same moment | exactly one session; `ListRooms` shows no leftover room of the loser.
| 15 | The pulpit joins briefly (audio test), disconnects, and a congregation member enters 25 minutes later | the room is still there (`departure_timeout` 1800 was honoured by LiveKit; with the default 20 s it would be gone and the session closed as `EMPTY`); the member gets a token. Confirm `CreateRoom` accepts `departure_timeout` and `ListRooms` reports it.
| 16 | A congregation member's network drops for ~10 s and `livekit-client` reconnects with the old token | the presence row survives the poller (two absent sweeps are needed); `listPresent` still answers the person.
| 17 | The BOARD withdraws the pulpit's office while the pulpit is connected | the pulpit is disconnected; a re-entry via `enterSpace` yields a listen-only token (`canPublish = false`). Also reconnect directly to LiveKit with the old token: this still succeeds with publish rights until `tokenTtlMinutes` expires (known limitation, see Assumptions about LiveKit).
| 18 | B2 (Chrome and Safari): a congregation member enters, never touching the page before | no permission prompt for camera or microphone; the pulpit is heard only after "Ton einschalten" if the browser blocked autoplay (the band appears), then without the band.
| 19 | B2: the pulpit person clicks "Eintreten und Kanzel übernehmen" | the camera starts in that click (one permission prompt), the microphone is off (red outline, slashed symbol on the left of the bar) and a screen reader announces "Ihr Mikrofon ist aus." once; the microphone button switches it on (no band, no second announcement).
| 20 | B2: two devices -- the congregation member raises a hand and sends an amen | the symbol appears at the member's seat on the other device (hand stays until lowered or 90 s without renewal, amen about 3 s); an office holder sees the hand in the list "Erhobene Hände"; no number appears anywhere.
| 21 | B2: a steward silences the member | the member's hand, amen and chat are disabled with "Sie wurden von einem Ordner stummgeschaltet." (after the member re-enters, `canPublishData = false`); reading continues.
| 22 | B2: a steward removes a congregation member, and tries the same against the pulpit person and against a BOARD member without an office | the member leaves and cannot re-enter this session; the menu is absent for the pulpit and for another steward; for the BOARD member the server refuses and the page shows "Diese Person können Sie hier nicht entfernen.".
| 23 | B2: the pulpit person (or a steward) clicks "Türen schließen" and confirms | everybody present lands on the entry/closed view with "Der Gottesdienst ist beendet."; the notice of the room is shown while closed.
| 24 | B2: a moderator starts the transmission (a non-listed test RTMP destination, if available) | only the pulpit's picture is sent (`SINGLE_PARTICIPANT`); there is no layout choice and no YouTube destination is created; everybody present sees "Live (nur Kanzel)" within 30 s; stop removes it.
| 25 | B2: open `#/begegnung/<id>` through the mobile bridge (`encounter/spaces/<id>/webview-session`) | the room opens inside the WebView with the session cookie; camera/microphone permissions are requested only for an office holder.
| 26 | B2: switch the scene off, reload, then enable the operating system's high-contrast mode | the choice survives the reload (one key in browser storage, nothing else); in high-contrast mode the scene is hidden by itself.
| 27 | B2: VoiceOver (Safari) or TalkBack | the pulpit region reads "Kanzel: <name>"; seats read the person's name (and "Hand erhoben"); the pews are a list "Gemeinde"; no single-letter shortcut fires; the side sheet traps the focus and Escape closes it.
| 28 | B2: leave the page (close the tab) while inside | within the poller period the presence row is gone (`pagehide` is best effort); `listPresent` no longer names the person.
| 29 | V1.9.67: create a room of the type "Versammlung" (assembly) with the default reactions | the bar offers "Hand heben" and "Applaus" (no "Amen"); the room speaks of "Podium", "Moderation" and "Teilnehmende" everywhere (entry panel, role editor, list, people tab); the scene is the podium floor plan.
| 30 | V1.9.67: while the assembly is open, try to change its type or its reactions (also with a second tab that was loaded before the deploy) | the form shows the fields disabled with a hint; the server answers a real change with a conflict; changing only the title works; an old tab that sends no profile leaves the room unchanged.
| 31 | V1.9.67: a guest who consented for a church service enters the assembly | the guest sees the assembly text ("Sie nehmen an einer Versammlung teil.") and must consent again; the church room still needs no second consent.
| 32 | V1.9.67: stage mode on a desktop, a tablet width (768-1199 px) and a phone | the podium is large (16:9), the bar sits below the stage; the side panel is a column from 1200 px, an overlay below (Escape closes it, focus returns to the button), a sheet on the phone; no horizontal scrolling at 360 px; bar targets at least 44 px.
| 33 | V1.9.67: "Vollbild" in Chrome, Firefox, Safari (desktop) and on an iPhone | desktop: real full screen, "Vollbild beenden" leaves it; iPhone Safari and the mobile WebView: the CSS fallback fills the screen, Escape or the button leaves it.
|===

== Temporary floor script (V1.9.138)

Written, not run. Needs three devices (steward, congregation member A, congregation member B) and shell access to LiveKit
(`lk room participants list`).

[cols="1,3,2", options="header"]
|===
| # | Check | Expected
| F1 | The steward raises member A's hand into the list (A raises a hand) and clicks "Wort erteilen" | A's band shows "Sie haben das Wort · noch 02:00" with "Mikrofon einschalten" and "Wort zurückgeben"; B sees "Gerade spricht jemand aus der Gemeinde" without a name; the steward's list shows "<Name> spricht". `lk room participants list` shows A with `canPublish = true` and sources limited to the microphone.
| F2 | A clicks "Mikrofon einschalten" and speaks | the browser asks for the microphone once (in that click); B and the steward hear A; the list says "Mikrofon an".
| F3 | Wait for the 120 s to run out | the band goes, the sound stops; `lk room participants list` shows A with `canPublish = false`. **Check whether LiveKit v1.13.5 removes A's track itself or only mutes it** (note it in the result log): the room must stay clean either way, and a muted leftover is gone from the participant list within 90 s.
| F4 | Grant again, extend twice by "+2 Min" and once by "+1 Min", then try once more | the total stops at 10 minutes; the extension buttons are disabled and "Höchstdauer 10 Min erreicht" appears.
| F5 | While A speaks, the steward clicks "Wort entziehen" | the sound stops at once; A's band is gone; permission as in F3.
| F6 | While A speaks, restart the server | the first poller run after the start disconnects A (the state is empty); A can re-enter listen-only.
| F7 | In A's browser console try to switch on the camera (SDK) | LiveKit refuses (sources limited to the microphone).
| F8 | In B's browser console publish a data packet on `lapis-encounter-floor` with `{"s":"YOU","t":600}` | A and the steward see nothing (a packet with a sender is dropped); B gets no right to publish.
| F9 | While A speaks, drop A's network for 10 s and let `livekit-client` reconnect with the old token | within 15 s the right is raised again (`canPublish = true`); if the reconnect is a full rejoin, A has to click "Mikrofon einschalten" again.
| F10 | While A has the floor, A sits down at a table / the steward removes A / silences A / A leaves | the floor ends in every case, A's permission goes back, nobody keeps a microphone.
| F11 | The steward tries to grant to a member who sits at a table, to the pulpit person, and to a silenced member | refused, the button is disabled in the list; B's and the steward's views stay unchanged.
|===

== Pulpit recording script (V1.9.143)

Written, not run. Needs four devices (steward, pulpit person, congregation member A, congregation member B), shell access to LiveKit and the egress service
(`LAPIS_RECORDING_ENABLED=true`, a writable egress volume) and an ADMIN login.

[cols="1,3,2", options="header"]
|===
| # | Check | Expected
| R1 | As ADMIN switch on "Aufzeichnung von Kanzel bzw. Podium erlauben" | the notice dialog opens, "Einschalten" is locked until the box is ticked, Esc cancels without a call; after switching on the audit log shows the version and the hash.
| R2 | As BOARD allow the option in a closed room; try the same in an open room | closed: allowed. open: refused ("Nur bei geschlossenem Raum einschaltbar").
| R3 | A guest (FRIEND) opens the entry panel of that room | the panel does not promise "no recording"; it names the pulpit and shows the operator's room notice; the guest must consent to the recording variant (new version/hash), even if they consented before.
| R4 | The steward starts the recording; the pulpit person has not agreed yet | the toggle shows "Warte auf Zustimmung der Kanzel"; the pulpit person gets a dialog with the focus on "Nicht aufzeichnen"; nothing is recorded (`ls` the egress volume: no `pulpit/` file).
| R5 | The pulpit person agrees | the band "Aufzeichnung läuft · Nur die Kanzel wird aufgezeichnet" appears for A, B, the steward and the pulpit person, also in the full screen and with the scene off; a screen reader says "Aufzeichnung gestartet".
| R6 | A joins late (after the start) | A sees the band within 15 s (point-to-point packet).
| R7 | Play back the finished file later | it contains the pulpit person's picture and voice **only**; neither A's nor B's nor the steward's.
| R8 | The steward grants the floor to A while the recording runs, with the pulpit person's loudspeaker on | the recording stops at once ("FLOOR_GRANTED" in the audit log, band off); A's voice is **not** in the file. Starting again while A speaks is refused. **Echo test:** repeat with the loudspeaker loud -- the file of the first part must not contain A.
| R9 | Two pulpit persons and a third | exactly two files, the third is reported ("Höchstzahl gleichzeitiger Aufnahmen erreicht").
| R10 | Withdraw the pulpit office of the recorded person via "Ämter" | their file stops within seconds, the band goes off, audit `SOURCE_NOT_PULPIT`.
| R11 | Stop by hand | the file closes, the band goes off, the document appears in "Aufzeichnungen Begegnungsraum" (level `BOARD_ONLY`, title without a name, file name with a UUID); `ls` shows the raw file gone.
| R12 | Delete the recording in the list under the room administration; then try to find the bytes | the document, the versions and the marker are gone, the blob is removed from the store. The dialog warned about older backups.
| R13 | Delete a second recording with the ordinary "delete document" of the store | the same hard delete.
| R14 | Restart the server **while a recording runs** | within one clock run the egress is stopped (audit `RESTART_ORPHAN`), the band goes off in the open room, the partial file stays on the volume (warning with a count, nothing deleted); starting again works.
| R15 | Check the rights of the egress volume for the `pulpit/` subdirectory (the ELB ACL trap) | the egress service can create `pulpit/<space>/` (otherwise the start fails with "Aufzeichnungsdienst nicht verfügbar").
| R16 | Does the encounter poller disconnect the egress participant? (`lk room participants list` during a recording) | the recorder stays connected (it publishes nothing). **If it is removed, the recording ends after a minute: report it.**
| R17 | Switch the instance switch off during a recording | the recording stops at once, the band goes off, audit `INSTANCE_DISABLED`.
|===

exist for it: `scripts/loadtest` and xref:encounter-load-test.adoc[]. Written, not run. Run it after checks 29 to 33 and the floor script passed on the same staging instance.
