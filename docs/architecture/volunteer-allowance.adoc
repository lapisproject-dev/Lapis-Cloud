= Volunteer/Instructor Allowance (§3 Nr. 26 / 26a EStG) (V1.4.12)

NOTE: This document's `[kuml]` block is rendered and validated by the `:docs-render:renderDocs`
Gradle task (see `docs-render/`), kept in sync by hand with
`lapis-server/src/main/kuml/46-volunteer-allowance.kuml.kts` (the actual, load-bearing model).

A board/functionary requests a tax-privileged payment for a member's volunteer activity, in one of
two mutually exclusive categories: `INSTRUCTOR` (§3 Nr. 26 EStG Übungsleiterpauschale, an
independent annual cap) or `HONORARY` (§3 Nr. 26a EStG Ehrenamtspauschale, its own independent
annual cap). The category is fixed once a payment is created -- no dropdown, `updateDraft` will
not accept a different one. This wave is the second "money out" bridge in this codebase (after
`TravelExpensePostingBridge`), and closes the "no integration with the Übungsleiter-/
Ehrenamtspauschale" scope boundary that wave's own README section named as a deliberate gap --
deliberately as its own, separate bridge, not a shared abstraction with travel expenses (the two
domains' booking shapes and legal constraints differ enough that forcing them together would cost
more clarity than it would save).

== Data shape

Two new tables -- `volunteer_allowance_payment` (one row per requested payment) and
`volunteer_allowance_self_declaration` (one row per member/category/calendar-year self-declaration
of the receiving person) -- plus one new column on `organization_settings`
(`volunteer_allowance_account_id`). See
`lapis-server/src/main/kuml/46-volunteer-allowance.kuml.kts` for the full kUML model and
`V30__volunteer_allowance.sql` for the migration.

[source,kuml,name="volunteer-allowance"]
----
classDiagram(name = "Volunteer/Instructor Allowance — ERM excerpt") {

== State machine

[cols="1,1,1,2"]
|===
| From | To | Method | Condition

| —
| DRAFT
| `createDraft`
| Subject active, < 10 open payments

| DRAFT
| DRAFT
| `updateDraft`
| Subject or requester; category unchangeable

| DRAFT
| REQUESTED
| `submitPayment`
| Formal validation only -- the annual cap is NOT checked here

| DRAFT/REQUESTED
| WITHDRAWN
| `withdrawPayment`
| Subject or requester

| REQUESTED
| REJECTED
| `decidePayment(false)`
| BOARD/ADMIN, neither subject nor requester, note mandatory

| REQUESTED
| EXECUTED
| `decidePayment(true)`
| + self-declaration exists + cap acknowledgment if required + booking succeeded

| REQUESTED
| APPROVED
| `decidePayment(true)`
| + declaration missing, or booking failed -> `executionError`

| APPROVED
| EXECUTED
| `retryPosting`
| Booking succeeded

| APPROVED
| APPROVED
| `retryPosting`
| Failed again, `executionError` updated

| APPROVED
| REJECTED
| `decidePayment(false)`
| Only way out of a permanently unbookable payment ("no dead end")
|===

Four-eyes over both paths, both booking-producing methods (`decidePayment` AND `retryPosting`):
`VolunteerAllowanceService` rejects the decider/retryer if they are **either** the payment's
subject **or** its requester -- the same fix `TravelExpenseService` already applies to both of its
own booking-producing methods (the V1.4.11 security finding: `retryPosting` is a SECOND
booking-producing method and must be guarded identically, not just `decidePayment`).

== The annual §3 Nr. 26/26a EStG cap

`VolunteerAllowanceCalculator` is pure, DB-free arithmetic: given a category, a requested amount,
and the prior EXECUTED total for that member/category/calendar-year, it splits the requested
amount into a tax-free portion and an exceeding (taxable) portion, using `BigDecimal.compareTo`
throughout -- never `equals`, which would treat `BigDecimal("960")` and `BigDecimal("960.00")` as
different values. **The cap is checked at decision time, not at `submitPayment` time** (a second
payment for the same subject/category/year may have been booked in between), and re-checked again
inside `VolunteerAllowanceExecution` immediately before the actual booking, comparing the freshly
recomputed prior total against the snapshot `decidePayment` froze
(`allowance_total_changed_since_decision` if they no longer match) -- the same "decide, then
re-verify under the same lock at execute time" idiom `TravelExpenseExecution` establishes.

Only `EXECUTED` payments count toward the annual total (`COUNTS_TOWARD_CAP`), mirroring
`priorPostedDonationTotalThisYear`'s own `POSTED`-only filter -- a `REQUESTED`/`APPROVED` payment
has not yet actually paid out and must not shrink another payment's headroom. **The calendar-year
key is `payment_date.year` (Zuflussprinzip), not the decision/booking date** -- the one deliberate
difference from the donation-total precedent, which uses `journal_entry.entry_date`: a payment
made on 31 December but booked in January must still consume the OLD year's cap.

When the under-lock recheck yields `EXCEEDS_CAP`, `decidePayment` requires a
`capAcknowledgment` that echoes the CURRENT `VolunteerAllowanceCapDisclaimer.VERSION`/`SHA256`
back unchanged (structurally identical to `AuctionComplianceDisclaimer`) -- and REJECTS an
acknowledgment sent when the payment does **not** exceed the cap, so a client cannot neutralize the
gate by always sending one pre-emptively. Unlike the organization-wide, once-per-org disclaimer
tables this codebase already has, this acknowledgment is **per payment** and lives directly on the
`volunteer_allowance_payment` row (`cap_disclaimer_version`/`cap_disclaimer_sha256`/
`cap_acknowledged_by`/`cap_acknowledged_at`), written atomically with the decision so
`chk_vap_exceeding_needs_ack` can guarantee the invariant DB-side without a cross-table race.

The §3 Nr. 26/26a EStG cap applies **per person per calendar year across ALL organizations** --
Lapis Cloud only ever knows the sum booked in the current organization, so
`VolunteerAllowanceYearStatusDto.remainingInThisOrganization` is a maximum, never a guarantee. The
UI must never label it "Restbetrag" for this reason (enforced by a string assertion in
`VolunteerAllowanceLabelsTest`).

== Self-declaration ("Raskin's Gate")

Every payment requires a self-declaration for `(subjectMemberId, category, paymentDate.year)`
before it can ever be booked -- missing one leaves the payment `APPROVED` with
`executionError = self_declaration_missing`, a retryable resting state, not a thrown exception (the
board's decision was legitimate; the declaration can be supplied afterwards). This is a
**completely separate object from the board's cap-acknowledgment disclaimer**: the board
disclaimer is the board confirming something about itself/its own booking decision; the
self-declaration is the RECEIVING PERSON confirming something about themselves. The class
deliberately does not carry the word "Disclaimer" and does not live alongside
`VolunteerAllowanceCapDisclaimer`.

Two sources: `IN_APP` (the member declares for themselves in Lapis -- `recordedBy` is server-set to
the caller, never accepted from the client, closing the exact self-service forgery scenario a
naive "recordedBy from input" design would open) and `ON_PAPER` (BOARD/ADMIN records a signed
paper declaration on behalf of someone else -- `recordedBy` must differ from the declaring member
and `signedOn` is mandatory). `uq_vasd_member_category_year` enforces at most one declaration per
triple; a declaration for year X does not cover year X+1.

== Booking (`VolunteerAllowancePostingBridge`)

[cols="1,1"]
|===
| Side | Account

| Debit
| `organization_settings.volunteer_allowance_account_id` (must be `EXPENSE`-typed)

| Credit
| `organization_settings.payment_bank_account_id`
|===

Sphere is hardcoded to `IDEELLER_BEREICH`, same as `TravelExpensePostingBridge`. **Exactly two
postings, always** -- unlike travel expenses (one debit per line), the tax-free/taxable split is
NOT booked as two separate ledger lines: this codebase has no payroll/Lohnsteuer concept, and
splitting the debit would misleadingly imply one portion is already payroll-processed. The split
survives structurally only in `journal_entry.description` (which spells out `frei <freeAmount> /
steuerpflichtig <exceedingAmount>`) and in the payment row's own snapshot columns -- a deliberate
scope boundary: actually withholding and remitting Lohnsteuer for the exceeding portion remains the
organization's own responsibility, exactly as `VolunteerAllowanceCapDisclaimer.TEXT` states to the
board at acknowledgment time.

**Degrades instead of failing** (`Failed(code)`, WARN log, no journal entry, no audit entry, the
APPROVED decision is preserved) for: `volunteer_allowance_account_not_configured`,
`payment_bank_account_not_configured`, `ledger_account_inactive`,
`volunteer_allowance_account_not_expense_type`, `cash_register_balance_insufficient`,
`cash_voucher_required` -- the same posture `TravelExpensePostingBridge` established. A
`JournalEntryBalance.validateBalanced` failure is **not** a degrading case and throws, rolling back
the whole transaction.

Idempotency: `posted_journal_entry_id` plus the plain (non-partial) unique index
`uq_vap_posted_journal_entry` guarantee at most one booking per payment.

== DSGVO erasure

The discriminator is **"was this payment ever actually booked"** (`status == EXECUTED`), not "was
it ever submitted" -- the same lesson `TravelExpensePersonalData` documents from its own prior
"DSGVO-Überaufbewahrung" finding. Every non-`EXECUTED` status is fully deleted regardless of how far
it progressed (a `DRAFT`, `REQUESTED`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, or `WITHDRAWN` payment has no
Buchungsbeleg -- `chk_vap_posted_entry_state` guarantees `posted_journal_entry_id IS NULL` for all
of them). An `EXECUTED` payment is redacted, not deleted: `activity_description` becomes
`"[gelöscht]"`; `decision_note` is RETAINED, not nulled (`chk_vap_decided_needs_note` requires it
non-null for every EXECUTED row, and it is the board's own text about its decision, not the
subject's own free text). Amount/date/category/status/every snapshot/the booking reference remain
too (Art. 5(2) DSGVO Rechenschaftspflicht + §147 Abs. 1 Nr. 4 AO, 10 years).
Only the SUBJECT role triggers deletion/redaction -- `requestedBy`/`decidedBy`/
`capAcknowledgedBy` are counted in the export but leave the row untouched.

A self-declaration row is deleted unless an `EXECUTED` payment of the same
member/category/calendar-year still exists, in which case it remains as that payment's
(freitext-free) legal-basis evidence. No periodic redaction sweep -- unlike the V1.4.10 wave's Art.
9 free-text reason, there is no special-category free text anywhere in this domain, and §147 AO's
10-year retention applies regardless of any later erasure request.

== What doesn't work yet (this wave)

See the README's own "Volunteer/instructor allowance" section for the full list of deliberate scope
boundaries (no configurable/jahresbezogene Deckel, no automated Lohnsteuer withholding for the
exceeding portion, no cross-organization cap awareness, no payout/payment run, single EXPENSE
account for both categories).
