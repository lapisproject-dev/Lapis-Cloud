= Staging test plan: collapsed create forms (V1.9.40)
:toc:

== Purpose

The wave is covered by DOM tests (`CollapsibleCreateFormDomTest`, `CollapsibleCreateFormScreensDomTest`) and a tripwire. What only a real browser on a
real deployment shows: the focus handling with a screen reader, the scroll on a phone, and the behaviour with real data. The plan was **not
executed** by the author of the wave: nothing was deployed (the staging server belongs to the operator), so every row of "Actual results" is still
open. It is a plan to be run by a person, not a script.

== Set-up

. Staging with this version deployed; a BOARD account and a MEMBER account (no committee role).
. A phone, or the browser's device mode at 360 px width; optionally a screen reader (VoiceOver, NVDA).

| 7 | Press "Abbrechen", then "Verwerfen" | the form closes; opening it again shows an empty form
| 8 | Fill the form and save | success message; the form folds back; the new committee is in the list; the focus is on the button
| 9 | Open the form, type something, press "Aktualisieren" in the list | the list reloads, the open form and its text stay
| 10 | Repeat 1 to 8 on "Vorstand" ("Neue Bestellung"), "Sitzungen" ("Neue Sitzung") and "Anträge" ("Neuer Antrag") | same behaviour; on "Vorstand" the section title "Manuelle Eintragung" stays above the place where the form opens

| 12 | At 360 px width open a form | no horizontal scrolling; the form is scrolled into view

| 14 | Switch the language to English and open a form, then cancel after typing | button, dialog and empty-state texts are translated

== Finance group (V1.9.45) -- additional steps, not executed

The finance screens were converted afterwards; the same plan applies with these accounts and screens. The steps below were **not executed**
(nothing was deployed by the author of the wave); every result is open.

. Accounts: TREASURER and BOARD. Data: at least two active ledger accounts, one cost center, one external donor, one submitted SEPA batch.
. As TREASURER open each of "Kostenstellen" ("Neue Kostenstelle"), "Spender" ("Neuer Spender"), "Offene Posten" ("Neuer offener Posten"), "Kontoauszüge"
  ("Kontoauszug hochladen", upload icon), "SEPA-Mandate" ("Neues Mandat"), "Bankkonten" ("Neues Bankkonto", opens the modal): the form is
  collapsed, the button is in the title row, and steps 2 to 8 of the table above behave the same (Escape with and without typing, "Abbrechen",
  save folds back and reloads, the focus returns to the button).
. "Kontenplan & Journal": both buttons are in the title row, "Neue Buchung" left of "Neues Konto". Open "Neue Buchung", press "Buchungszeile hinzufügen",
  type an amount into line 3, press `Escape`: the discard dialog appears. Press "Weiter bearbeiten": the amount is still there.
. Journal: add a posting line without typing anything and press `Escape`: the dialog appears as well (an added line counts as a change; this is intended).
. Journal: select a draft, press "Als neuen Entwurf duplizieren": the form opens pre-filled; `Escape` closes without a question. Change a field, press
  "Als neuen Entwurf duplizieren" on another draft: the dialog asks before the form is overwritten.
. Journal: "Direkt buchen" still asks for confirmation; a double click on "Endgültig buchen" books exactly once.
. "SEPA-Lastschrift": both buttons ("Neuer Lastschriftlauf", "Neue Rücklastschrift") in the title row. Open "Neue Rücklastschrift", wait until the batch and the
  item are preselected, press `Escape`: no dialog. Type a fee first, then `Escape`: the dialog appears.
. "Offene Posten": open "Neuer offener Posten", type a counterparty, press "Abbrechen": the dialog appears (before this wave the input vanished silently).
. "Kontoauszüge": the empty state names the button and does not open the form by itself; pick a file, press `Escape`: the dialog appears.
. At 375 px width the two buttons of the journal and of the SEPA page wrap without horizontal scrolling.
. As BOARD open all of the above: no button and no empty gap in the title row.
. Switch the language to English: buttons, empty-state sentences and the dialog are translated.
