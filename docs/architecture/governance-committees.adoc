= Committees: the General Assembly (V1.9.101), the automatic end of seats (V1.9.102) and election succession (V1.9.103)
:toc:

== Scope

The committee screens ("Gremien", "Sitzungen", "Anträge") treated the General Assembly (`CommitteeType.GENERAL_ASSEMBLY`, "Mitgliederversammlung")
like any other committee: every screen loaded the whole member directory (`IMemberService.listMembers()`) to offer people in a picker, and the
roster let a board member add single members to a group that, by definition, already contains every active member. V1.9.101 ("Welle A") removes both
for the assembly. It changes **no** right, **no** quorum rule and **no** vote rule, and adds **no** migration (the schema stays at V82).

== The assembly is automatic, with one predicate

For a `GENERAL_ASSEMBLY` committee the eligible set is "every member with `MemberStatus.ACTIVE`", defined once, in `generalAssemblyCondition()`
(`CommitteeEligibility.kt`, `internal` since V1.9.101, never copied). It is the single source for

* N, the number shown to the user (`IGovernanceService.countGeneralAssemblyEligible`);
* the quorum (`computeQuorum`, `checkQuorum`), the right to cast a ballot (`castVoteBallot`) and the voter snapshot of an election
  (`ElectionService.openVoting`), through `eligibleMemberIds` and `isCommitteeEligible`;
* the recipients of "invite all active members" (PDF and postal, see below).

`countGeneralAssemblyEligible(committeeId)` is one `COUNT` over that predicate. Any authenticated member may ask: the directory itself was already
visible to every member through `listMembers()`, and a single number reveals less. A committee of another type is answered with 400, an unknown
id with 404. There is no cache.

== Offices of the assembly

The roster of the assembly ("Besetzung") shows the fact "all active members (automatic)" with the current N and the sentence that N is the basis of
quorum, votes and elections. Below it:

* **"Ämter der Versammlung"**: the committee rows whose role is **not** `MEMBER` (chair, deputy chair, secretary, ...). BOARD/ADMIN add one with the
  person search (never the directory) through the unchanged `addCommitteeMember`; the role list offers every role except `MEMBER`. "Ausgeschiedene
  anzeigen" belongs to this block.
* **"Frühere Einzeleinträge (n)"**: the rows with role `MEMBER`. They are kept, collapsed (`<details>`, closed) and read only (a manager can still end
  the term, there is no delete function and none was added). They have no effect on attendance, voting right or quorum; the sentence says so.

[IMPORTANT]
====
An office row is a real committee row and **does** carry rights, exactly as before: `hasCommitteeRole` makes its holder able to record meetings
(`canRecordForMeeting`: chair, deputy chair, secretary), and the committee roles feed polls and the conference-room binding. Only plain `MEMBER`
rows are inert for the assembly. Adding an office is still BOARD/ADMIN only.
====

== The person search

`IGovernanceService.searchCommitteeCandidates(committeeId, query, page)` replaces `listMembers()` in the pickers of chair, minute-taker, agenda
presenter, attendance (person and representative) and committee office. It is built so that it cannot be used to harvest the directory:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Rule | Detail
| Right first | `canRecordForMeeting(committeeId)` (BOARD/ADMIN or chair/deputy chair/secretary of THAT committee), checked before any read of member data; an unauthorized caller gets 403 whether or not the committee exists
| Rate limit | 60 requests per minute and acting member, in memory (`FederationInboxRateLimiter`, process wide: the service is built per call, a per-instance limiter would never count). Checked AFTER the right, so a refused caller spends no budget. The answer is the typed `SearchRateLimitedException` (Kilua RPC carries only the type and has no HTTP 429)
| Minimum | at least 2 and at most 64 characters after trimming; `page` 0..9; 20 hits per page; so at most 200 hits per query
| No total | the page carries `hasMore` only, never a count
| Minimal fields | `MemberPickDto(id, displayName)` -- no e-mail, no address, no number
| Candidates | ACTIVE and not anonymized, for every committee type (as `listMembers()` was); `committeeId` only decides the right
| Matching | case-insensitive "contains" on the display name; `%` and `_` match literally (`containsLikePattern`, the single shared helper)
| Logging | the search text and the names are never logged
|===

The client picker (`RemoteMemberPicker`) waits 300 ms after the last keystroke, sends nothing below 2 characters, discards answers that arrive after a
newer query, and offers "Weitere anzeigen" for the next page (never endless scrolling, nothing beyond page 9: "Bitte Suche verfeinern."). It is an
ARIA combobox (`role=combobox`, `aria-activedescendant`, a polite live region) and shows names only as plain text.

The pickers outside the assembly (election board, the maintenance form of other committees, board membership screen, ...) still use `listMembers()`;
they are named in the changelog as follow-up work, not silently dropped.

== Invitation to the assembly

For the assembly the invitation form offers "An alle aktiven Mitglieder (N)" (preselected) or "Ausgewählte Mitglieder" (searched, shown as removable
chips, at most 1,000).

* **PDF** (free): `POST /api/mailmerge/invitations` with `audience=ALL_ACTIVE`, `committeeId` and `part` (0-based). The server resolves the
  recipients itself in one transaction and one query, sorted by id, 1,000 per part (`K = max(1, ceil(N / 1000))`, one download button per part;
  a `part` beyond K is a 400). `audience` and `recipientMemberId` exclude each other; without `audience` (or `SELECTED`) the request is the old one,
  byte for byte, and `committeeId`/`part` are refused. Every PDF of the "all" request starts with a cover sheet ("Versandübersicht": part k of K, as-of
  time, number of letters); server PDFs are fixed German (like "Termin:" and "Der Vorstand"), so the cover sheet is not translated.
* **Post** (paid, Letterxpress): `PostalInvitationDispatchInput(audience = ALL_ACTIVE, committeeId)`. The cap of 50 letters is checked on a single
  `COUNT` before anything is loaded, PDF-rendered, logged or sent; the gate `postalMailEnabled` and the all-addresses-complete rule are unchanged
  (one incomplete address rejects the whole dispatch with 409, nothing is sent). The client offers the button only while N is known and at most 50,
  and confirms with the exact number (no names: they are not loaded).
* **No e-mail** is ever sent for an invitation, so the hourly mail budget and the outbox of V1.9.81 are not involved.
* Anonymized records are never given a letter. They are still `ACTIVE` and therefore still count in N and the quorum (a separate question, left
  unchanged on purpose); the cover sheet names the number of letters actually produced.

== Automatic end of committee seats on a status change (V1.9.102, "Welle B")

When a member's status changes to `WITHDRAWN`, `REJECTED`, `DECEASED` or `DONOR` (`MemberStatusSets.COMMITTEE_SEAT_ENDING`), every open committee seat
of that member ends automatically, in the same transaction as the status change. Before V1.9.102 this happened for the first three only (the set
`MEMBERSHIP_ENDED`), through `endAllOpenCommitteeMembershipsForMember`, which wrote `until` but left no trace of why.

*Trigger sets.* `COMMITTEE_SEAT_ENDING` is a strict superset of `MEMBERSHIP_ENDED`. `DONOR` is in the first but deliberately not in the second:
`MEMBERSHIP_ENDED` also revokes SEPA mandates (`SepaBatchPoller`), and a donor keeps a running mandate. A donor is not a member, so a seat (which
presupposes membership) cannot continue -- that is a product decision beyond "leaving". `COMMITTEE_SEAT_KEEPING` (`ACTIVE`, `APPLICATION`,
`FRIEND`, `GUEST`) is the complement; a test pins that every status is in exactly one of the two sets. `rejectApplication` falls back to `FRIEND` for
a former friend; the seats end there too, with the reason `REJECTED` (the hook takes an explicit `reason` that is separate from the target status).

*The hook.* `CommitteeSeatLifecycle.endSeatsForStatusChange` is called from exactly three places (pinned by `CommitteeSeatLifecycleTripwireTest`):
`MemberRoleStatusMutations.applyStatusChangeLocked` (admin UI, four-eyes execution, operator console), `RegistrationService.leaveMembership` and
`RegistrationService.rejectApplication`. It runs after every permission check (transition rules, admin-peer guard, self-change ban, death-date
plausibility), so none of those is weakened. Every seat of the member with `until IS NULL OR until > stichtag` gets `until = stichtag` and
`auto_end_reason = reason` (migration V83). `stichtag` is the date of the status change in the organisation time zone, also for a death some days
ago (the date of death stays on the member row and is not copied anywhere). Seats that ended on or before the stichtag are never touched, so the hook
is idempotent. A seat that has not started yet (`since > stichtag`) gets the same `until`, which makes its interval empty: the person is eligible on
no day, and the row is kept for the record. An EXECUTIVE_BOARD seat also closes the open `BoardMembership` (`cascadeExecutiveBoardEnd`, shared with
the manual end) and creates the Transparenzregister reminder as before.

[kuml, committee-seat-auto-end, svg]
----
sequenceDiagram(name = "Automatic end of committee seats (V1.9.102)") {
    val caller = participant(name = "Status change (admin UI, exit, rejection)")
    val checks = participant(name = "Permission and transition checks")
    val hook = participant(name = "CommitteeSeatLifecycle phase 1")
    val others = participant(name = "Mandates, photo/bio revokes, officer grant")
    val audit = participant(name = "Audit log (phase 2)")

    message(from = caller, to = checks, text = "peer guard, self-change ban, transition rules, death date")
    message(from = checks, to = hook, text = "newStatus in COMMITTEE_SEAT_ENDING: end seats (until = stichtag, auto_end_reason), board cascade, no audit yet")
    message(from = hook, to = others, text = "same transaction, remaining row-locking writes")
    message(from = others, to = audit, text = "recordAudits(): one COMMITTEE_MEMBERSHIP entry per seat (triggering human as actor)")
    message(from = audit, to = audit, text = "then the MEMBER entry of the status change (if the path writes one)")
}
----

*Two phases, because of the audit lock.* `AuditLogRecorder.record` locks the single hash-chain row and must be the last row-locking write of a
transaction. Phase 1 therefore writes seat and board rows but no audit and returns a `CommitteeSeatEndResult`; the caller does its other writes and
then calls `recordAudits()` (phase 2), directly before its own `MEMBER` audit entry. The entries carry the triggering human as actor (the deciding BOARD/ADMIN, or the leaving member; null only for the operator console), `entity_type =
COMMITTEE_MEMBERSHIP`, and snapshots with ids, enums and dates only (`CommitteeMembershipAuditSnapshot`): no name, no mail address, no date of
death. The same actor is recorded on the cascaded `BOARD_MEMBERSHIP` end entry (section 20 GwG), because `rejectApplication` and `leaveMembership` write no `MEMBER` entry. Where one exists, the `MEMBER` entry of the same change links by `occurred_at` and member id. A manual end of a seat
(`endCommitteeMembership`) resets `auto_end_reason` to null.

*Failure policy.* A database error rolls the whole status change back: "withdrawn but still chair" must not exist. A retry is safe. An executive
board left without a CHAIR is a business result, not an error: the status change succeeds, the result lists the board, it is logged (ids only) and
shown in the notice band.

*Notice band.* `IGovernanceService.getCommitteeAutoEndNotice` (BOARD/ADMIN only) returns (1) seats ended automatically in the last 30 days
(at most 50, newest first), (2) active executive boards without an eligible CHAIR, computed on every call and evaluated for the following day (see
the inclusive `until` below), and (3) a read-only list (at most 50) of still-open seats of persons whose current status is in
`COMMITTEE_SEAT_ENDING` -- legacy data from before this wave. There is deliberately **no backfill**: changing old rows silently would be a
retroactive change; the board cleans them up by hand. The reason (`autoEndReason`) is sent only to BOARD/ADMIN callers of `listCommitteeMembers`
(`DECEASED` and `DONOR` are personal data); it is visible on a roster row only with "show ended". It is in the data export of the person concerned.

*Tripwire.* `CommitteeSeatLifecycleTripwireTest` classifies every file that writes `member.status` (hook called or "inserts new members only"),
pins the three hook call sites and their `recordAudits()`, allowlists the files that may write `committee_membership.until`/`auto_end_reason`
(`GovernanceService`, `CommitteeSeatLifecycle`, `ElectionService` for the re-election of a winner, `StagingSeedGovernance`) and forbids raw SQL on
the table.

== Election succession: from an election result to an office (V1.9.103, "Welle C")

Before V1.9.103 the tally seated the winners and touched nobody else. Now an election states what happens to the previous holders of the role it
fills, and the tally carries that out in the same transaction.

*Data model (migration V84, purely additive, no backfill).* `election.incumbent_policy` (`REPLACE` = "Neuwahl": the previous holders of the target role
end with the tally; `KEEP` = "Nachwahl": they stay) is `NULL` for YES_NO elections and for **every election opened before this wave**; such an
election keeps the old tally path unchanged, including its refusal of a winner who is no longer active. The policy, `target_role` and
`target_committee_id` are immutable after opening (a tripwire pins that no `ElectionTable.update` writes them). `committee_membership` gains the
provenance columns `started_by_election_id` / `ended_by_election_id` (real foreign keys; a seat has at most one end cause, the CHECK forbids
`auto_end_reason` together with `ended_by_election_id`, and `ended_by_election_id` needs `until`). The new append-only table `election_seat_outcome`
(one row per person and outcome: `SEATED`, `CONTINUED`, `ENDED`, `SKIPPED`, `PREDECESSOR_KEPT`, with a reason for the last two) is the succession log;
its UNIQUE key `(election_id, member_id, outcome)` makes a second succession of the same election a programming error that rolls the transaction back.

*Opening an election (`openElection`).* A personnel election (SINGLE_CHOICE/MULTI_CHOICE) needs a role and a policy (no silent `MEMBER` default for new
elections); a YES_NO election must have neither. A role with exactly one holder (`SINGLE_HOLDER_COMMITTEE_ROLES`) needs `seatCount = 1` and `REPLACE`:
`BoardMembershipEvents.recordBoardJoin` ends a displaced single holder anyway, so `KEEP` would make `committee_membership` and `BoardMembership`
disagree. The general assembly has no `MEMBER` seats to fill (V1.9.101). An election may only seat its motion's own committee, unless the motion belongs
to the general assembly -- otherwise a small working group could vote the executive board out.

*The tally* (`ElectionSeatSuccession.apply`, called only from `tallyElectionLocked`):

[kuml, election-seat-succession, svg]
----
sequenceDiagram(name = "Election succession at the tally (V1.9.103)") {
    val board = participant(name = "Tally (election board)")
    val core = participant(name = "tallyElectionLocked")
    val succession = participant(name = "ElectionSeatSuccession")
    val seats = participant(name = "committee_membership, BoardMembership, election_seat_outcome")
    val audit = participant(name = "Audit log (phase 2)")

    message(from = board, to = core, text = "tally(): motion lock, election lock, outcome computed")
    message(from = core, to = succession, text = "policy set and a result exists: apply(winners, stichtag)")
    message(from = succession, to = seats, text = "lock winners (member rows, by id), lock open seats (by id), skip inactive winners")
    message(from = succession, to = seats, text = "REPLACE: end previous holders first (until = stichtag, ended_by_election_id, board cascade)")
    message(from = succession, to = seats, text = "seat the winners (CONTINUED, role change, SEATED), write the succession log")
    message(from = core, to = audit, text = "recordAudits(): COMMITTEE_MEMBERSHIP, BOARD_MEMBERSHIP, then the RESOLUTION entry")
}
----

. The stichtag is the day of the tally in the organisation time zone. `until` stays inclusive: on the stichtag a predecessor and a winner both count,
  from the next day only the winner (documented, not corrected; `until = stichtag - 1` would be retroactive).
. Winner rows are locked first (`forMemberUpdate()`, ordered by id), then the open seat rows of the committee (`FOR UPDATE`, ordered by id). A winner
  whose status is outside `ORGANIZATION_MEMBER`, or who already holds a seat that starts after the stichtag, is `SKIPPED`; the tally never fails for that
  reason (it does for an election without a policy, as before).
. `REPLACE` without a skipped winner ends every previous holder of the role who is not a winner and whose seat has started (`ENDED`); this happens
  **before** the winners are seated, so the `BoardMembership` of an executive board is closed by the cascade (with its audit entry and its LEFT
  reminder) and not silently by `recordBoardJoin`. A previous holder whose seat starts later is never touched (`PREDECESSOR_KEPT`, `SEAT_STARTS_LATER`).
  With at least one skipped winner nobody is ended (`PREDECESSOR_KEPT`, `WINNER_SKIPPED`): the committee may temporarily have too many seats, never none.
. A winner who already holds the role is `CONTINUED` (no new row, no seat audit, `BoardMembership` untouched). A winner who holds another seat in the
  committee changes role: the old seat ends (`ended_by_election_id`), the new one starts, the executive board gets one JOINED and no LEFT reminder.
. No result, no succession: a tie, a single-choice result without the required majority and a YES_NO election seat nobody and write no log row.
. `AccountRole` and `member.role` are never written; a seat grants committee rights only.

*Audit.* One `COMMITTEE_MEMBERSHIP` `CREATE` per started seat and one `UPDATE` per ended seat (trigger `ELECTION_SEATED`, `ELECTION_SUCCESSION` or
`ELECTION_ROLE_CHANGE`; ids, enums and dates only, the tallying actor), written in phase 2 before the `RESOLUTION` entry. Every audit call comes
after the updates of the motion and the election (the deadlock contract of `AuditLogRecorder`).

*Reading.* `ElectionResultDto` carries `incumbentPolicy`, `seatOutcomes` and `pendingAdminReview`. `SKIPPED` and `PREDECESSOR_KEPT` rows (member
status, reasons) are dropped on the server before mapping unless the caller is the election board, may manage the election or is BOARD/ADMIN; everybody
else gets the flag `pendingAdminReview` instead (and can infer only that something is under review). The names of seated, continued and ended holders
are exactly as visible as the committee roster, which every signed-in member may read. `CommitteeAutoEndNoticeDto.electionSeatChanges` lists seats
started and ended by an election in the last 30 days (a role change is not reported as an ended seat). Ending such a seat by hand resets
`ended_by_election_id`; that is also the way to handle a person who declines the election.

*Tests.* `ElectionSeatSuccessionTest` (+ Postgres lane), `ElectionSeatSuccessionConcurrencyTest` (+ Postgres lane), `ElectionSeatSuccessionServiceTest`,
`ElectionSeatSuccessionMigrationTest` (+ Postgres lane), the extended `CommitteeSeatLifecycleTripwireTest` and
`ServerElectionResultDisclosureTripwireTest`, and three DOM tests.

== Rule-based committees and the frozen electorate (V1.9.112, "Welle D")

Eligibility (`CommitteeEligibility.kt`) has a third branch: a committee with `membership_mode = RULE` has no seat rows, its members are whoever its stored rule
selects today (`eligibleIdsForRule`, the one evaluator behind the live set, the count and the preview). Whoever decides a right to vote or a quorum asks
`frozenOrLiveElectorate` (the snapshot taken when the decision was opened, otherwise live) and never `eligibleMemberIds` directly; the order of precedence and
the new table are described in link:../features/rule-based-committees.adoc[Rule-based committees].

*Lock order of the wave.* Committee rows in the database's own `ORDER BY id` (the own row plus every referenced one), then the seat rows (by id), the webhook
outbox, and the `COMMITTEE` audit entry as the last locking operation. A member row is never locked by the rule switch; `addCommitteeMember` locks member, then
committee, so the two cannot form a cycle. `openVote` locks the motion first and then the committee row (it freezes the electorate under it); `openElection`
locks the target committee after the motion. `CommitteeSeatLifecycle.endSeatsForRuleMode` is the second (and last) writer of a non-null `auto_end_reason`
(reason `RULE_MODE`); the notice band of V1.9.102 deliberately does not list these ends -- they are a board decision, not a surprise.

== Meeting <-> event link (V1.9.227, "Sitzung in der Videokonferenz", wave A)

A Sitzung ("meeting") may be announced by exactly one Veranstaltung ("event"), and an event may announce exactly one Sitzung (1:1, both optional).
The link is the nullable column `event.meeting_id` (`V123`, unique index `ux_event_meeting_id`, FK `ON DELETE SET NULL`). It has **one writer**: the
package `network.lapis.cloud.server.governance.meetingevent` (`MeetingEventLinkStore`; `MeetingEventLinkWriterTripwireTest` pins that).
Since V1.9.229 an officer also manages that event (see below).

.Who may do what (V1.9.229)
[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Action | Right
| Announce, unlink, align (`IGovernanceService`) | `canRecordForMeeting(committee)`: BOARD/ADMIN, or chair, deputy chair, general secretary, secretary, managing director **of that committee**. Align by an officer: not for a PUBLIC event
| Link an existing event, list the linkable Sitzungen | **BOARD/ADMIN only** (a link would otherwise be a rights key: link a foreign public event, then edit it)
| Read the link on the Sitzung page | every member; an unpublished event shows only the neutral title "Veranstaltung (Entwurf)", no data, no link
| Read the link on the event page | BOARD/ADMIN and the officers of that Sitzung's committee; the member view of events shows nothing of it
| Open, edit, publish, cancel THE event of the officer's own Sitzung (`EventService.getEvent/updateEvent/publishEvent/cancelEvent`) | BOARD/ADMIN, or an officer holding a recording office (seat based, as of today) in the committee of the Sitzung the event is linked to -- within the limits below
| Every other event method (registrations, check-in, refunds, series, rooms, capture, create, delete, ...) | BOARD/ADMIN only, unchanged
|===

*The officer rule (V1.9.229).* `CurrentMember.meetingEventGrant(eventMeetingId, meetingCommitteeId)` is the one decision: BOARD/ADMIN -> `Full`; otherwise an
active recording seat (`activeRecordingRole`, lowest `CommitteeRole` ordinal wins, seat based -- no BOARD/ADMIN branch) in the committee of the Sitzung the event is
linked to -> `Officer`; otherwise `None`. On a write path the ids come from the **locked** rows (`MeetingEventOfficerAccess.lockForOfficer`: unlocked read of
`event.meeting_id`, Sitzung `FOR UPDATE`, event `FOR UPDATE`, `meeting_id` unchanged?, then the seat check), never from the client. Every refusal of that lookup
(unknown id, no link, link changed meanwhile, foreign committee, office ended) is the same `NotFoundException` an unknown id gets.

*What an officer may change* (`MeetingEventOfficerAccess.checkOfficerUpdate`): title, description, start, end, place, the online kind (not `LAPIS_ENCOUNTER`
unless already set) and the external link (unless the board made it public). Visibility must stay `MEMBERS_ONLY`; capacity, fee (compared with `compareTo`,
scale-insensitive), currency, registration deadline, room, summary, cover alt text, the public-link flag and the encounter space are compared with the stored
values and refused with `MeetingEventFieldReservedException`. `publishEvent` needs a DRAFT with `MEMBERS_ONLY`; `cancelEvent` needs a free (`fee == 0`)
`MEMBERS_ONLY` event that is not an instance of a running series (`MeetingEventCancelReservedException` for a paid one). An event that is already `PUBLIC`
(linked by the board) is read-only for an officer: edit, publish, cancel and align end with `MeetingEventVisibilityReservedException`. `getEvent` returns
`EventDto.manageScope` (`FULL` / `MEETING_OFFICER` / `NONE`, default `NONE`); the officer path never loads registrations, guests or the room list.

*Audit.* `MEETING_EVENT_LINK` / `UPDATE` with kind `OFFICER_UPDATED` (with the changed field names -- never a text), `OFFICER_PUBLISHED` or `OFFICER_CANCELLED` (followed
by `UNLINKED`), plus the `officerRole` seat; `ALIGNED` by a non-board officer carries `officerRole` too. BOARD/ADMIN edits of an event write no entry (as before).

*Lock order* (every operation, officer path included): the Sitzung row `FOR UPDATE`, then the event row `FOR UPDATE` (then room / conference, registrations), the audit entry
last. The BOARD/ADMIN path of `cancelEvent` locks the event row only (its link update touches the event only), so the two cannot deadlock. Cancelling a Sitzung (`updateMeetingStatus` -> `CANCELLED`) or an event (`cancelEvent`, `cancelSeriesEvent`) dissolves
the link in the same transaction (`UNLINKED`, reason `MEETING_CANCELLED` / `EVENT_CANCELLED`); `HELD` keeps it. Nothing else is deleted, except an unused Protokoll approval item on the cancelled Sitzung (see _Approval item_).

*Rooms.* The link does not touch any conference room. An online event gets its own room on the first join (`EventConferenceProvisioner`), and an event
room is never bound to a Sitzung (`EventConferenceGuard`, V1.9.113). Which room is "the" room of a Sitzung is decided by the next wave.

*Difference.* `MeetingEventDifferenceCalc` compares start (to the minute), place (trimmed, case-insensitive) and online/offline. "Align" copies the
Sitzung's start (the event keeps its own duration) and place to the event; it is refused for a Sitzung in the past because the event rules reject a start
in the past. The online setting is never aligned. A Sitzung has no end of its own: an announced event is 2 hours long. The description is a fixed sentence
and never contains the agenda, a title or a note of the Sitzung.

*Not covered.* There is no "committee" audience: the announcement is `MEMBERS_ONLY`, visible to every member once published. Aligning does not notify
registered people (neither does `updateEvent`). Events of a running series cannot be linked. Hard-deleting a series occurrence leaves no audit entry
(the row is gone; the FK sets nothing anywhere else).

== Sitzung in the conference room (V1.9.236, "Sitzungsmodus", wave B)

A Sitzung bound to a conference room (`conference_room.meeting_id`) can be run from that room. The code is the package
`network.lapis.cloud.server.governance.session`; `GovernanceService` only delegates. Migration `V127__meeting_conference_session.sql` is additive and repeatable
(V1 and V126 are untouched).

.Stored (all on `meeting`, written only by the package above)
[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Column | Meaning
| `opened_at` | the moment the lead opened the Sitzung; the phase is **derived** (`meetingSessionPhase`): `HELD`/`CANCELLED` -> CLOSED, `PLANNED` + no `opened_at` -> NOT_OPENED, else OPEN
| `quorum_established_at`, `quorum_present_count`, `quorum_required_count`, `quorum_eligible_count` | the quorum as established: all NULL or all set (`chk_meeting_quorum_counts`)
| `quorum_overridden` | established although the calculation said "not met"
| `current_agenda_item_id` | the agenda item being treated. **No foreign key**: `meeting` <-> `agenda_item` would be a reference cycle and the organization backup restores in FK order and refuses cycles (`OrganizationSchemaCatalog.restoreOrder`). `GovernanceService.removeAgendaItem` clears a marker that points at the removed item, and every reader treats a marker that is not an item of the Sitzung as "none"
|===

`attendance.status` gains `GUEST` (the unnamed inline CHECK of V1 is dropped by its PostgreSQL name `attendance_status_check` and by its generated H2 name, deterministic
because V1 is frozen; `AttendanceStatusMigrationScenarios` pins the result on both engines). `audit_log_entry.entity_type` gains `MEETING_SESSION`.

*Access.* `MeetingSessionAccess.resolve` is the one place that turns `(roomId, expectedMeetingId)` into a context. An unknown room, a room without a Sitzung, an
encounter or event room, a Sitzung other than the one the client names (the room was re-bound meanwhile) and a caller without a seat are one and the same
`null` (read) or `NotFoundException` (mutation). Roles use only existing predicates (`canRecordForMeeting`, the minute taker, `canSubmitMotion`); there are no
new rights keys. BOARD/ADMIN without a seat are LEAD, as they are for `recordAttendance`. A PARTICIPANT never mutates (`ForbiddenException`); MINUTES may only set
attendance and adopt.

.Who may call what
[cols="2,1,1,1", options="header"]
|===
| RPC | LEAD | MINUTES | PARTICIPANT
| `getConferenceMeetingState` | roster, live quorum, hint | roster, live quorum | agenda, phase, established line only
| `openConferenceMeeting`, `establishConferenceQuorum`, `moveConferenceAgenda` | yes | Forbidden | Forbidden
| `setConferenceAttendance`, `adoptConnectedAttendance` | yes | yes | Forbidden

*State machine.* Every mutation but `open` needs OPEN; CLOSED is read-only; an ended room is read-only; opening twice is a conflict. `establish` before the
opening is a conflict ("open the meeting first").

*The rule per committee type.* `CommitteeAttendanceRules.forType`: `NameListAttendanceRule` for the executive board, working groups, commissions and others,
`UnsupportedAttendanceRule` for the general assembly. The roster is `frozenOrLiveElectorate(...)` -- the very set `computeQuorum` counts, so what is shown and
what is calculated cannot drift (after the first vote of the Sitzung was opened this is the **frozen** circle). The rule validates: roster statuses only for people
on the roster; `GUEST` only for an active member outside the circle (never a federation guest); `REPRESENTED` needs a stand-in who is on the roster, is not the person
and is not represented themselves; no chains; "open" (status `null`) deletes the entry unless somebody stands in for that person. No refusal carries a name.

*Who is connected.* `CachedConferenceConnectedMembers` asks LiveKit for the participants of the room (10 s cache, at most 256 rooms, a failure is an empty set and is
not cached; the log line carries the exception class only). It is asked **outside** any transaction and **only** for LEAD/MINUTES. Identities that are member ids and on the
roster are "connected"; the other member ids are only counted; identities that are not UUIDs (egress, bots) are ignored.

*Adoption.* `adoptConnectedAttendance` inserts `PRESENT` for roster members who are connected and have no entry, under the Sitzung lock; a unique violation (the old
`recordAttendance` takes no Sitzung lock) is skipped under a savepoint, never thrown. It never overwrites an entry.

*Locks and audit.* Every mutation locks the Sitzung row `FOR UPDATE` first and writes the audit entry last. `MEETING_SESSION` entries (`entityId` = the meeting id)
exist for OPENED, QUORUM_ESTABLISHED, AGENDA_MOVED and ATTENDANCE_ADOPTED; the snapshot (`MeetingSessionAuditSnapshot`) holds ids, enums and counts only. Single attendance
changes are **not** audited: a member id is personal data and the chain is append-only. Reads are limited to 40, mutations to 60 requests a minute and member.

*Not verified.* The behaviour with several live participants, real browsers and phones, a screen reader, and the LiveKit cache against a real LiveKit server.

== Motions and resolutions in the Sitzung panel (V1.9.238, wave C)

Two operations append to `IGovernanceService` (after `moveConferenceAgenda`, `registerService` order unchanged): `attachConferenceMotion(roomId, meetingId, motionId, expectedAgendaItemId)` and
`openConferenceMotionVote(roomId, meetingId, motionId, expectedAgendaItemId, secret, electionBoardMemberIds)`. Code: `MeetingSessionMotionOperations` (writes), `MeetingSessionMotionReads`
(the motion block of `ConferenceMeetingStateDto`), and `ElectionOpening.kt`, which holds the bodies of `ElectionService.openElection`, `appointElectionBoard` and `openVoting` **unchanged**
so the Sitzung can run all three in ONE transaction. **No migration** (all columns exist); V127 is not touched.

*Access.* LEAD **and** `canRecordForMeeting(committee)`, exactly what `scheduleMotion` / `openElection` require. LEAD alone is not enough: the Sitzung chair without a recording office is LEAD
for the panel (so `canManageMotions` is false and the buttons are absent) but gets `ForbiddenException`. A motion that is unknown, of another committee, or of another Sitzung is
the same `NotFoundException` as a stranger's request (no confirmation that it exists).

*Locks.* Sitzung row -> motion -> election and its electorate snapshot -> `conference_room` (secret vote only) -> audit entry last. Only this package and the meeting-event operations lock the Sitzung row
(`MeetingTable ... forUpdate`, checked by search), neither locks a motion afterwards, so no path takes motion -> Sitzung. `MOTION_VOTE_OPENED` is written after the stream-pause entries of
`markPausingForSecretBallot`; both are pure audit steps. The LiveKit stop (`quiesceStreamsForMeeting`) runs after the commit. `GovernanceService` takes the stream guard as an optional constructor parameter; **without
one a secret vote is refused** (fail closed), `Application` passes the real guard (pinned by `MeetingSessionRpcWiringTest`).

*Rules.* The vote is `YES_NO`, `tallyThreshold` 2 (fixed), simple majority; at least 3 and at most 25 distinct board members, all on the roster of the Sitzung, the caller among them unless privileged
(otherwise nobody with the right could close it). Only one decision at a time: opening is refused while an election of the Sitzung is in PREPARATION..CLOSED (a closed one must be counted first), a
meritocratic vote is OPEN or a systemic consensus is in COLLECTION/RATING/CLOSED; `moveConferenceAgenda` is refused while a vote or consensus is **running** (open). The rule is one-sided: `openVote`
on the motion page, `openElection` in the election view and `SystemicConsensusService` do not check against a running Sitzung vote.

*Timing of the quorum (F7).* `computeQuorum` runs at the **count** (`tally`), not when the vote is closed. N comes from the electorate frozen by `openVoting`; the number of present people is the live
attendance at the count. The resolution stores only the boolean `quorum_met`; the numbers are not kept (a later recomputation would use today's attendance), so the resolution card shows the word only.

*Privacy.* A secret vote is never counted for display: `ballotCount` / `eligibleCount` are null for every role, also for an administrator; for an open vote only LEAD/MINUTES get them. Resolution figures
come through `toResolutionDtos`, so below the minimum participation they are withheld (`figuresWithheld`). The audit snapshot holds the Sitzung, motion, election and item ids, the `secret` flag and the
board size, never a name, a title, a text or an answer.

*Known limitations.* A motion cannot be detached from an item. `removeAgendaItem` of an item that holds an attached motion hits the foreign key `motion.agenda_item_id` (no `ON DELETE`) -- as before.
`updateMeetingStatus(HELD)` with an open vote is not prevented; counting still writes the resolution. The "Abstimmen" panel still shows "Stimmen bisher" for a secret vote, and still opens itself for a new
ballot on a wide screen. A chair without a recording office cannot attach or open (see Access). *Not verified:* live operation with several participants, real browsers and phones, and the legal validity of the results.

A new RPC service `IProtocolDraftService` is the LAST `registerService` of `Application.kt` (after `ILupaBbSyncService`; Kilua numbers routes by declaration order, new methods go to its end). Four methods:
`getMeetingProtocolDraft`, `saveMeetingProtocolBlock`, `addMeetingProtocolTask`, `removeMeetingProtocolTask`. Code: `MeetingProtocolDraftOperations` (everything), `MeetingProtocolText` (the sanitiser),
`ProtocolDraftService` (authenticates and delegates). DTOs are named `MeetingProtocol*` / `Protocol*` because `ProtocolDraftDto` (the older "generate a draft" result of `IGovernanceService`) already exists.

*Schema.* Migration `V129__meeting_protocol_draft.sql` (additive and repeatable, no `CASCADE`; V1, V127 and V128 are untouched): `protocol_draft` (one per Sitzung, `phase` DRAFT/LOCKED), `protocol_draft_block`
(`VARCHAR(8000)` text per agenda item, `version`, unique per `(draft, item)`) and `protocol_draft_task`. A block has **no `updated_by`**: who wrote is in the audit log, so the table holds less personal data. The
model is part of `05-governance.kuml.kts`. A new migration was chosen over changing V127/V128 because those are on `master` and a database that already ran them would see a changed checksum.

*Access.* Through the Sitzung (`MeetingSessionAccess.requireMeeting`), **not** through the conference room, so the minute taker can finish after the room ended. The role is the same `roleOf` as in the
panel. Whatever the caller must not see -- an unknown or malformed id, a Sitzung without a role for the caller, a room guest, a federation guest -- is the same `NotFoundException`, raised before anything is read.
Every role reads; writing needs LEAD or MINUTES (`ForbiddenException` otherwise, and then no row and no audit entry exist).

[cols="2,1,1,1"]
|===
| Operation | PARTICIPANT | MINUTES | LEAD

| read the draft | yes | yes | yes
| save text, add or remove a task | no | yes | yes
|===

*State.* `NOT_OPENED`: read-only. `OPEN` and `HELD`: writable while `phase = DRAFT`. `CANCELLED` after the opening: read-only. `LOCKED` (set when the Protokoll is finalised, V1.9.240): every write is refused. Writing in `HELD` is a
deliberate exception to "CLOSED is read-only" of the conference room: the Protokoll is often finished after the Sitzung. A read **never** creates, writes or locks anything; the draft row is created by the
first write (in a savepoint, so a concurrent creator is tolerated).

*Derived at read.* Header, attendance, quorum and resolutions are not copied into the draft; they are derived from the stored rows whenever it is read. Until the draft is finalised that is acceptable; the snapshot of V1.9.240 (wave D2) is taken exactly at the transition DRAFT -> LOCKED, after which the snapshot is shown instead. Attendance comes from the attendance table only (confirmed entries), never from the raw "connected" suggestions of
the room. Resolutions are listed per agenda item (`resolution.agenda_item_id`), the rest under "Ohne Tagesordnungspunkt". **No ballot data is ever read:** the only thing taken from the election side is the
flag `election.secret`; the resolution DTO carries counts and the flag `secretBallot`, no person.

*Text.* Plain text only, 8,000 characters per agenda item, 500 per task, at most 50 tasks per draft. `sanitizeProtocolText` normalises line breaks, removes C0/C1 controls (except newline and tab), bidirectional
controls, zero-width characters, line and paragraph separators and unpaired surrogates, and never escapes or interprets anything. The length is counted in UTF-16 units after cleaning: H2 measures
`VARCHAR(n)` in UTF-16 units, PostgreSQL in code points, so the unit count is the one bound that fits both (a character outside the BMP costs two).

*Concurrency.* Writes lock the Sitzung row `FOR UPDATE` first. A text names the version it was based on; version 0 means "no text yet". A lost race is an ordinary answer (`saved = false` with the current text and
version), not an exception; an unchanged text makes no update, no new version and no audit entry. Tasks go only to a person of the committee roster (for the general assembly: people recorded as present or
represented).

*Audit.* Four kinds on the existing entity type `MEETING_SESSION` (appended at the END of `MeetingSessionAuditKind`): `PROTOCOL_DRAFT_CREATED`, `PROTOCOL_BLOCK_UPDATED`, `PROTOCOL_TASK_ADDED`,
`PROTOCOL_TASK_REMOVED` (the last one is recorded as `VOID`). The snapshot holds ids and a block version only -- never a text, a name or a due date. The audit entry is the last lock-taking step. Reads are
limited to 60, writes to 30 requests a minute per member and Sitzung.

*DSGVO.* `protocol_draft_task` is covered by `GovernancePersonalData`: the export lists the tasks given to the member (the free texts are not attributed to a person and not exported); erasure retains the rows
with the documented reason (committee record), like the other governance tables.

*Relation to the older API.* `IGovernanceService.generateProtocolDraft`, `getMeetingDetail` and `getAttendance` still return attendance and resolutions to **every** signed-in member. The new draft's
confidentiality ("only members of the committee") does not extend to them; changing them would change `MeetingsScreen`, so it is not done in this wave and is an open decision.

*Not in this wave (delivered by V1.9.240, wave D2):* PDF, finalising, the snapshot, the approval agenda item and the push of tasks to "Für Sie". *Still open:* the end time of the Sitzung (not recorded anywhere yet, shown as a dash).
`removeAgendaItem` refuses an item with Protokoll text or a task; an empty block goes with the item.

*Not verified.* Live operation with several participants, real browsers and phones, and the legal validity of a Protokoll.

== Finalising the Protokoll (V1.9.240, wave D2)

A second RPC service `IMeetingProtocolService` is the LAST `registerService` of `Application.kt` (after `IProtocolDraftService`; new methods go to its end). Eight methods:
`previewMeetingProtocol`, `getMeetingProtocolVersions`, `finaliseMeetingProtocol`, `confirmMeetingProtocolVersion`, `startMeetingProtocolCorrection`, `recordProtocolApproval`, `getMyProtocolTasks`,
`setMyProtocolTaskDone`. The PDF is served by `GET /api/meeting-protocol/{versionId}/pdf` (`MeetingProtocolRoutes`). Code: `MeetingProtocolOperations` (all rules), `ProtocolSnapshot` (canonical content and hash),
`ProtocolVersionStore` (the only writer of the version tables), `ProtocolPdfArchiver` (PDF and document, outside any transaction), `ProtocolApprovalItems` (the approval agenda item),
`MeetingProtocolPdfGenerator` and `ProtocolPdfTexts` (PDF), `MeetingSessionAccess.rolesOf` (all roles of the caller).

[source,kuml,name="protocol-version-states"]
----
stateDiagram(name = "Protokoll version") {
    val start = initialState()
    val awaiting = state(name = "AWAITING_CONFIRMATION")
    val confirmed = state(name = "CONFIRMED")
    val superseded = state(name = "SUPERSEDED")

    transition(source = start, target = awaiting) { trigger = "finalise (hash matches, version number = max + 1)" }
    transition(source = awaiting, target = confirmed) { trigger = "every required role confirmed" }
    transition(source = awaiting, target = superseded) { trigger = "a correction is started (nobody is left to confirm)" }
    transition(source = confirmed, target = confirmed) { trigger = "PDF archived afterwards (conditional update of pdf_document_version_id)" }
}
----

*Schema.* Migration `V130__meeting_protocol_versions.sql` (additive and repeatable, no `CASCADE`; V1 to V129 untouched -- V129 is on `master` and a changed checksum would break Flyway):
`protocol_version` (canonical JSON `content_json`, `content_sha256`, `status`, `confirmation_mode`, `pdf_document_version_id`), `protocol_version_confirmation` (one row per version and role),
`protocol_version_task` (tasks of a version with a stable `task_key`) and `protocol_task_status` (done-state, kept OUTSIDE the immutable version). Further columns: `protocol_draft.correction_*`,
`protocol_draft_task.task_key`, `agenda_item.protocol_approval_*`, `meeting.protocol_approval_source_meeting_id` and `organization_settings.protocol_confirmation_mode`.
The tombstone `meeting.protocol_approval_source_meeting_id` has **no foreign key**: `meeting -> meeting` would be a reference cycle the organisation backup cannot restore (same reason as `current_agenda_item_id`).

*Append-only, and why there is no trigger.* The repository has no database-trigger or `REVOKE` pattern (one role, Flyway runs as it, and a trigger would differ between H2 and PostgreSQL). The guarantee is
therefore: `ProtocolVersionStore` is the only file that writes the version tables (`ProtocolVersionStoreTripwireTest` fails otherwise), a version row is inserted once, and afterwards only `status` and
`pdf_document_version_id` change, each by a *conditional* update (`WHERE status = AWAITING_CONFIRMATION`, `WHERE pdf_document_version_id IS NULL AND status = CONFIRMED`). There is no delete and no upsert.
Any later change of `content_json` would no longer match `content_sha256`, so tampering is detectable. The generic document routes refuse new versions on a Protokoll document (409).

*Content and hash.* `ProtocolSnapshotV1` has a fixed field order (declaration order) and is serialised without whitespace; every string is NFC-normalised. The hash is
`sha256("lapis-protocol-v1\n" + json)` over UTF-8. The content holds header, attendance, quorum, per item the text, the resolutions (figures only through `toResolutionDtos()`, i.e. with the k-anonymity
filter), the tasks (by name, **not** by member id) and the approval; **not** the done-state, the confirmations, `serverNow` or `finalisedAt`. Preview and finalising derive the content from the same code, so the
hash the lead saw is the hash that is checked (`STALE` otherwise). Finalising needs the hash the caller saw: a text changed in between cannot be confirmed by mistake.

*Roles.* LEAD (chair, recording office, BOARD/ADMIN) and MINUTES (the minute taker) may finalise and confirm; a person holding both confirms both at once and gets two rows. If the Sitzung has no minute
taker, LEAD alone is enough (`minutes_required = false`, printed as "Protokollführung: nicht besetzt"). If a minute taker is named but gone, a correction may be started from `AWAITING_CONFIRMATION`
(the waiting version becomes `SUPERSEDED` and gets no PDF) -- otherwise the version would wait forever.

*Order of work.* Lock order is always `meeting` -> `protocol_draft` -> version rows; the approval item (which locks another Sitzung) is inserted before the audit entry, which takes the chain lock last.
No external effect runs inside a transaction: the PDF is rendered and written AFTER the commit (`ProtocolPdfArchiver`), then ONE short transaction creates the `document_version`, links the PDF to the
protocol version (conditional) and writes the audit entry; if the link changes no row, everything rolls back. The only work in the finalising transaction is a pure page-limit probe (no confirmation page,
one page short, at most 20 s), so a Protokoll that would later not fit is refused at finalising and not at confirmation. A confirmed version whose PDF failed stays confirmed; LEAD or MINUTES retry by
confirming again (the single way to build a missing PDF; an existing PDF is never rebuilt).

*PDF.* PDFBox, saved whole (no incremental save, no encryption, no form, no JavaScript, no open action, no external resource), so a later PAdES signature can be appended as an incremental update without
touching the bytes (`MeetingProtocolPdfTest` proves it with a dummy signer). Limits: 200 pages, 10 MB, 20 s. Texts are German only (`ProtocolPdfTexts`: stable keys, a `lang` hook; server PDFs are not
translated, like every other server PDF). Characters outside Latin-1 (Cyrillic, Georgian) print as `?` -- the hash is unaffected, it covers the content, not the PDF. The last page lists the confirmations
(organisation wall-clock) and the full hash in Courier, and says it is a simple electronic confirmation, not a qualified signature.

*Approval item.* "Genehmigung des Protokolls der Sitzung vom ..." is put on the next Sitzung of the committee exactly once per source Protokoll, whichever happens first: the Sitzung is created after the
confirmation (`GovernanceService.createMeeting`) or the Protokoll is confirmed after the Sitzung exists (`onConfirmed`). The tombstone (UNIQUE) and the item are written in one savepoint; position 1 if free,
otherwise behind the existing items. A manually removed item is not offered again. Cancelling the Sitzung that carries an unused item (not opened, no result, no Protokoll text, task, resolution or motion) releases the item and the tombstone, and the Protokoll is offered again to the next Sitzung that is not cancelled (`ProtocolApprovalItems.onMeetingCancelled`; it locks that other Sitzung, so it runs before any audit write). An item with a result or in an opened Sitzung cannot be removed. The lead records the result (`APPROVED`,
`APPROVED_WITH_CORRECTIONS`, `NOT_APPROVED`) while the Sitzung is open and its own draft is not locked; "Korrekturfassung ausstehend" is derived (result with corrections and no newer version of the source).

*Tasks.* A task keeps its `task_key` across versions. `getMyProtocolTasks` returns only the caller's tasks of the newest CONFIRMED version of each Protokoll; `setMyProtocolTaskDone` accepts only such a
task of the caller (else the same not-found). The dashboard shows two reasons (`meeting.protocolTaskOverdue`, URGENT; `meeting.protocolTaskOpen`, ACTION within seven days else INFO) with a count and the earliest
deadline -- never a text and never an object id (the click goes to the list on the Sitzungen page).

*Audit.* Eight kinds on `MEETING_SESSION`, appended at the END of `MeetingSessionAuditKind`: `PROTOCOL_FINALISED` (carries the hash), `PROTOCOL_CONFIRMED`, `PROTOCOL_PDF_ARCHIVED`,
`PROTOCOL_CORRECTION_STARTED`, `PROTOCOL_VERSION_SUPERSEDED`, `PROTOCOL_APPROVAL_ITEM_INSERTED`, `PROTOCOL_APPROVAL_RECORDED`, `PROTOCOL_TASK_STATUS_CHANGED`. Snapshots hold ids, enums and numbers only; no log
line carries a text, a name or a hash.

*DSGVO.* The four tables are covered. Export: own confirmations, finalised versions (ids), own tasks, own done-states. Erasure deletes `protocol_task_status`; versions, confirmations and version tasks are the
committee's immutable record and stay (retention like documents, Art. 17(3) GDPR; the member row is anonymised). **Names inside the stored snapshot and the PDF are text and are NOT anonymised.**

*Not in this wave.* PAdES or a qualified signature (the mode column is only a hook), the AI draft (wave E), translated PDF texts, a Unicode font. *Not verified.* Live operation with several people, real
browsers and phones, how the PDF looks in real viewers, and the legal validity of the confirmation.

== Known limitations

* *Inclusive end date.* `until` is inclusive everywhere in the eligibility code (`CommitteeEligibility`). A person whose seat ended automatically on
  the stichtag still counts on that day (quorum, ballots, voter snapshots of an election opened that day) and no longer from the next day. This
  deviates from "from the stichtag on, no longer counts"; changing the shared predicate was ruled out (it would alter every existing seat), and
  `until = stichtag - 1` would rewrite the past for a same-day exit.
* In the case of death the seat ends on the day of the status change, not on the date of death.
* Frozen voter snapshots of elections already open, ballots cast, closed elections and earlier resolutions are not touched; only seat rows of the
  member change.
* The only mandatory role that is watched is CHAIR of an executive board; this is not configurable.
* No e-mail to the board, only the notice band (30 days, at most 50 entries).
* There is no separate status for an expulsion (it runs through `WITHDRAWN`).
* Legacy seats are shown, not repaired (see above). Coming back to `ACTIVE` restores nothing; the confirmation dialog says so.
* Without "show ended", the roster does not list ended seats, so the note "ended automatically" is only visible with that option.
* N is the CURRENT member status, not the status at the date of the meeting (the same simplification as the quorum).
* With more than 1,000 recipients the parts are separate requests: a member who joins between two downloads can shift the boundary (sorted by id;
  the cover sheet carries the as-of time). The drift is documented, not prevented.
* There is no mass postal dispatch above 50 letters.
* The server does not yet refuse a NEW `MEMBER` row at the assembly (`addCommitteeMember` is unchanged); the UI no longer offers it. The server-side
  refusal is deferred until after "Welle B".
* The help pages of the website about committees and meetings are checked at rollout, not in this wave.
* *Election succession (V1.9.103):* no term of office and no start in the future; the acceptance of the election by the elected persons is recorded
  outside the system (assembly minutes) and nothing waits for it; no e-mail; with a skipped winner the committee can temporarily have too many seats;
  on the stichtag predecessor and winner overlap (inclusive `until`); an old election without a policy with an inactive winner still aborts at the tally
  (cancel the election instead); with fewer winners than seats and `REPLACE` the free seats stay free; an ordinary member can infer from
  `pendingAdminReview` that something is under review, but never the reason.
