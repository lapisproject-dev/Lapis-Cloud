= Staging test plan: auction hints between two real servers (V1.9.322)
:toc:

== Purpose

Steps c1 to c4 of the auction hint exchange are covered by unit, scenario (H2 and PostgreSQL), DOM and tripwire tests. The scenario tests run every store and the feed/pull path in **one** database and one JVM. What only two **real** servers can show: real TLS, real DNS, the real HTTP stack and clocks, the pins from a real partner poll, and the behaviour of both databases over hours. This plan is the part of the acceptance that a human must do. **It is not executed by the pipeline and it was not executed by the author of the wave.** Until somebody has run it, the sentence "tested against a real partner" must not be written anywhere.

WARNING: Run it on **staging servers only**, never on a production instance (PdV, ELB, Apolda). The feature is off by default and has not been legally reviewed.

== Set-up

. Two servers, **A** and **B**, both on the same version (V1.9.322 or newer), each with its own database, a real hostname and a valid certificate, `LAPIS_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEY` set, and an ADMIN account.
. On each: the auction module and the Euro module on; at least two ACTIVE members (A1, A2 and B1, B2), one guest, one friend. A1 owns an open euro auction with a presentable title and at least 3 hours left.
. A way to see the database (`psql`) and the logs of both servers. A second channel (phone) between the two administrators.
. A partner pair "A and B", not yet added to each other. The exchange is off on both.

== Steps

[cols="1,4,4",options="header"]
|===
| # | Action | Expected
| 1 | On A: add B as partner. On B: add A. Compare the fingerprints of the pinned keys by phone | the numbers match the other server's own key
| 2 | Both switches off (instance and partner). A1 tries to give the consent | the consent box says that sharing is not possible; `SELECT federation_opt_in FROM auction` stays false
| 3 | On both: switch the instance on (hop limit 1). On A: share auctions with B. A1 gives the consent | no hint on B yet (B has not allowed A); `auction_received_hint` on B is empty
| 4 | On B: allow auctions from A. Wait one pull tick | the pull state is READY; B1 (active) sees the auction in "Auktionen von Partnerservern" with host A, price and age; the guest and the friend see no section
| 5 | On B read `SELECT teaser_id, origin_host, hops FROM auction_received_hint` | exactly one row, hops 1, origin host A; no member, no description, no payment data
| 6 | B switches the auction module off, then on again | B shows nothing while it is off (the display needs both switches)
| 7 | B2 bids on A (through A's own site). Look at B within 30 minutes, then after 31 minutes and one pull | B still shows the old price, afterwards the new one; the price age is shown
| 8 | On A: Sofortkauf of the auction | after the next pull the hint is gone at B; a tombstone row exists on A
| 9 | A1 shares a second auction; on B the administrator hides the hint | the hint is gone on B and does not come back after a pull; A learns nothing; the audit entry holds host and id, no amount
| 10 | On A: switch the partner switch for B off | B's copies are gone after one pull; a tombstone scoped to B exists on A
| 11 | Rotate A's signing key | B shows "key changed", stops pulling and drops the hints; after the fingerprint check by phone B pulls again
| 12 | A1 switches a shared auction from EUR to LTR (before the first bid) | the hint is withdrawn at once; the consent is gone; switching back does not share again
| 13 | GDPR: export the data of A1; then erase A1 (member with a shared auction) | the export shows `sharedWithPartnerServers=true`; after the erasure the hint is gone at B after one pull, `federation_opt_in` is false, the auction row is retained
| 14 | Backup on A, A1 shares another auction, then restore the backup on a fresh instance A' with A's hostname (or on A with the allow-non-empty option) | the exchange is off on A', no consent stands, an audit entry `AUCTION_RESET_BY_RESTORE` exists; B drops the hint of the old state after its next pull (a trailing withdrawal)
| 15 | Optional: add C as a partner of B and raise the hop limit to 2 on B | C shows the hint with hops 2 and host A; the withdrawal at A reaches C through B within about an hour (24 hours worst case)
| 16 | Read the logs of both servers for the whole test | no hint id, no title, no amount, no key and no signature in any line
|===

== Evidence to attach

The date and the versions of both servers, the database queries used, the fingerprints compared, and for each failed step the log excerpt. A step that was not run must be marked "not run", never "ok".
